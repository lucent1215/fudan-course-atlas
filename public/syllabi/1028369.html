课程简介（中文）

学术英语（文史哲）旨在培养人文专业学生运用英语进行学术研究的能力。完成课程后，学生应掌握批判性阅读本专业文献的方法，学会撰写规范的学术论文，并能在学术场合进行演示汇报。

课程简介（英文）

Academic English for Humanities entails training students majoring in disciplines of humanities to use English appropriately for study and research. After taking this course, students are expected to acquire critical thinking skills in reading relevant literature for their own disciplines, learn how to write decent academic essays and deliver presentations in academic settings. 

教学目标

By the end of this term, students should have an increased awareness of some of the key features of academic English for humanities, and they should be able to:

  • listen to academic lectures
  • take effective notes
  • read academic texts quickly and effectively
  • express ideas clearly and accurately in academic writing
  • give oral presentations
  • write a short academic essay (or a specific section of an academic essay)
  • examine concordances to gain information on language patterns

教学方式

  1. Lecture: The lecture will cover the main concepts of each unit, and highlight the academic features of the articles under discussion, including the use of formal English, the strength of the author’s argument and the signpost language.
  2. Presentation: each student is required to make an academic presentation, which is fundamentally a research proposal based on the term project (writing an introduction to a hypothetical academic essay).
