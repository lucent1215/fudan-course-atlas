课程简介（中文）

本课程采用主题式、体验式与比较式的教学方法，引导学生认识和理解泰国文化与社会。课程基于本学期已实施的教学活动与学习体验，从节庆文化、传统游戏、饮食文化、当代新闻以及日常社会价值观等多个角度，全面探讨泰国社会的多样面貌。

课程不以事实记忆为核心，而是强调通过参与、反思与比较来进行学习，鼓励学生将泰国的文化实践与自身文化背景进行联系与对照。通过融入泰国传统游戏、烹饪活动、新闻讨论以及 “Bangkok Pain” 桌游等互动式学习活动，帮助学生在轻松且富有意义的学习过程中，深入理解传统与现代并存的泰国社会。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to Thai culture and society through a thematic, experiential, and comparative approach. Drawing on activities and learning experiences implemented during the semester, the course explores Thailand through festivals, traditional games, food culture, contemporary news, and everyday social values.

Rather than focusing on memorization of facts, the course emphasizes learning through participation, reflection, and comparison, encouraging students to connect Thai cultural practices with their own cultural backgrounds. Interactive activities such as Thai games, cooking sessions, news discussions, and the “Bangkok Pain” board game are integrated to help students understand both traditional and modern Thai society in an engaging and meaningful way.

教学目标

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Describe key features of Thai culture and social life, including festivals, food, games, beliefs, and daily practices.
  2. Explain the cultural meanings behind major Thai traditions and rituals.
  3. Analyze aspects of modern Thai society through media and current news.
  4. Understand how cultural practices reflect Thai values, such as family, community, respect, and social harmony.
  5. Compare Thai culture with their own culture, identifying similarities and differences.
  6. Reflect critically on how their perceptions of Thailand have changed throughout the course.

Communicate cultural understanding clearly through discussion, written reflection, and presentation.

教学方式

This course adopts an experiential and student-centered learning approach, including:

  • Interactive cultural activities (games, cooking, simulations)
  • Game-based learning, including traditional Thai games and the Bangkok Pain board game
  • Discussion-based learning using guided questions and case studies
  • Media-based learning through Thai news and contemporary examples
  • Reflective learning, including pre-course and post-course journals
  • Comparative analysis, encouraging students to relate Thai culture to their own cultural contexts

Group work and presentations to foster collaboration and cultural exchange

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 – Overview of Thailand
Introduction to Thailand, including basic demographics, geography, and key cultural elements. The session focuses on building a general understanding of Thai society through collaborative discussion.

Week 2 – Geography of Thailand
Exploration of Thailand’s geographical regions and their cultural characteristics. Students engage in collaborative map-making and short presentations.
Homework: Regional map assignment (“The Map”).

Week 3 – Historical Context
Overview of significant historical events that shaped Thai society, with attention to connections between Thai and Chinese history. Students work with a historical timeline.
Homework: Short report on one selected historical event.

Week 4 – Language and Communication
Introduction to Thai language use in daily life, including basic phrases and modes of verbal and non-verbal communication. Activities focus on practical language awareness and cultural norms.

Week 5 – Foreign Influences on the Thai Language
Examination of loanwords and foreign influences in Thai. Students participate in a “Loanword Rally” activity.
Homework: Reflection on “My Favorite Thai Word.”

Week 6 – Thai Beliefs: Religion and Spirit Traditions
Introduction to major religious beliefs, spiritual practices, and folk traditions in Thailand. Activities include a Thai horror and superstition guessing game and a “Build Your Spirit House” activity.

Week 7 – Midterm Exam
Midterm assessment covering cultural concepts and themes from Weeks 1–6.

Week 8 – Festivals and Celebratory Practices
Study of major Thai festivals and celebrations, focusing on their cultural and religious significance. Activities include festival matching, crafts, and guided reflection.
Homework: Comparison of Thai and Chinese festivals.

Week 9 – Thai Games
Exploration of traditional Thai games as forms of social interaction and community bonding. Students participate in Thai-style playtime activities.
Homework: Continued reflection on Thai and Chinese festivals.

Week 10 – Thai Arts and Literature (1)
Introduction to traditional and contemporary Thai arts and literature. Students take part in a Thai art scavenger hunt.
Homework: Group preparation for the “Showcase Thai Art & Literature” presentation.

Week 11 – Thai Arts and Literature (2)
In-depth exploration through student group presentations in the “Showcase Thai Art & Literature” session.
Homework: Group preparation of Thai food.

Week 12 – Thai Food and Cuisine
Discussion of Thai food culture and its social significance, supported by tasting and interactive discussion.
Homework: Prepare and share a Thai news article or feature in the TSC WeChat group.

Week 13 – Modern Thailand: Society, Politics, and Culture
Analysis of contemporary Thai society, focusing on social change, politics, and current issues through news-based discussion.
Homework: Reflection on “Thailand in the News.”

Week 14 – Final Review and Integration
Review and synthesis of key course themes. Students participate in the “Bangkok Pain” board game and complete an in-class reflective journal.

Week 15 – Final Project Presentation
Student presentations of final projects, demonstrating cultural understanding, analysis, and reflection.

Week 16 – Final Exam
Final examination assessing overall understanding of Thai culture and society.
