课程简介（中文）

国际传播能力已成为新时代高校人才培养工作的重要一环。一流学术人才必须能用国际通用语英语在国际期刊上发表成果、在国际学术研讨会上熟练交流，讲述好中国故事。本课程聚焦学术英语口语及写作中的核心、可迁移技能以及学术人文素养，帮助学生提升专业领域的国际交往能力和国际竞争力。

课程简介（英文）

International communication competence has become a vital component of talent cultivation in universities in the new era. Academic professionals should be capable of publishing research in international journals and communicating effectively at international conferences using English, the global lingua franca. Furthermore, they should be able to tell China’s stories well and effectively share China’s voice with the world. This course focuses on core, transferable skills in academic English speaking and writing, as well as academic literacy and integrity. It aims to help students engage in their professional studies and future work in English, thereby enhancing their international communication skills and competitiveness in their respective fields.

教学方式

  1. Lecture: The instructor explains key concepts, providing theoretical frameworks and background knowledge.
  2. Pair work/ Group discussion: Students will work in pairs or groups to share viewpoints and insights.
  3. Presentation: Students are guided to give presentations related to their major field of study.
