课程简介（中文）

在中国成功创业，既需把握商机，更需构筑法律防线。缺乏法律护盾的创业者，在长期商业竞赛中不仅难以守护创新成果，更可能使全部心血付诸东流。因此，在创业腾飞之前，必须配备完善的风险缓释机制。本课程聚焦运用法律工具为商业活动保驾护航，结合真实案例，从初创企业视角，系统审视商业全周期。内容涵盖：市场准入策略、企业设立程序、日常运营合规管理等。

课程简介（英文）

How to make money in China? How to avoid to losing money within China? While the first issue suits business schools, the latter is a typical law school issue. However, this course, Legal Aspects of Start-ups Doing Business in China, stands up in between, in the belief that without the helmet and shield from the legal premise, a business pioneer would risk all the efforts of his innovations, products and business models in the long-term race, not to mention safeguarding his harvest from such business. A proper parachute is instantly and constantly necessary before the entrepreneurship soars high into blue sky.This course applies PEST and SWOT analyses, as well as case-study methods, in the POV of a start-up, to walk the students through the whole business-life, including but not limited to soft-landing phase, the formation of business, the transaction rules, the legal compliance of routine operations, and the dispute resolutions.

 

教学目标

This course aims to walk you through the current legal environment of China, its origin, logic, structure, and evolution, hence fostering a deep understanding and practice in real-life scenarios, upon the issue of start-ups conducting better businesses in China.

The main goals of this course lays in three sectors:

  • To provide the in-person academic teaching and coaching experience, a completion of 2-credit course.
  • To provide the international students a framework to equip, a toolkit to find, an in-depth observation of cross-culture businesses, with theiron-hand business blueprints or business extension plans throughout China, if any.

Local students are also welcome to attend, though this course’s focus is for the international students, however, in an unusual perspective to dive deep and understand Chinese legal environments may also craft for their future careers.

教学方式

  • This course starts with challenging issues for start-ups doing business in China and will provide an in-depth survey of Chinese Legal Tradition & Legal Philosophy on a historic-cultural analysis basis. The course will aid the international students a better understanding of Chinese legal culture as a part of business environment, the students will be learning not only from the legal texts but also from discovering the unspoken rules of cultural-genetic application of laws and regulations in China under the current transition.

    Doctrine-hermeneutics and case-analysis will be applied in each part of the course.
