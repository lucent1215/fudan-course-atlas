课程简介（中文）

本课程专为来自不同国家的 HSK 1 级左右或能够认读拼音的来华留学生设计。课程整合听、说、读、写各项技能训练，以实用汉语为核心，围绕日常生活、学习场景展开教学。同时融入中西文化对比内容，帮助学生理解文化差异，提升跨文化交际能力。通过丰富多样的教学活动，激发学生学习兴趣，为其进一步深入学习汉语及适应在华学习生活奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is specifically designed for international students coming to China who are at around HSK Level 1 or are able to recognize Pinyin from different countries. The course integrates training in listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, with practical Chinese as the core, and centers teaching around daily life and study scenarios. Meanwhile, it incorporates content on comparisons between Chinese and Western cultures to help students understand cultural differences and enhance their cross-cultural communication abilities. Through a variety of teaching activities, it stimulates students' interest in learning and lays a solid foundation for their further in-depth study of Chinese and adaptation to study and life in China.

教学目标

This course aims to enable beginners who are at around HSK Level 1 or capable of recognizing Pinyin to master basic Chinese vocabulary and grammar, possess the ability to engage in simple daily communication, understand daily conversations, read simple short passages, and write simple sentences. Through the comparison between Chinese and Western cultures, it helps students grasp the characteristics of Chinese culture, enhances their cultural sensitivity and cross-cultural communication awareness, cultivates their autonomous learning and cooperative learning abilities, and provides linguistic and cultural support for their subsequent Chinese language learning and study and life in China.

教学方式

A variety of teaching methods are employed. The classroom teaching primarily focuses on interactive approaches. Through activities like scenario simulations and role-plays, students can enhance their listening and speaking skills in practical situations. Multimedia resources, such as images and videos, are utilized to assist teaching and enhance its visual appeal. Group discussions are organized to encourage students to share their viewpoints, fostering their cooperative and expressive abilities. Meanwhile, appropriate reading and writing tasks are assigned. Combined with cultural comparison explanations, these tasks guide students to conduct independent exploration, achieving the synchronous improvement of language skills and cultural literacy.
