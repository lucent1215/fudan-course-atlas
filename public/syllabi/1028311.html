课程简介（中文）

本课程选用的教学材料为各类科学技术学科科普语篇和学术论文，帮助学生了解不同学科的知识、发展动态。课程将系统介绍学术英语的阅读策略，鼓励学生了解专业学科前沿研究方向，选取话题进行口头汇报。课程通过阅读引导学生剖析论文结构。将科技文献阅读与思辨、口头交流、写作结合，提高学术英语理解性技能和表达性技能。

课程简介（英文）

The materials selected for this course are popular science texts and academic papers from various disciplines of science and technology, which help students understand the knowledge and development trends in different fields. The course will systematically introduce reading strategies for academic English and encourage students to explore cutting-edge research directions in their specialized disciplines and select topics for oral presentations. Through reading, students will be guided to analyze the structure of academic papers and learn academic paper writing skills. By integrating the reading of scientific and technological literature with critical thinking, oral communication and writing, the course aims to enhance students' receptive and productive skills in academic English. This will lay a solid foundation for students' future academic research and communication.

教学目标

This course is designed to help students develop academic reading strategies, enhance their academic reading proficiency, and gain insights into the knowledge and advancements across various scientific and technological disciplines. Through guided reading, students will learn to analyze the writing techniques of scientific and technical texts, while cultivating critical thinking abilities (including logical reasoning, critical thinking, creative thinking, etc.), autonomous learning abilities, and teamwork spirit. By integrating unit themes and classic texts with ideological and political education, it aims to foster students' awareness of building a strong country through science and technology, ethical awareness in the development of science and technology, the sense of a community with a shared future for mankind, environmental protection consciousness, etc., so as to achieve the trinity educational goal of "value shaping, capability cultivation, and knowledge imparting”.

教学方式

The teaching approach of this course combines lecturing and discussion, emphasizing students' thinking and active participation.

Through forms such as case analysis, group cooperation, teacher-student interaction, and student-student interaction, it guides students to analyze the main ideas, viewpoints, writing strategies, and academic value of the articles, and encourages students to explore interdisciplinary research topics.
