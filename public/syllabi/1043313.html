课程简介（中文）

为提供丰富的背景知识，本课程将首先解析中国的全球参与，并介绍理解中国国际关系背后动态的基本方法。随后，课程将介绍“一带一路”的主要驱动因素及其关键组成部分，并指出中国在发展中世界各地区以及全球范围内的经济、政治和文化参与的鲜明特征。此外，学生将被要求自行探究中国在发展中国家足迹不断扩大的实例与案例。通过讲座、演示和讨论的结合，本课程旨在帮助学生更加全面、平衡地理解中国在世界舞台上不断变化的角色及其对其他国家的重大影响。更重要的是，课程将鼓励学生批判性地思考关于崛起中国的不同叙事。

课程简介（英文）

To provide rich background knowledge, the course would start by unpacking China’s global engagement and introducing basic approaches to understanding the dynamics behind
China’s international relations. The course then introduces major drivers of the BRI and its key components, and identify distinctive features regarding China’s economic, political,
and cultural engagement in various parts of the developing world as well as on a global scale. In addition, students will be asked to make their own inquiries into examples and cases of China’s enlarging footprints in developing countries. Through the combination of lectures, presentations, and discussions, the course is expected to assist students to obtain a more informed and balanced understanding of China’s changing role on the world stage and its significant impact on other countries. More importantly, the course would encourage
students to critically think about the different narratives about an ascending China

教学目标

The Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) proposed by the Chinese government in 2013 has
become an important pillar of China’s domestic development strategy and its foreign policy.
Through the BRI, China is reshaping its relationships throughout Asia and beyond as it
engages politically, economically, diplomatically, and culturally with other developing
countries and sub-regions. The BRI and its multi-faceted implications have invoked heated
discussions, politicized debates and conflicted interpretations of China’s motives and
impacts. The primary goal of this course is to provide a comprehensive and balanced picture
of the BRI and China’s growing influence in the developing world. It aims to facilitate
students’ understanding of the background, policy objectives, and implementation of the
BRI. Throughout the course, students will engage with ongoing discussions about China’s
rise and its international engagement in the developing world and beyond.

教学方式

The course is lecture-based, while students interact with the instructor in an informed manner
