课程简介（中文）

《英语论说文写作》课程适用于《中国英语能力等级量表》中提高阶段（四至六级）的学习者。课程旨在培养学生初步的论说能力：使其学会对相关话题的语言材料把握要点，理清逻辑关系；能对语言材料进行初步的分析，判断和评价，并用论据支持观点。

 

通过本课程的修读，学生应能达到《中国英语能力等级量表》中书面表达、组构及语用相关能力的六级水平

课程简介（英文）

This course begins by introducing argumentation frameworks, establishing students' foundational awareness of argumentation through four key aspects: analyzing language materials, refining viewpoints, logical discourse, and evidential support. It also incorporates the analysis and reading of argumentative texts to provide students with a clearer and more intuitive understanding of argumentation. Starting with outline writing, students will familiarize themselves with the classic-five argumentative style before transitioning to paragraph writing, honing their ability to express details and utilize supporting evidence. The course will include discussions on trending social topics and classic debate subjects, as well as written assignments both in and outside the classroom.  

教学目标

《英语论说文写作》课程适用于《中国英语能力等级量表》中提高阶段（四至六级）的学习者。课程旨在培养学生初步的论说能力：使其学会对相关话题的语言材料把握要点，理清逻辑关系；能对语言材料进行初步的分析，判断和评价，并用论据支持观点。

通过本课程的修读，学生应能达到《中国英语能力等级量表》中书面表达、组构及语用相关能力的六级水平。

教学方式

面授。通过以下安排培养学生对写作的理解和鼓励他们实际写作：

1.讲解写作理论

2.分析范文

3.组织讨论

4.段落-篇章写作

教学内容与进度安排

Week   Lecture and Activity

周         授课与活动

1          Introduction and Course Overview

2          Paragraph: Writing a well-structured paragraph

3          Paragraph (Cont’d) + Overview of an Essay: Essay structure and parts of an essay

4          Introduction: The thesis statement and choosing appropriate topics

5          Supporting Points: Supporting your thesis statement with strong and logical arguments; principle and practical arguments + Concession & Rebuttal: How to write a counterargument and refutation (Assignment 1A due)

6          Evidence: Different types of evidence; using convincing and appropriate evidence + Analysis + Citation (Assignment 1B due)

7          Conclusion + Avoiding Plagiarism + Outline: How to write an outline; using an outline as a writing tool + First Essay: Learning to write a classical 5-paragraph argumentative essay (Assignment 1C due)

8          Continuation of Week 7

9          Second Essay: Strategies for longer essays including framing, pathos (emotional appeal), comparisons, and further analysis (Assignment 2 due)

10        Analyzing and Critiquing: Framing, pathos, comparisons, and further analysis in readings

11        Editing: How to revise essays; how to peer edit + Avoiding Plagiarism (Review)

12        Third Essay: Learning to write an essay responding to an author’s arguments (Assignment 3 due)

13        Continuation of Week 12

14        Paragraph: Writing a well-structured paragraph (Review for exam) (Assignment 4 due)

15        Practice Exam: Overview of the final exam; learning to write under exam conditions

16        Q&A

17        Final Exam
