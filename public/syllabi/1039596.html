课程简介（中文）

本课程探讨古代两河流域的历史——从公元前10000年左右新石器时代的兴起到亚历山大大帝征服该地区（公元前330–323年）。通过考察该地区（从伊朗高原到地中海沿岸）的艺术与考古遗存，我们将追溯西亚城市文明的发展历程。

本课程将侧重通过考古与艺术史材料来阐明古代世界不同社会与政治组织形态在各个阶段所取得的成就及面临的挑战。

课程简介（英文）

This course explores the history of ancient Mesopotamia from the rise of the Neolithic period starting around the 10thmillennium BCE to the conquest of Alexander the Great (330-323 BC). By examining art and archaeological remains from across the region – spanning from the Iranian plateau to the Mediterranean Sea – we will trace the development of urban civilization in Western Asia.

Through analyzing these materials, we will:

  • Investigate the emergence, development, and evolution of ancient Mesopotamian society.
  • Understand key aspects of social development, such as the rise of writing, states, and empires; the relationship between religion and power; and the dynamics of trade and urban growth.
  • Consider Mesopotamia as a case study for analyzing broader patterns in human social development.

This course will emphasize visual materials and monumental architecture, using them to illuminate the achievements and challenges of different stages of social and political organization in the ancient world.

教学目标

引导学生：
 理解艺术、历史与文明之间的相互联系：探索研究古代社会的多种途径，重点关注物质文化。
 培养分析能力：学习通过关联遗迹、器物与文献资料，解读考古学及艺术史方面的证据。
 开展研究并进行有效沟通：掌握研究方法，并磨练口头表达能力，以分析中亚地区的艺术、考古、历史与文化。
