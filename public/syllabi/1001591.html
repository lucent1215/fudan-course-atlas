课程简介（中文）

通过本课程的教学，我们期望达到以下目标：1）通过系统的讲解，使学生理解和掌握深度学习的基本概念和算法，包括深度学习的主流模型架构和技术，以及深度学习的发展脉络和最新进展；2）通过精心设计的实验和项目实践，提升学生的动手能力，学生将学习如何使用主流的深度学习框架进行模型的设计、实现、训练和优化，从而在实践中巩固所学知识，培养解决实际问题的能力；3）通过启发式教学和引导讨论，培养学生的批判性思维、沟通表达能力和创新意识。

课程简介（英文）

Through the teaching of this course, we hope to achieve the following goals: 1) to enable students to understand and master the basic concepts and algorithms of deep learning, including the mainstream model architecture and technology of deep learning, as well as the development and latest progress of deep learning; 2) Enhance students' hands-on ability through well-designed experiments and project practice, and students will learn how to use mainstream deep learning frameworks to design, implement, train and optimize models, so as to consolidate the knowledge they have learned in practice and cultivate the ability to solve practical problems; 3) Through heuristic teaching and guided discussion, students will develop critical thinking, communication and expression skills and innovative awareness.
