课程简介（中文）

  本课程全面介绍学术英语的核心素养，重点培养科学与工程领域所需的特定交流技能。课程涵盖核心学术语言技能，包括听力、批判性阅读和笔记技巧、高效学术写作、口头报告和学术研讨。课程着重解析科技学术语篇的构成规律。此外，针对理工科专业的语言需求，课程将让学生熟悉相关领域的专业术语、文本结构与表达策略。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers a comprehensive introduction to the essential qualities of academic English, emphasizing the specific communication skills required in scientific and engineering contexts. It covers core academic language skills, including listening, critical reading and note-taking, effective academic writing, oral presentations, and academic discussion. A key focus of the course is on the analysis of academic moves—recognizing and understanding the structures, functions, and rhetorical patterns commonly used in academic discourse. Additionally, the course addresses discipline-specific language needs for science and engineering, equipping students with specialized vocabulary, structures, and strategies that are most relevant to these fields.

教学目标

This course aims to enhance students' academic English proficiency in science and engineering through targeted listening, speaking, reading, and writing activities, with a focus on developing effective communication skills in discipline-specific academic contexts. The curriculum framework aims to solidify foundational academic language abilities while ensuring the acquired skills are transferable to other university courses or self-directed learning. Upon completion of the course, students will not only master the application of academic English in scientific and engineering fields, but also cultivate research interest, scientific rigor and teamwork spirit. This will lay a solid foundation for students' future academic research and communication.

教学方式

The teaching methodology of this course combines lectures and discussions.
Instructors will systematically explain the core features of academic English in science and engineering, facilitate discussions, and focus on the structural framework of research papers and the application of academic language.
