课程简介（中文）

本课程的内容涉及文明的传播与域内外文明的比较、中华文明的扩散与演变、文明的内部演进三大部分，讲授内容在涵盖本文明发展的基本路径的基础上，对中国文明在世界的角色与地位，同样予以重视。并且，在我们视野中的“中国”，是多元文化的共同体，是“大中国”，讲授与讨论的内容将有意识地向域内各种文化的融合过程倾斜。我们期望，在这门课程中构建的文明史，是能够展现文明边界扩展与内涵更新的变迁史。

本课程主要通过讲授与讨论密切结合的形式，为了易与讨论课相结合，并免于泛化，本课程的讲授方式，将以专题为纲，不求面面俱到但重视系统性，课程内容力图包括最能影响文明的塑造过程、内部凝聚力的生成，以及最能体现本文明特点的要素，并将动态与比较的理念贯彻始终。

通过此课程的讲授与讨论，我们希望能达成两个目标：拓宽视野，使寻找问题与寻求解决之道，成为下意识的行为；初步接触材料与研究，提升辨析能力，通往追寻“真实”之路。

出于系统性的考虑，本课程共设计12个专题，基本涵括了与中国古代文明发展路径与特质最为相关的要素。同时，为了充分发挥授课教师的专业特长，保证教学质量，授课教师将选取其中的8个专题，每专题讲授4课时，讨论2课时。

课程简介（英文）

1. Formal Academic Translation
This version uses standard academic terminology suitable for a course catalog or syllabus.

This course is divided into three major components: the dissemination of civilization and cross-cultural comparisons, the expansion and evolution of Chinese civilization, and the internal progression of civilization. While covering the fundamental developmental path of our own civilization, the lectures place equal emphasis on China’s role and status within a global context. Furthermore, the "China" within our scope of study is a multicultural community—a "Greater China"—and the course content will intentionally focus on the integration process of various cultures within the region. Our goal is to construct a history of civilization that demonstrates the expansion of boundaries and the continuous renewal of cultural essence.

The course adopts a format that closely integrates lecturing with discussion. To facilitate effective discussion and avoid over-generalization, the lectures are organized by specific themes. We prioritize systemic depth over exhaustive breadth. The content focuses on the elements most critical to the shaping of civilization, the generation of internal cohesion, and the defining characteristics of our culture, while consistently applying the principles of dynamic change and comparative analysis.
