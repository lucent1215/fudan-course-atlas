课程简介（中文）

本课程是《中国概况（上）》的进阶课程，全英文授课。如果说上门课在回答“何以中国”，本课程则聚焦于更具挑战性的问题：“中国向何处去？”。课程以“重建坐标系”为主线，探索自1840年危机以来，中国如何在旧秩序的废墟上，通过革命、改革与技术创新，重新寻找其在现代世界中的位置。结合上海“行走课堂”与进阶版“AI学术对话”，你将穿越从晚清变局到数字未来的历史现场，构建起理解当代中国复杂性的动态框架，不仅读懂它的转型逻辑，更预见它的未来走向。

课程简介（英文）

As the advanced continuation of "China Survey I," this course shifts focus from "Why China?" to the critical question: "Whither China?". Centered on the theme of "Rebuilding the Coordinates," we explore how China, since the crisis of 1840, has sought to rediscover its place in the modern world through revolution, reform, and innovation. Combining Shanghai's "Walking Classroom" with advanced "AI Academic Dialogue," you will traverse from the late Qing transformations to the digital future. This course builds a dynamic framework to understand the complexities of contemporary China, enabling you not only to decode its transformation but also to envision its future trajectory.

教学目标

This course aims to enable students to:

  • Cultivate Values: Understand the historical logic behind China's path to modernization. Develop an objective and empathetic perspective on the struggles and achievements of the Chinese people over the past two centuries.

  • Impart Knowledge: Master the key historical turning points and systemic transformations of modern China, including the transition from empire to republic, the socialist construction, and the market-oriented economic reforms.

  • Develop Abilities: Enhance the ability to analyze contemporary Chinese news and social issues by tracing their historical roots. Through "City Detective" projects (PBL), students will practice connecting historical archives with urban field research.

教学方式

  • Inquiry-based Modular Teaching: Guides learning through core questions, integrating multi-dimensional content from history, culture, and society.
  • Multimedia Case Studies: Analyzing films, propaganda posters, and contemporary social media trends.
  • Group Discussions & Debates: Focused on weekly topics, this method encourages students to share perspectives from their diverse cultural backgrounds.
  • AI-Enhanced Research: Using AI as a "debate partner" to simulate historical scenarios or analyze complex policy documents.
  • On-site & Localized Learning: Utilizing resources on the Fudan campus as well as Shanghai’s unique urban landscape as a living textbook to read modern history. (Specific routes and locations to be determined based on actual conditions).
  • Student-led Project Presentations: Students will work in groups to complete two thematic research projects and present their findings.

教学参考资料

A. Core Course Pack (Self-compiled): The primary learning materials are self-compiled lecture notes, selected articles, and digital dossiers (comprising government white papers, news archives, and multimedia links) prepared by the instructor, released weekly on the course platform.

B. Recommended Reference Books (Essential Trio):

  1. Spence, Jonathan D. The Search for Modern China (3rd Edition). W. W. Norton & Company, 2013.

    • (History / Foreign Perspective): The definitive textbook for understanding the "Collapse" and "Reconstruction" of China from the Qing Dynasty to the present. A must-read for Modules 1 & 2.

  2. Yu, Hua. China in Ten Words. Pantheon, 2011.

    • (Society & Culture / Chinese Perspective): A renowned Chinese author uses ten keywords (e.g., "People," "Leader," "Copycat") to narrate the emotional and social transformation of modern China. Perfect for Module 3 & 4.

  3. Jin, Keyu. The New China Playbook: Beyond Socialism and Capitalism. Viking, 2023.

    • (Economy & Future / Chinese Perspective): A new-generation economist explains the logic behind China’s unique model, innovation, and global role. Essential for understanding "Reform," "Tech," and "Whither China" in Module 3 & 5.
