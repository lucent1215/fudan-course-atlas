课程简介（中文）

本课程系统介绍中国政治传播的发展背景，并在全球视野下全面梳理中国国际传播的整体格局与实践路径。
课程要求学生以小组形式围绕专题开展研讨汇报，积极分享观点、参与互动评议，通过深度研讨深化对核心议题的理解与思考。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces students to the background of China’s political communication and provides overview of China’s international communication in a global context.

The students are required to participate in group presentations on specific topic on seminars and are encouraged to give his or her ideas and comments on each topic, so that the course will explore issues more deeply.

教学目标

By the end of the course, the students will have acquired a broad perspective of China’s international communication and gain familiarity with its political consequences in globalization.

教学方式

Lecture, presentation, group discussion
