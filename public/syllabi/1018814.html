课程简介（中文）

本课程是一门学科交叉融合的课程，是“复旦行知”服务学习课程体系中的一门。旨在将音乐作为一种服务工具，引导学生把课堂理论、模拟实践应用于满足真实需要和回应实际问题的社会服务中，并依靠结构化的反思活动强化教学效果。“在服务中学习，为服务而学习”。服务学习强调知识与需求、学生与社群、学校与社会的三个联系。

在课程中将对于具有原本性音乐教育理念的三大音乐教育体系梳理与结合案例学习，培养学生掌握一定实用性音乐技能以及编创能力；基于EBP设计基础和社工服务必备知识的学习，在教师团队、服务点专职工作人员的指导下，设计、参与到针对不同人群（以社交方式、社会背景、经济背景划分），旨在提高不同人群的生活质量、促进个人积极情绪产生，具有人文关怀与疗愈的社会服务项目。

在这个社会服务过程中，促进与提升对于课程学习和社会服务的理解，提高自主学习、沟通共享、团队协作、交流表达、反思等综合能力，培养同理心，进一步提高大学生的有社会责任感与志愿服务意识。

课程简介（英文）

This course is an interdisciplinary course and one of the "Fudan Xingzhi" service learning course system. It aims to use music as a service tool to guide students to apply classroom theories and simulated practices to social services that meet real needs and address actual problems, and to enhance teaching effectiveness through structured reflection activities. "Learning through service, learning for service." Service learning emphasizes the connection between knowledge and needs, students and communities, and schools and society.

In the course, students will sort out and combine the three major music education systems with original music education concepts through case studies, and cultivate their practical music skills and creative abilities. Based on the learning of EBP design fundamentals and essential knowledge for social work services, under the guidance of the teaching team and full-time staff at the service sites, students will design and participate in social service projects for different groups (divided by social interaction, social background, and economic background), aiming to improve the quality of life of different groups and promote the generation of positive emotions in individuals, with humanistic care and healing.

During this social service process, students will enhance their understanding of course learning and social services, improve their comprehensive abilities such as autonomous learning, communication and sharing, teamwork, communication and expression, and reflection, cultivate empathy, and further enhance college students' sense of social responsibility and volunteer service awareness.

6.3 简易音乐乐器制作
