课程简介（中文）

影视与英美文化讨论课程面向对英美影视感兴趣并希望了解英美文化、进行中外文化互鉴、增强民族自信、更好地肩负起文化交流重任、为建设人类命运共同体贡献力量的全校非英语专业本科生。
本课程为大学英语进阶课程，采用主题教学法。
学生需要出席课堂，观看教师提供的视频，阅读配套文字材料，展开讨论，参加小组报告，完成实时测验，通过期末考试。

课程简介（英文）

Visual Media and American/British Culture welcomes students who are keen on American/British films and TV products as well as the underlying cultural themes. This theme-based course will empower students to gain insight into American/British culture, to boost cultural confidence, to become bridge builders across culture, and to contribute to a community with a shared future for mankind.
This intermediate-level College English course requires students to attend classes, watch curated video clips, read supplementary materials, participate in discussion and team presentation, and sit the final exam.

教学目标

1.学生在老师指导下观看多种主题的系列英语影视作品，了解其中的文化现象。
2.学生阅读相关材料，分析文化现象的由来及影响。
3.学生在此基础上，描述大学教育、师生关系、金钱观、商业社会、多元文化、情感关系、科技与人类等英美文化现象，参与课堂讨论和报告，让他人理解自己的观点。
4.学生能比较东西方文化中的各种异同，解释背后的相关文化理念。
5.学生愿意理解与自己文化不同的人和行为，并坚持文化自信，做出正确的价值判断。
6.学生能成为有效的自主学习者。

教学方式

以讲授为主。主要包含：
1.基于视听材料的授课
2.基于视听和阅读材料的讨论
3.基于小组同伴研究的报告

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1 College Life
Learning objectives
Upon completion of this learning module, students will be able to:
1) analyze the purpose and value of higher education
2) analyze representation of college life across cultures
3) reflect on the influence of popularity and conformity on their college experience
 
Week 2  Professor-student Relationship
Learning objectives
Upon completion of this learning module, students will be able to:
1) analyze representations of professor-student relationships in different cultures
2) compare and contrast professor-student relationship within different student populations
3) evaluate the impact of technology on professor-student relationship
 
Week 3  Diversity
Learning objectives
Upon completion of this learning module, students will be able to:
1) identify cultural stereotypes and critique the representation of multiculturism
2) analyze the causes and consequences of intercultural misunderstanding and conflict
3) evaluate the complexities of cultural diversity in a globalized context, particularly focusing on immigration and integration

 

Week 4  Life Choices
Learning objectives
Upon completion of this learning module, students will be able to:
1) analyze materialism and its influence on individual choices
2) evaluate alternative lifestyles that prioritize non-materialistic values and explore their potential benefits and challenges
3) evaluate the impact of life choices on personal fulfillment and relationships
 
Week 5  Businesses
Learning objectives
Upon completion of this learning module, students will be able to:
1) analyze the ethical dimensions of business practices, including both corporate social responsibility and profit-driven motivations
2) examine the interplay between personal values, career choices, and corporate ethics
3)analyze current trends and future projections related to business models and consumer behavior
