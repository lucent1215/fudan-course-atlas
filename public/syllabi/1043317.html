课程简介（中文）

本课程将重点教授两种中国民间舞：藏族舞和长袖舞，涵盖其历史文化背景及基本动作。学生将练习基本舞蹈技巧，并学习这两种舞蹈各一个表演片段，为告别晚会上的演出进行排练。

课程简介（英文）

This course will focus on the teaching of two types of Chinese folk dance: Tibetan dance and long-sleeve dance, including their historical and cultural background as well as the basic moves. Students will practice the basic dance techniques and will learn an excerpt from a performance of each of these two types of dances and rehearse for the performance at the farewell party.

教学目标

To provide the students with the basic knowledge of Chinese folk dance and to offer them an opportunity to practice the basic moves and present a performance.

教学方式

Introducing the historical and cultural background of Chinese folk dance;

Offering the students hands-on experience in practicing the dance moves;

Working on two specific excerpts from each dance and rehearsing for the final performance;

 

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1  Introduction to Tibetan dance, including the historical and cultural knowledge, body posture and rhythm and practice of the basic moves.

Week 2   Rehearsal for a specific excerpt from Tibetan dance performance.

Week 3   Introduction to the long-sleeve dance, including the historical and cultural knowledge, body posture and rhythm and practice of the basic moves.

Week 4   Rehearsal for a specific excerpt from a long-sleeve dance performance; final evaluation.
