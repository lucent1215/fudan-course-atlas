课程简介（中文）

本课程从介绍论说框架入手，从分析语料，提炼观点，逻辑论述，证据支撑五个方面搭建学生基本的论说意识；并引入论说文本分析、阅读，让学生对论说有更清晰直观的认识。学生将从大纲写作开始，熟悉五段论说文体，再过渡到段落写作，培养细节表达能力和使用论据的习惯。课程将包括对社会热点话题和经典辩论题的讨论，和课堂内外的书面写作任务。

课程简介（英文）

This course begins by introducing the foundational framework of argumentation, guiding students through five key components—analyzing reading materials, formulating thesis statements, constructing logical reasoning, and marshaling substantive evidence—to build a core competency in argumentative writing. It further incorporates the analysis and close reading of argumentative texts, enabling students to develop a more concrete and nuanced understanding of what constitutes effective argumentation. Progression proceeds from outline writing, with an emphasis on mastering the five-paragraph essay structure, to full paragraph development, where attention is given to cultivating precision in expression and the habitual use of supporting evidence. The course also engages students in discussions on contemporary social issues and debate topics, complemented by both in-class and take-home writing assignments.

教学目标

《英语论说文写作》课程适用于《中国英语能力等级量表》中提高阶段（四至六级）的学习者。课程旨在培养学生初步的论说能力：使其学会对相关话题的语言材料把握要点，理清逻辑关系；能对语言材料进行初步的分析，判断和评价，并用论据支持观点。通过本课程的修读，学生应能达到《中国英语能力等级量表》中书面表达、组构及语用相关能力的六级水平

教学方式

线下教学；理论与实践结合。
