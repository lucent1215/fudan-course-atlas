课程简介（中文）

组织行为学研究个体、群体及组织系统中的行为规律。课程以“人”为中心，涵盖个体差异、动机与态度、沟通与领导、团队合作、组织文化等议题，旨在帮助学生掌握组织行为理论及管理实践应用。

课程强调理论与实践结合，培养学生自我认知、沟通能力、领导潜能与跨文化理解，塑造兼具学术素养与实践智慧的管理者。

课程简介（英文）

Organizational Behavior is a core management course that examines the behavioral patterns of individuals, groups, and organizational systems. Focusing on “people in organizations,” the course explores key topics such as individual differences, motivation and attitudes, communication and leadership, teamwork, organizational culture, and change. It aims to help students understand the fundamental theories, research methods, and practical applications of organizational behavior in real-world management contexts.

Through lectures, case analyses, role plays, team projects, and reflective exercises, students will learn how to motivate employees, lead teams effectively, enhance organizational communication, and improve both individual and organizational performance. Emphasizing the integration of theory and practice, the course also develops students’ self-awareness, interpersonal competence, leadership potential, and cross-cultural understanding—preparing them to become thoughtful and capable managers in a rapidly changing organizational world.

教学目标

Through this course, students will be able to:

  • Master and Apply Core Theories—Understand and accurately explain key Organizational Behavior concepts and major theories (e.g., motivation, leadership, social identity, organizational culture) as well as research methods (e.g., surveys, interviews, experiments). Apply these frameworks to analyze behaviors at individual, group, and organizational levels and interpret organizational phenomena.
  • Diagnose and Solve Organizational Problems—Identify and analyze key workplace issues such as low motivation, communication barriers, leadership conflicts, and team inefficiency. Propose evidence-based and practical solutions to support managerial decision-making and improve team and organizational performance.
  • Enhance Self-Awareness and Interpersonal Skills—Through case discussions, role-playing, team projects, and reflective exercises, recognize personal traits, values, and behavioral patterns. Develop effective communication, collaboration, and situational leadership skills, strengthen self-regulation, and positively influence organizational outcomes.
  • Develop Critical and Creative Thinking—Analyze organizational behavior from multidisciplinary and cross-cultural perspectives, critically evaluate management practices, and propose innovative and feasible solutions in complex organizational contexts.
  • Strengthen Professional Ethics and Social Responsibility—Understand the links between Organizational Behavior, sustainability, employee well-being, and corporate responsibility. Cultivate ethical, responsible, and socially conscious management practices.

教学方式

Lectures

The instructor systematically explains the core concepts, classic theories, and latest research findings in organizational behavior to help students build a solid knowledge framework. The content covers topics at the individual level (e.g., motivation, attitudes, emotions), group level (e.g., leadership, team), and organizational level (e.g., culture, structure).

Seminars

Centering on specific topics (e.g., personality, motivation, leadership), students participate in group or whole-class discussions. The instructor guides students to critically examine theoretical assumptions, compare different research perspectives, and analyze issues using real-world cases.

Presentations

Students give group presentations on selected topics (e.g., team decision-making, incentive systems). Presentation formats may include PPT slides, short videos, role-playing, or scenario simulations. Students may also engage in group debates on certain topics (e.g., personality).

Case Analysis

By analyzing real or instructional cases (e.g., decision making bias, conflicts in leadership styles, low team performance), students apply organizational behavior theories to propose diagnoses and solutions.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Course Introduction and Overview of Organizational Behavior

Teaching Content:
Definition and scope of Organizational Behavior (OB)
The multilevel perspective: individual, team, and organizational levels
Key theories, research methods, and trends in OB
Relevance of OB to managerial practice

Teaching Objectives:
Understand what OB studies and why it matters
Recognize how OB concepts connect to workplace outcomes
Become familiar with the course structure and expectations

Week 2: Work Attitude

Teaching Content:
Job attitudes: job satisfaction, organizational commitment, organizational identification
Antecedents of work attitudes (job characteristics, personality, leadership)
Consequences: performance, citizenship behavior, turnover

Teaching Objectives:
Explain the major components of work attitudes
Analyze factors influencing employee attitudes
Evaluate how attitudes shape workplace behavior

Week 3: Work Behavior

Teaching Content:
Task performance, citizenship behavior, counterproductive work behavior 
Attendance, turnover, and proactive behaviors
Predictors of work behavior

Teaching Objectives:
Distinguish different forms of work behavior
Understand the drivers and outcomes of key workplace behaviors
Apply OB theories to predict employee actions

Week 4: Personality

Teaching Content:
Big Five personality model
MBTI
Dark Triad
Trait activation theory
Person–job and person–organization fit

Teaching Objectives:
Identify major personality traits and their workplace implications
Assess how personality shapes individual attitudes and behavior
Evaluate the role of fit in influencing employee outcomes

Week 5: Values

Teaching Content:
Cultural values
Schwartz’s value theory
Value congruence with jobs and organizations

Teaching Objectives:
Understand how values guide individual decision making and behavior
Explain the importance of value congruence
Analyze how values shape organizational culture and employee experiences

Week 6: Diversity in Organizations

Teaching Content:
Surface- and deep-level diversity
Benefits and challenges of workforce diversity
Inclusion, equity, and bias in organizations

Teaching Objectives:
Recognize different types of diversity and their consequences
Evaluate strategies for managing and leveraging diversity
Develop awareness of unconscious bias and inclusive practices

Week 7: Motivation I: Early Theories of Motivation

Teaching Content:
Maslow’s hierarchy of needs
McClelland’s needs theory
Herzberg’s two-factor theory
Theory X and Theory Y

Teaching Objectives:
Understand foundational motivation theories
Compare traditional frameworks and their implications
Apply early theories to diagnose real workplace issues

Week 8: Motivation II: Contemporary Theories of Motivation

Teaching Content:
Self-determination theory
Expectancy theory
Goal-setting theory
Equity and justice theories

Teaching Objectives:
Explain modern, evidence-based motivation theories
Analyze how motivation operates cognitively and socially
Design motivational strategies grounded in contemporary frameworks

Week 9: Emotion

Teaching Content:
Discrete emotions
Affective events theory
Emotional labor and regulation
Effects of emotions on decision making, leadership, and performance

Teaching Objectives:
Understand the role of emotions in shaping workplace experiences
Evaluate emotional regulation strategies
Explain why emotions are central to daily organizational life

Week 10: Individual Decision Making I: Theory Session

Teaching Content:
Rational decision-making model
Bounded rationality and heuristics
Common decision biases and errors

Teaching Objectives:
Identify the limits of rationality in decision making
Analyze how heuristics influence judgments
Apply theory to identify and avoid common decision traps

Week 11: Individual Decision Making II: Discussion Session

Teaching Content:
Applying decision-making concepts to real-world cases
Group discussion of biases, dilemmas, and trade-offs
Peer learning and experience sharing

Teaching Objectives:
Strengthen analytical and critical thinking skills
Practice diagnosing decision biases in practice
Develop strategies to improve individual decision quality

Week 12: Leadership I: Traditional Leadership Theories

Teaching Content:
Trait approach, behavioral approach
Contingency theories (Fiedler, Path–Goal, Situational Leadership)
Foundations of effective leadership

Teaching Objectives:
Understand classical theories and their evolution
Evaluate strengths and limitations of traditional perspectives
Apply traditional models to diagnose leader effectiveness

Week 13: Leadership II: Contemporary Leadership Theories

Teaching Content:
Transformational and transactional leadership
Leader–member exchange (LMX)
Ethical, authentic, and servant leadership
Abusive supervision
Contemporary challenges: digital leadership, shared leadership

Teaching Objectives:
Explain modern leadership frameworks
Analyze how leaders motivate, inspire, and build relationships
Apply current theories to emerging leadership contexts

Week 14: Team

Teaching Content:
Different types of team
Team development and dynamics
Roles, norms, and processes (communication, conflict, cohesion)
Team decision making and effectiveness

Teaching Objectives:
Understand how teams form, function, and succeed
Diagnose team problems using OB frameworks
Develop strategies for building effective teams

Week 15: Organizational Structure and Culture

Teaching Content:
Common organizational structures (functional, divisional, matrix, network)
Organizational culture: levels, dimensions, and formation
Alignment between structure, culture, and strategy

Teaching Objectives:
Describe the relationship between structure and behavior
Understand how culture shapes employee beliefs and actions
Evaluate how structure and culture influence organizational effectiveness

Week 16: Final Term Paper Submission
