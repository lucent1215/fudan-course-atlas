课程简介（中文）

本课程面向非英语专业学生开设，旨在提升学生的英语语言能力、文化理解能力、文化互鉴能力和国际传播能力。课程以中华文化为核心，探索如何在国际语境中用英文讲好中国故事，以外国人喜闻乐见的方式有效地传播中华文化。同时，课程通过设置传播实践任务，促进学生将理论知识与实践相结合，实现知行合一。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for non-English majors, aiming to enhance students’ English proficiency, cultural understanding, intercultural competence, and global communication skills. Centered on Chinese culture, the course explores how to effectively narrate China’s stories in an international context, employing engaging approaches to communicate cultural heritage in ways that resonate with global audiences. Meanwhile, through setting communication practice tasks, the course promotes students to combine theoretical knowledge with practice, achieving the unity of knowledge and action.

教学目标

一、知识目标（文化理解力）

  1. 识记中国当代文化核心领域（生活、饮食、文学等）的英文专业术语与特色表达。
  2. 列举中国文化的典型符号、传统智慧与现代创新成果，系统阐述其文化内涵与历史逻辑。
  1. 识记受众意识、文化维度、文化适应等文化传播理论的核心概念。

I. Knowledge Objectives (Cultural Comprehension Ability)

  1. Memorize the English professional terms and distinctive expressions in the core areas of contemporary Chinese culture, such as daily life, cuisine, literature, etc.
  2. List the typical symbols of Chinese culture, traditional wisdom, and modern innovative achievements, and systematically expound their cultural connotations and historical logic.
  3. Memorize the core concepts of cultural communication theories such as audience awareness, cultural dimensions, and cultural adaptation.

二、能力目标（国际传播力）

  1. 应用传播理论，针对不同国际受众，创作适配的文化传播创意文案或短视频。
  2. 策划中国文化国际传播项目，整合传播渠道与创意内容，输出具影响力的传播方案。

II. Ability Objectives (International Communication Ability)

  1. Apply communication theories to create creative cultural communication copies or short videos that are suitable for different international audiences.
  2. Plan international communication projects of Chinese culture, integrate communication channels and creative content, and produce influential communication plans.

三、价值目标（传播使命感）

  1. 践行文化自信与家国情怀，主动承担中华文化国际传播责任。
  2. 激发创新性传播思维，探索数字化传播路径，倡导中华文化国际传播意识。

III. Value Objectives (Sense of Mission in Communication)

  1. Practice cultural confidence and feelings of patriotism and love for the homeland, and take the initiative to assume the responsibility for the international dissemination of Chinese culture.
  2. Stimulate innovative communication thinking, explore digital communication paths, and advocate the awareness of international dissemination of Chinese culture.

教学方式

本课程使用线上线下混合式教学模式。线上课程依托慕课与SPOC课程，学生可自主学习文化传播理论知识与六大模块中国文化主题内容；线下课程以研讨与学生实践相结合。

This course adopts a blended teaching mode combining online and offline learning. The online courses rely on Massive Open Online Courses (MOOC) and Small Private Online Courses (SPOC). Students can independently study the theoretical knowledge of cultural communication and the content of the six modules themed on Chinese culture. The offline courses combine seminars with students' practical activities.

课内环节包括：

1.    小组研讨：学生分组讨论翻译案例与文化传播策略，分享观点并形成解决方案。

2.    师生共评：对翻译作业或传播方案进行集体点评，提升批判性思维与实践能力。

3.    模拟实践：通过角色扮演模拟真实场景，增强文化理解与传播能力。

The in-class sections include:

1.      Group Discussions: Students are divided into groups to discuss translation cases and cultural communication strategies, share their viewpoints, and formulate solutions.

2.      Joint Evaluation by Teachers and Students: Conduct collective evaluations of translation assignments or communication plans to enhance critical thinking and practical abilities.

3.      Simulated Practice: Simulate real-life scenarios through role-playing to strengthen the ability of cultural understanding and communication.

课外环节包括：

1.    线上任务：完成MOOC与SPOC课程的学习，参与在线讨论与测验。

2.    实践活动：如翻译中国文化宣传材料、设计中国文化传播方案，或参与校内外文化交流活动，将所学应用于实际。

The extracurricular sections include:

1.      Online Tasks: Complete the study of Massive Open Online Courses (MOOC) and Small Private Online Courses (SPOC), and participate in online discussions and quizzes.

2.      Practical Activities: Such as translating promotional materials of Chinese culture, designing communication plans for Chinese culture, or participating in cultural exchange activities both on and off campus, applying what has been learned to practice.

教学内容与进度安排

 

第一周：课程导学与破冰活动

  • 课程导学：介绍课程学习目标、混合式学习模式、主要任务、考核方式及能力要求。

  • 破冰与学习准备：组织学生相互认识，开展学情调查，并介绍在线学习平台与学期项目。

Week 1: Course Orientation and Ice-breaking Activities

  • Course Orientation: Introduce the course objectives, blended learning model, major tasks, assessment methods, and expected competencies.

  • Ice-breaking and Learning Preparation: Help students get to know one another, conduct a learner survey, and introduce the online learning platform and semester project.

第二周：第一单元——乐以载道：中国音乐的跨文化讲述（线下课）

  • 学习流程示范：通过示范单元帮助学生了解“文化理解—CARE表达—受众沟通”的基本学习流程。

  • 跨文化表达入门：引导学生初步练习如何清楚、简洁地向不同文化背景的受众介绍中国文化。

Week 2: Unit 1 — Hearing China, Sharing Harmony (In-class Learning)

  • Model Learning Process: Use a sample unit to demonstrate the basic learning process of cultural understanding, CARE-based explanation, and audience engagement.

  • Introduction to Intercultural Communication: Guide students to practise introducing Chinese culture clearly and concisely to audiences from different cultural backgrounds.

第三至四周：第二单元——观色闻道：从色彩感知到跨文化阐释（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：了解色彩感知、语言命名与文化意义之间的关系，积累相关文化知识与语言表达。

  • 线下实践：通过跨文化比较与表达活动，练习面向不同受众解释中国色彩概念。

Weeks 3–4: Unit 2 — Reading Colours, Reading the World (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Explore the relationships among colour perception, linguistic naming, and cultural meaning, while developing relevant cultural and linguistic knowledge.

  • In-class Practice: Use intercultural comparison and communication activities to practise explaining Chinese colour concepts to different audiences.

第五至六周：第三单元——食味中国：从菜名解释到文化故事（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：了解中国饮食名称、饮食习俗及其文化内涵，积累相关词汇与表达方式。

  • 线下实践：在跨文化交际情境中练习清楚解释中国饮食文化，并根据受众需要组织信息。

Weeks 5–6: Unit 3 — Tasting Culture, Telling Stories (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Develop an understanding of Chinese dish names, dining customs, and their cultural meanings, together with relevant vocabulary and expressions.

  • In-class Practice: Practise explaining Chinese food culture clearly in intercultural situations and organising information according to audience needs.

第七至八周：第四单元——万物有意：动植物文化词的跨文化表达（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：认识动植物文化词所承载的象征意义、文化联想与相关故事。

  • 线下实践：练习根据语境解释文化负载词，避免机械寻找英语对应词，提高表达的准确性与可理解性。

Weeks 7–8: Unit 4 — More than Words (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Explore the symbolic meanings, cultural associations, and stories connected with culture-loaded plant and animal terms.

  • In-class Practice: Practise explaining culture-loaded terms in context rather than mechanically searching for English equivalents, with an emphasis on accuracy and accessibility.

第九至十周：第五单元——诗中有境：唐诗意象的理解与传播（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：理解唐诗意象、文化联想及其表达特点，积累诗歌文化传播所需的基础知识。

  • 线下实践：练习将诗歌中的文化意蕴转化为跨文化受众容易理解和感受的表达。

Weeks 9–10: Unit 5 — Words into Worlds (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Develop an understanding of imagery, cultural associations, and expressive features in Tang poetry.

  • In-class Practice: Practise making the cultural and poetic meanings of Tang poetry accessible and engaging to intercultural audiences.

第十一至十二周：第六单元——仁与道：语境中的中国哲思（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：了解“仁”与“道”等中国思想概念的基本内涵、语境意义及常见英文表达。

  • 线下实践：练习通过定义、语境化和例证等方式，向跨文化受众解释中国哲学思想。

Weeks 11–12: Unit 6 — Ren and Dao: Chinese Ideas in Context (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Explore the basic meanings, contextual interpretations, and common English expressions of Chinese ideas such as Ren and Dao.

  • In-class Practice: Practise explaining Chinese philosophical ideas to intercultural audiences through definition, contextualisation, and illustration.

第十三至十四周：第七单元——经典新声：中国古典小说的跨文化讲述（线上学习与线下课堂）

  • 线上学习：了解中国古典小说的基本叙事特点、文化内涵及当代传播方式。

  • 线下实践：从受众视角出发，练习以清晰、具有吸引力的方式讲述中国经典故事。

Weeks 13–14: Unit 7 — Classics Reimagined (Online and In-class Learning)

  • Online Learning: Explore the narrative features, cultural meanings, and contemporary communication of Chinese classical novels.

  • In-class Practice: Practise retelling Chinese classics clearly and engagingly from an audience-centred perspective.

第十五周：课程复习与学期作品展示（线上课）

  • 课程复习：梳理各单元的核心文化知识、重点语言表达及CARE跨文化沟通方法。

  • 成果展示与考试准备：交流学期项目成果，并根据课程目标和考试要求进行综合复习。

Week 15: Course Review and China Story Studio (Online Class)

  • Course Review: Review the key cultural knowledge, language expressions, and CARE-based communication strategies covered during the semester.

  • Project Presentation and Exam Preparation: Share semester project outcomes and prepare for the final examination in accordance with the course objectives and assessment requirements.

第十六周：期末答疑与考试（线下）

  • 综合考核：考查学生对文化词汇与固定搭配的掌握情况，以及运用CARE框架完成跨文化交际任务的能力。

Week 16: Final Examination (In Class)

  • Comprehensive Assessment: Assess students’ command of cultural vocabulary and collocations, as well as their ability to complete an intercultural communication task using the CARE framework.
