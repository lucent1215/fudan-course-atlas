课程简介（中文）

本课程教学以中国化时代化的马克思主义为主题，以马克思主义中国化时代化为主线，重点讲授马克思主义中国化时代化的必要性和科学内涵，毛泽东思想、邓小平理论、“三个代表”重要思想、科学发展观创立的时代条件、主要内容、历史地位。帮助学生从理论上认识清楚要以中国式现代化全面推进中华民族伟大复兴，只能是而且必须是在中国共产党的领导下，以马克思主义作为指导思想，把马克思主义基本原理同中国具体实际相结合、同中华优秀传统文化相结合，坚定不移走社会主义道路，坚持不懈地建设和发展中国特色社会主义。

课程简介（英文）

Centered on Marxism adapted to the Chinese context and the needs of the times and guided by the main thread of adapting Marxism to the Chinese context and the needs of the times, this course focuses on teaching the necessity and scientific connotations of adapting Marxism to the Chinese context and the needs of the times, as well as the historical conditions for the formation, main contents and historical status of Mao Zedong Thought, Deng Xiaoping Theory, the Theory of Three Represents and the Scientific Outlook on Development. It helps students gain a clear theoretical understanding that to advance the rejuvenation of the Chinese nation on all fronts through a Chinese path to modernization, we can only and must adhere to the leadership of the Communist Party of China (CPC), take Marxism as the guiding ideology, integrate the basic tenets of Marxism with China’s specific realities and fine traditional Chinese culture, unswervingly follow the socialist path, and consistently build and develop socialism with Chinese characteristics.

教学目标

学生通过本课程学习，能够了解中国共产党怎样运用马克思主义的立场、观点和方法来解决中国在革命、建设、改革各个历史时期遇到的时代命题，能够掌握马克思主义中国化时代化的理论成果，能够深刻认识到坚持马克思主义指导地位对实现中华民族伟大复兴的重要性，进一步坚定“四个自信”。
