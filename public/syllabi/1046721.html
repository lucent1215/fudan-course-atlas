课程简介（中文）

本课程以石油为线索，考察其如何塑造现当代美国的经济发展、社会生活与文化想象。从十九世纪末的石油开采，到二战时期的战时动员；从战后高速公路、郊区住宅与汽车文化的兴起，到能源危机、环境保护运动与全球能源政治，课程将通过电影、摄影、广告、纪录片、文学作品等多种媒介，探讨石油如何深刻影响美国人的生活方式、空间景观与未来想象，并反思化石能源时代的遗产及其当代挑战。

课程简介（英文）

This course takes oil as its central thread and examines how it has shaped modern America’s economic development, social life, and cultural imagination. From late nineteenth-century oil extraction to wartime mobilization during World War II, and from the postwar rise of highways, suburban housing, and automobile culture to energy crises, environmental movements, and global energy politics, the course uses films, photography, advertisements, documentaries, and literary works to explore how oil has profoundly influenced American ways of life, spatial landscapes, and visions of the future, while also reflecting on the legacy of the fossil-fuel age and its contemporary challenges.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will be able to understand the central role of petroleum in shaping modern American society, culture, and politics. Through the study of films, photography, advertisements, documentaries, literary works, and historical documents, students will learn how oil has influenced American economic development, patterns of mobility, suburbanization, environmental consciousness, and global power. The course aims to cultivate interdisciplinary thinking by introducing students to cultural, historical, and environmental approaches to energy, while strengthening their ability to analyze visual and textual materials critically and to reflect on the continuing challenges of the fossil-fuel age.
