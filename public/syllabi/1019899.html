课程简介（中文）

On the basis of general chemistry, systematically learn the knowledge of organic chemistry, and strengthen students' understanding and application ability of organic compounds and organic reactions. The main contents include (1) introduction of organic chemistry and organic molecules; (2) the nomenclature of organic compounds and the structure and stereochemistry of alkanes; (3) chemical reactions, stereochemistry, substitution and elimination of haloalkane; (4) structure, synthesis, reactions of olefins and alkynes; (5) symmetry between conjugated system, infrared spectroscopy, and nuclear magnetic resonance spectroscopy; (6) structure, synthesis, and reactions of alcohol, ether, thioether, and epoxy compounds; (7) aromatic compounds and their reactions; (8) ketones, aldehydes and amines; (9) condensation and substitution reactions of carboxylic acids and their derivatives.

教学目标

Organic Chemistry is the main basic course for chemistry and medicine majors. The main task of this course is to enable students to systematically master the basic knowledge, basic theory, and basic methods of organic chemistry, be familiar with the typical reaction process of organic chemistry and general methods of organic chemistry research, and know all kinds of representative organic compounds and their applications, so that students can be well trained in scientific thinking, improve their ability to analyze and solve problems, and lay a solid foundation for further study.

教学内容与进度安排

Course Schedule (Please supply the details about each lesson with 72 academic hours in a total of 16 weeks):

 

Chapter 1      Introduction (4 credit hours)

The development of organic chemistry, atomic structure, chemical bond, chemical valence and chemical structure are introduced.

Chapter 2      Structures and properties of organic compounds (3 credit hours)

Electronic orbitals, atomic orbitals and hybridized orbitals are introduced.

Chapter 3      Alkane (4 credit hours)

The nomenclature, physical properties and chemical properties of alkanes are introduced.

Chapter 4      Alkene (4 credit hours)

The nomenclature, physical properties, chemical properties and preparation methods of alkynes are introduced.

Chapter 5      Alkyne (4 credit hours)

The nomenclature, chemical properties and conformation of alkynes are introduced.

Chapter 6      Cycloalkane (2 credit hours)

The structure and reactivity of benzene and the aromaticity of benzene derivatives are introduced.

Chapter 7      Aromatics (4 credit hours)

The structure and reactivity of benzene and the aromaticity of benzene derivatives are introduced.

Chapter 8      Stereochemistry (3 credit hours)

The classification and molecular chirality of isomers are introduced.

Chapter 9      Halogenated hydrocarbon (4 credit hours)

The classification, nomenclature, physical properties, chemical properties and reaction mechanism of halogenated hydrocarbons are introduced.

Chapter 10      Alcohols and phenols (6 credit hours)

The classification and nomenclature of alcohols and phenols and their physical and chemical properties are introduced.

Chapter 11      Ether, epoxides and thioether (3 credit hours)

The classification, nomenclature, physical properties, chemical properties and preparation methods of ethers, epoxides and thioethers were introduced.

Chapter 12      Aldehydes and ketones (4 credit hours)

The classification, nomenclature, physical properties, chemical properties and preparation methods of aldehydes and ketones are introduced.

Chapter 13      Nuclear magnetic resonance (3 credit hours)

The principles of nuclear magnetic resonance, chemical shift and spin coupling split are introduced.

Chapter 14      Infrared spectroscopy (2 credit hours)

The principle of infrared spectroscopy and the characteristic absorption of functional groups are introduced.

Chapter 15      Carboxylic acid (2 credit hours)

The nomenclature, physical properties, chemical properties and preparation methods of carboxylic acids are introduced.

Chapter 16      Carboxylic acid derivatives (4 credit hours)

The physical and chemical properties of carboxylic acid derivatives are introduced.

Chapter 17      Amine (4 credit hours)

The classification, physical properties, chemical properties and preparation methods of amine are introduced.
