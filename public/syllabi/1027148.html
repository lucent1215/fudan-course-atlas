课程简介（中文）

本课程聚焦材料科学、生命科学与医学的交叉领域，以“材料为桥，连接生命与医学”为核心，助力学习者构建跨学科知识体系。课程开篇以总论梳理三者关联脉络，随后逐层深入：先解析医用材料核心属性——生物相容性的科学内涵与评价标准；再介绍可降解材料、人工脏器、组织工程器官等实用领域的材料应用与设计要点；最后聚焦智能与仿生材料，展现学科前沿潜力。课程兼顾理论与实践，无论是相关专业学生还是行业从业者，都能在此掌握生命、医学与材料交叉领域的核心认知，为后续学习与实践奠基。

课程简介（英文）

The course focuses on the interdisciplinary field of materials science, life science and medicine, with the core positioning of "materials as a bridge connecting life and medicine", aiming to help learners build an interdisciplinary knowledge system. Starting with a general introduction that sorts out the connection between the three fields, the course proceeds in a progressive manner: it first explains the scientific connotation and evaluation criteria of biocompatibility, the core attribute of medical materials; then introduces the material applications and design key points in practical fields such as degradable materials, artificial organs, and tissue-engineered organs; finally, it focuses on intelligent and bionic materials to demonstrate the cutting-edge potential of the discipline. Balancing theory and practice, this course enables both relevant major students and industry practitioners to grasp the core knowledge of the interdisciplinary field of life, medicine and materials, laying a solid foundation for their subsequent study and practice.

教学目标

本课程的教学目的是借助医用材料科学方面的教学与讨论，让学生在交叉领域：材料科学、生命科学、化学、生物学、解剖学、病理学、临床医学、药物学等多学科相互交叉的学习中打开学生的思维视野、增强其独立思考的能力和学术创新的能力，为其日后在某一专业领域中做出创造性成果提供可能性。
