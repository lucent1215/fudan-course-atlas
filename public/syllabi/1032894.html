课程简介（中文）

通过调研最新体系结构领域研究进展，阅读最新研究进展相关论文和结合最新发展趋势进行必要的实验和分析，使学生理解体系结构领域最新的研究进展，培养学生熟悉主流的体系结构以及发展方向，在这个过程中熟悉体系结构主流的工具，并在这个过程中培养必要的动手能力。

课程简介（英文）

By conducting research on the latest advancements in the field of architecture, reading relevant papers on these advancements, and performing necessary experiments and analysis in conjunction with the latest development trends, students will comprehend the latest research progress in the field of architecture. This process will help students become familiar with mainstream architectures and their development directions, acquaint them with mainstream architecture tools, and cultivate essential practical skills.
