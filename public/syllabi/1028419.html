课程简介（中文）

本课程为通用学术英语类 (GAE) 课程，非专用学术英语 (ESAP)。本课程对标学术场景中的英语视听说核心技能，通过多媒体素材系统培养学生听懂基础通用性学术表达、准确捕捉关键信息的能力。课程注重实用性与场景适配性，引导学生在模拟讨论和专题汇报等互动中清晰表达学术观点并有效参与沟通交流，全面适配学术学习与国际交流需求。

课程简介（英文）

This course is primarily a General Academic English (GAE) course. It aligns with core academic English listening, speaking and viewing skills required in academic contexts. It aims to systematically enhance students' language proficiency in comprehending generic academic expressions and in accurately capturing key information through extensive exposure to authentic multimedia materials. Emphasizing practicality and scenario adaptation, the course guides students to explicitly express academic views and effectively participate in academic exchanges through interactive activities like simulated discussions and thematic presentations, fully catering to the demands of academic learning and international communication.

教学目标

By the end of the course, students will be able to comprehend and summarize ideas from different academic scenarios across disciplines and engage in effective academic discussions. Above all, the course will foster in students a critical thinking disposition to critically evaluate and deliver information, laying a solid foundation for further academic quest and international communication.

教学方式

The course features a student-centered approach, integrating multimedia exploration, situational simulations and interactive discussions. It emphasizes immersive training through lectures, seminars and presentations. Students will engage in listening comprehension exercises, oral summary tasks and group debates, cultivating practical academic communication skills in a professional and interactive setting.
