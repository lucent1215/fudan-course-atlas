课程简介（中文）

本学期课程延续《有机化学 AI》的学习，教学内容包括：脂肪胺、缩合反应、芳香亲核取代反应、含氮芳香化合物、酚和醌、杂环化合物等章节，与《有机化学 AI》相关学习内容有密切关联。例如：缩合反应章节在上学期“醛和酮 加成反应”以及“羧酸衍生物”的性质基础上进一步展开；“芳香亲核取代反应”、“含氮芳香化合物”、“酚和醌”、“杂环化合物”章节则都包含芳香化合物的性质，是上学期“苯和芳烃”章节内容的进一步拓展，在之前学习基础上有提升，更加考验知识的灵活运用能力。

课程简介（英文）

This semester's course continues the study from "Organic Chemistry AI," covering chapters including aliphatic amines, condensation reactions, aromatic nucleophilic substitution reactions, nitrogen-containing aromatic compounds, phenols and quinones, and heterocyclic compounds, all of which are closely related to the content of "Organic Chemistry AI." For example, the chapter on condensation reactions builds upon the properties of "addition reactions of aldehydes and ketones" and "carboxylic acid derivatives" from the previous semester. Chapters such as "aromatic nucleophilic substitution reactions," "nitrogen-containing aromatic compounds," "phenols and quinones," and "heterocyclic compounds" all involve the properties of aromatic compounds, serving as an extension of the "benzene and arenes" chapter from the previous semester. These sections elevate the prior knowledge base and place greater emphasis on the flexible application of the learned concepts.

教学目标

在《有机化学 AI》学习的基础上，进一步巩固和拓展：

  1. 反应机理引领的基础有机化学学习；
  2. 反应区域选择性和立体选择性及其影响因素；
  3. 多官能团之间的相互影响；
  4. 有机化合物制备方法及有机合成策略。

关键是在掌握扎实的有机化学知识基础上，形成化学思维习惯，具备分析和解决问题的能力，为科研创新做好准备。
