课程简介（中文）

课程介绍人机混合增强智能沿进展，应用和存在不足。课程主要介绍人机混合增强智能中的基本理论、在线演化与动态自适应、直觉推理及案例分析等。

具体包括，人工智能历史介绍、特征表示、视觉知识理论、脉冲神经网络、不确定估计理论及归纳偏置；人机协同知识学习、在线知识演化、动态自适应人机协同、具身智能、脑机接口与脑神经媒体组学；因果学习及因果发现、人机协同知识推断、基于直觉推理的场景推理/认知地图；人机协同的感知认知与决策；人机协同的图像视频生成与创意设计；以及相应的人机协同混合增强智能的平台包括创作平台、抓取平台、共驾平台和数字孪生平台介绍。另外，本课程也会适时加入在人机混合增强智能领域里最新研究成果介绍，确保课程内容始终与时俱进。

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces the advancements, applications, and existing shortcomings of human-machine hybrid augmented intelligence. It primarily covers the fundamental theories, online evolution and dynamic adaptation, intuitive reasoning, and case studies in human-machine hybrid augmented intelligence.

Specifically, the course includes: an introduction to the history of artificial intelligence, feature representation, visual knowledge theory, spiking neural networks, uncertainty estimation theory, and inductive bias; human-machine collaborative knowledge learning, online knowledge evolution, dynamic adaptive human-machine collaboration, embodied intelligence, brain-computer interfaces and brain neuromics; causal learning and causal discovery, human-machine collaborative knowledge inference, scene reasoning/cognitive mapping based on intuitive reasoning; human-machine collaborative perception, cognition, and decision-making; human-machine collaborative image and video generation and creative design; and corresponding platforms for human-machine collaborative hybrid augmented intelligence, including creation platforms, grasping platforms, co-driving platforms, and digital twin platforms. Additionally, this course will incorporate the latest research findings in the field of human-machine hybrid augmented intelligence as appropriate, ensuring that the course content remains up-to-date.

教学目标

针对人工智能前沿发展方向，讲授人机混合增强智能的理论、算法及应用。目的是让学生在学习完其中蕴含的基本理论基础上，全面了解人机混合增强智能的最新进展以及在图像视频多媒体创作、机器人、自动驾驶等领域的实际应用，帮助学生能够用更现代的观点去思考如何进行人机混合增强智能。
