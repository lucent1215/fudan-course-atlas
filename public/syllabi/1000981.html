课程简介（中文）

一、事件与概率
随机事件及其运算(随机试验, 随机事件与样本空间, 事件之间的关系及其运算)，概率的定义、性质及其运算(频率, 概率的统计定义, 古典概率, 几何概率，概率的公理化定义, 概率的性质)。
二、条件概率与统计独立性
条件概率及三个重要公式(乘法公式, 全概率公式, 贝叶斯公式)，事件的独立性及贝努里(Bernoulli)概型，直线上的随机游动。
三、随机变量与分布函数
随机变量的概念，随机变量的分布函数概念及其性质，离散型随机变量及其概率分布、离散型随机变量常见分布，连续性随机变量及其概率密度函数、连续性随机变量常见的分布，随机变量的函数的分布。
    二维随机变量的联合分布函数与边缘分布函数，二维离散型随机变量及其概率分布，二维连续型随机变量及其分布，随机变量的独立性定义及其判别法，随机向量的函数的概率分布 
四、数字特征与特征函数
随机变量数学期望的定义及其性质、随机变量函数的数学期望，随机变量方差的定义及其性质，协方差、相关系数的定义与计算公式，几种重要随机变量的数学期望与方差，特征函数的概念及其性质。
五、极限定理
契比雪夫不等式  贝努里大数定律、契比雪夫大数定律、辛钦大数定律（独立同分布场合的大数定律）。中心极限定理的概念，Lindeberg-Levy中心极限定理（独立同分布场合的中心极限定理），De Moivre-Laplace中心极限定理，二项分布的正态逼近方法。
六、引论（数理统计）
总体和样本、样本的联合分布，统计量与样本的数字特征，参数的矩估计法的基本思想及其矩估计量的求法，参数极大似然估计法的基本思想及其极大似然估计的求法，点估计的评价标准。
六、抽样分布
正态总体的样本均值、样本方差的分布  三个重要抽样分布( 分布, t分布, F分布)的定义及其性质。
七、假设检验
假设检验的基本思想和基本概念：统计假设、检验法则、两类错误，假设检验的一般步骤，正态总体的参数检验(单个总体均值和方差的检验，两个正态总体的均值差和方差比的假设检验)，多项分布的 －检验，广义似然比检验; 正态母体参数的置信区间.

 

课程简介（英文）

Content Overview

I. Events and Probability
Random events and their operations (random experiments, random events and sample spaces, relationships and operations between events). Definition, properties, and operations of probability (frequency, statistical definition of probability, classical probability, geometric probability, axiomatic definition of probability, properties of probability).

II. Conditional Probability and Statistical Independence
Conditional probability and three key formulas (multiplication formula, total probability formula, Bayes' formula). Independence of events and Bernoulli trials. Random walk on a straight line.

III. Random Variables and Distribution Functions
Concept of random variables. Distribution functions of random variables: definition and properties. Discrete random variables and their probability distributions; common discrete distributions. Continuous random variables and their probability density functions; common continuous distributions. Distribution of functions of random variables.
Joint distribution functions and marginal distribution functions of two-dimensional random variables. Two-dimensional discrete random variables and their probability distributions. Two-dimensional continuous random variables and their distributions. Definition and criteria for independence of random variables. Probability distributions of functions of random vectors.

IV. Numerical Characteristics and Characteristic Functions
Definition and properties of mathematical expectation for random variables; mathematical expectation of functions of random variables. Definition and properties of variance for random variables. Covariance and correlation coefficient: definitions and calculation formulas. Mathematical expectations and variances of several important random variables. Concept and properties of characteristic functions.

V. Limit Theorems
Chebyshev’s inequality. Bernoulli’s Law of Large Numbers, Chebyshev’s Law of Large Numbers, Khinchin’s Law of Large Numbers (Law of Large Numbers for independent and identically distributed cases). Concept of Central Limit Theorems. Lindeberg–Lévy Central Limit Theorem (Central Limit Theorem for independent and identically distributed cases). De Moivre–Laplace Central Limit Theorem. Normal approximation method for binomial distributions.

VI. Introduction (Mathematical Statistics)
Population and samples; joint distribution of samples. Statistics and numerical characteristics of samples. Basic principles of the method of moments for parameter estimation and derivation of moment estimators. Basic principles of maximum likelihood estimation and derivation of maximum likelihood estimators. Evaluation criteria for point estimation.

VII. Sampling Distributions
Distributions of sample mean and sample variance for normal populations. Definitions and properties of three key sampling distributions (χ²-distribution, t-distribution, F-distribution).

VIII. Hypothesis Testing
Fundamental concepts and principles of hypothesis testing: statistical hypotheses, test rules, Type I and Type II errors. General procedure for hypothesis testing. Parameter testing for normal populations (tests for mean and variance of a single population; hypothesis tests for difference in means and ratio of variances between two normal populations). χ²-test for multinomial distributions. Generalized likelihood ratio test. Confidence intervals for parameters of normal populations.

教学目标

通过本课程的学习，使学生较为深入地了解概率论特有的分析问题、解决问题的思想，并在一定程度上掌握利用概率论认识问题、解决问题的方法，对数理统计基本概念和结果有一定的了解，并能运用其思想解决实际生产中的简单课题。
