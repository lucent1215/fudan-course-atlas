课程简介（中文）

本课程面向非英语专业学生开设，旨在提升学生的英语语言能力、文化理解能力、文化互鉴能力和国际传播能力。课程以中华文化为核心，探索如何在国际语境中用英文讲好中国故事，以外国人喜闻乐见的方式有效地传播中华文化。同时，课程通过设置传播实践任务，促进学生将理论知识与实践相结合，实现知行合一。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for non-English majors, aiming to enhance students’ English proficiency, cultural understanding, intercultural competence, and global communication skills. Centered on Chinese culture, the course explores how to effectively narrate China’s stories in an international context, employing engaging approaches to communicate cultural heritage in ways that resonate with global audiences. Meanwhile, through setting communication practice tasks, the course promotes students to combine theoretical knowledge with practice, achieving the unity of knowledge and action.

教学目标

一、知识目标（文化理解力）

  1. 识记中国当代文化核心领域（生活、饮食、文学等）的英文专业术语与特色表达。
  2. 列举中国文化的典型符号、传统智慧与现代创新成果，系统阐述其文化内涵与历史逻辑。
  1. 识记受众意识、文化维度、文化适应等文化传播理论的核心概念。

I. Knowledge Objectives (Cultural Comprehension Ability)

  1. Memorize the English professional terms and distinctive expressions in the core areas of contemporary Chinese culture, such as daily life, cuisine, literature, etc.
  2. List the typical symbols of Chinese culture, traditional wisdom, and modern innovative achievements, and systematically expound their cultural connotations and historical logic.
  3. Memorize the core concepts of cultural communication theories such as audience awareness, cultural dimensions, and cultural adaptation.

二、能力目标（国际传播力）

  1. 应用传播理论，针对不同国际受众，创作适配的文化传播创意文案或短视频。
  2. 策划中国文化国际传播项目，整合传播渠道与创意内容，输出具影响力的传播方案。

II. Ability Objectives (International Communication Ability)

  1. Apply communication theories to create creative cultural communication copies or short videos that are suitable for different international audiences.
  2. Plan international communication projects of Chinese culture, integrate communication channels and creative content, and produce influential communication plans.

三、价值目标（传播使命感）

  1. 践行文化自信与家国情怀，主动承担中华文化国际传播责任。
  2. 激发创新性传播思维，探索数字化传播路径，倡导中华文化国际传播意识。

III. Value Objectives (Sense of Mission in Communication)

  1. Practice cultural confidence and feelings of patriotism and love for the homeland, and take the initiative to assume the responsibility for the international dissemination of Chinese culture.
  2. Stimulate innovative communication thinking, explore digital communication paths, and advocate the awareness of international dissemination of Chinese culture.

教学方式

本课程使用线上线下混合式教学模式。线上课程依托慕课与SPOC课程，学生可自主学习文化传播理论知识与六大模块中国文化主题内容；线下课程以研讨与学生实践相结合。

This course adopts a blended teaching mode combining online and offline learning. The online courses rely on Massive Open Online Courses (MOOC) and Small Private Online Courses (SPOC). Students can independently study the theoretical knowledge of cultural communication and the content of the six modules themed on Chinese culture. The offline courses combine seminars with students' practical activities.

课内环节包括：

1.    小组研讨：学生分组讨论翻译案例与文化传播策略，分享观点并形成解决方案。

2.    师生共评：对翻译作业或传播方案进行集体点评，提升批判性思维与实践能力。

3.    模拟实践：通过角色扮演模拟真实场景，增强文化理解与传播能力。

The in-class sections include:

1.      Group Discussions: Students are divided into groups to discuss translation cases and cultural communication strategies, share their viewpoints, and formulate solutions.

2.      Joint Evaluation by Teachers and Students: Conduct collective evaluations of translation assignments or communication plans to enhance critical thinking and practical abilities.

3.      Simulated Practice: Simulate real-life scenarios through role-playing to strengthen the ability of cultural understanding and communication.

课外环节包括：

1.    线上任务：完成MOOC与SPOC课程的学习，参与在线讨论与测验。

2.    实践活动：如翻译中国文化宣传材料、设计中国文化传播方案，或参与校内外文化交流活动，将所学应用于实际。

The extracurricular sections include:

1.      Online Tasks: Complete the study of Massive Open Online Courses (MOOC) and Small Private Online Courses (SPOC), and participate in online discussions and quizzes.

2.      Practical Activities: Such as translating promotional materials of Chinese culture, designing communication plans for Chinese culture, or participating in cultural exchange activities both on and off campus, applying what has been learned to practice.

教学内容与进度安排

 第一周：课程介绍与破冰活动

课程目标与内容介绍：课程介绍，包括课程学习模式、主要任务、测评方式及能力要求。

- 破冰活动：组织学生进行自我介绍、分组活动，以及分享对中国文化的理解与体验。

 Week 1: Course Introduction and Ice-breaking Activity

- Course Overview：Provide a comprehensive introduction to the course, covering its blended learning model, core tasks, assessment mechanisms, and expected competencies.

- Ice-breaking Activity：Organize students to introduce themselves, carry out group activities, and share their understandings and experiences of Chinese culture.

 第二周：第一单元：饮食风尚与文化脉络

- 文化传播理论学习：系统讲解文化适应四阶段理论，结合 “中餐出海” 案例，引导思考外国友人接触中国饮食文化的适应过程及促进策略。

- 语言与符号解码：学习 “茶道精神”“节气饮食” 等术语，通过相关纪录片片段积累文化表述素材。

 Week 2: Unit 1: Culinary Trends and Cultural Contexts 

- Cultural Communication Theory Study：Systematically explain the four - stage theory of cultural adaptation. With the case of "Chinese cuisine going global", guide students to think about the adaptation process of foreign friends when exposed to Chinese food culture and promotion strategies.

- Language and Symbol Decoding：Learn terms such as "the spirit of tea ceremony" and "seasonal cuisine", and accumulate cultural expression materials through clips from documentaries.

 第三周：第一单元：饮食风尚与文化脉络

- 饮食文化深度展示：小组选取 “八大菜系” 之一，学生分组选一种饮食文化深入研究，制作英文展示材料，课堂上介绍特点、渊源等，其他小组提问交流。

 Week 3: Unit 1: Culinary Trends and Cultural Contexts

- In - depth Culinary Culture Presentation：Each group selects one of the "Eight Great Cuisines of China", conducts in - depth research on the selected cuisine, creates English presentation materials, and introduces its characteristics and origins in class, with other groups asking questions and communicating.

第四周：第二单元：当代生活与传统映照

- 文化传播理论学习：深入解析文化维度理论中的集体主义与个人主义，结合中国 “和合理念” 分析其对当代生活的深层影响。

- 语言与符号解码：学习 “委婉社交”“家族观念” 等词汇及文化隐喻，通过中外社交行为对比视频（如 “已读不回” 现象）强化语言表达与文化关联。

 Week 4: Unit 2: Reflection of Contemporary Life and Tradition

- Study of Cultural Communication Theories：Conduct an in-depth analysis of collectivism and individualism within the cultural dimension theory, and analyze the profound influence of China's concept of "harmony and rationality" on contemporary life in combination with it.

- Decoding of Language and Symbols：Learn vocabulary such as "euphemistic social interaction" and "family concept" as well as cultural metaphors. Strengthen the connection between language expression and culture through comparison videos of social behaviors at home and abroad (such as the phenomenon of "seen but not replied" in messaging).

 第五周：第二单元：当代生活与传统映照

- 文化冲突研讨：分组分析 “点赞文化”“节日红包” 等场景的中外差异，探究文化根源并提出传播优化建议。

- 文化模拟交流：设定特定受众的文化场景，学生分组角色扮演，模拟交流互动，反思总结应对文化差异的方法。

 Week 5: Unit 2: Reflection of Contemporary Life and Tradition

- Cultural Conflict Seminar：Divide students into groups to analyze the differences between China and foreign countries in scenarios such as "like - culture" and "festival red envelopes", explore the cultural roots, and put forward suggestions for optimizing communication.

- Cultural Simulation Communication：Set up cultural scenarios for specific audiences. Students are divided into groups for role - playing, simulate communication and interaction, and reflect on and summarize methods for dealing with cultural differences.

 第六周：第三单元：时代演绎与文化符号

- 文化传播理论学习：引入媒介理论，介绍文化传播的媒介理论，探讨利用现代媒介传播中国文化符号的方法。

- 语言与符号解码：学习 “汉服”“国潮” 等关键词，整理国际社交媒体（如 TikTok）热门帖文中的高频表达，收集整理资料建素材库。

 Week 6: Unit 3: The Interpretation of the Era and Cultural Symbols 

- Cultural Communication Theory Study：Introduce media theory, explain the media theory of cultural communication, and explore methods of using modern media to spread Chinese cultural symbols.

- Language and Symbol Decoding：Learn key terms such as "Hanfu" and "Guochao". Organize frequently - used expressions from popular posts on international social media (such as TikTok), and collect and sort out materials to build a database.

 第七周：第三单元：时代演绎与文化符号

- 文化外译实践：教师提供含文化符号的文本，学生用异化、归化策略翻译并对比效果，小组讨论不同语境下的策略选择，教师总结评价。

 Week 7: Unit 3: The Interpretation of the Era and Cultural Symbols

- Cultural Translation Practice：Teachers provide texts containing cultural symbols. Students translate them using foreignization and domestication strategies, compare the effects, discuss the strategy selection in different contexts in groups, and teachers summarize and evaluate.

 第八周：第四单元：文学经典的当代解读

- 翻译理论学习：讲解翻译的忠实性与灵活性原则，结合文学经典翻译案例分析平衡方法。

- 语言与符号解码：学习“侠义精神”“诗词意象”等文学概念，分析英文译本中的文化留白与再创造。

 Week 8: Unit 4: Contemporary Interpretation of Literary Classics 

- Translation Theory Study：Explain the principles of fidelity and flexibility in translation, and analyze the balancing methods with reference to translation cases of literary classics.

- Language and Symbol Decoding：Learn literary concepts such as "chivalrous spirit" and "poetic imagery", and analyze the cultural blank spaces and re - creations in English translations.

 第九周：第四单元：文学经典的当代解读

- 文学经典对比分析：分组对比中国文学经典与西方相似情节、人物，从文化根源解读差异，制作英文PPT展示，其他小组提问讨论。

- 翻译实践与讨论：提供文学经典段落，学生按原则翻译，小组互评，推选优秀译文全班展示，教师点评，提升翻译能力与防范误读意识。

 Week 9: Unit 4: Contemporary Interpretation of Literary Classics

- Comparative Analysis of Literary Classics：Divide students into groups to compare Chinese literary classics with similar plots and characters in the West, interpret the differences from the cultural root, create English PPTs for presentation, and conduct Q&A and discussions with other groups.

- Translation Practice and Discussion：Provide paragraphs from literary classics. Students translate according to the principles, conduct peer reviews within groups, select excellent translations for class presentation, and receive teacher comments, so as to improve translation ability and the awareness of preventing misinterpretation.

 第十周：第五单元：日常实践与传统智慧

- 文化传播理论学习：解析文化休克与复原理论，结合“中医海外推广”案例探讨传统实践的国际接受路径。

- 语言与符号解码：学习“太极拳”“二十四节气”等术语，收集海外博主相关体验视频作为语料库。

 Week 10: Unit 5: Daily Practices and Traditional Wisdom 

- Cultural Communication Theory Study：Analyze the theory of culture shock and recovery, and explore the international acceptance paths of traditional practices in combination with the case of "the promotion of traditional Chinese medicine overseas".

- Language and Symbol Decoding：Learn terms such as "Tai Chi" and "the 24 Solar Terms", and collect experience videos of overseas bloggers as a corpus.

 第十一周：第五单元：日常实践与传统智慧

- 传播效果优化：针对文化休克场景，学生分组角色扮演，运用知识策略介绍实践活动，应对文化休克，反思总结提升交流能力。

 Week 11: Unit 5: Daily Practices and Traditional Wisdom

- Optimization of Communication Effect：For culture shock scenarios (such as the controversy over the practice of confinement), students role - play in groups, introduce practical activities using knowledge and strategies to deal with culture shock, reflect and summarize to improve communication skills.

 第十二周：第六单元：现代革新与当代价值观

- 文化传播理论学习：以党的二十大精神为指引，解析相关理念理念，探讨对中国式现代化及文化发展的影响，以“中国高铁出海”“抖音国际版”为例分析技术革新的文化附加值。

 Week 12: Unit 6: Modern Innovations and Contemporary Values 

- Cultural Communication Theory Study：Guided by the spirit of the 20th National Congress of the Communist Party of China, analyze concepts. Explore the influence of concepts on Chinese - style modernization and cultural development, and analyze the cultural added value of technological innovation through cases.

 第十三周：第六单元：现代革新与当代价值观

- 革新成果与二十大精神展示：选取“中国新能源技术”“数字政务”等案例，制作英文传播文案，突出其全球价值与文化独特性，展现中国在科技与社会治理领域的创新成果与文化自信。

 Week 13: Unit 6: Modern Innovations and Contemporary Values

- Exhibition of Innovation Achievements and the Spirit of the 20th CPC National Congress: Select cases such as "China's new energy technology" and "digital government affairs" to create English communication copies. Highlight their global value and cultural uniqueness, and demonstrate China's innovative achievements and cultural confidence in the fields of science and technology and social governance.

 第十四周：综合复习与案例拓展

- 知识综合复习：复盘六大单元文化传播理论脉络，构建“解码 - 转化 - 触达”能力模型，着重强调重点与难点内容。

- 学生制作的案例学习与互评：开展学生制作的视频案例分享活动，并组织学生进行相互评价。

 Week 14: Comprehensive Review and Case Expansion 

- Comprehensive Knowledge Review: Review the theoretical context of cultural communication in the six units, construct a competency model of "decoding - transformation - reaching", and emphasize the key and difficult points.

- Learning and Peer Evaluation of Student - Produced Cases: Carry out activities for sharing video cases produced by students and organize students to conduct peer evaluations.

 第十五周：答疑与期末考试

- 答疑与考试安排：进行期末考试，全面考查学生对中国文化知识点、文化传播知识的掌握程度，以及运用语言进行中国文化国际传播的实践能力。考试题型包括翻译、论述、案例分析等。

 Week 15: Q&A and Final Exam

- Q&A and Examination Arrangements: Conduct the final exam to comprehensively assess students' mastery of Chinese culture knowledge points, cultural communication knowledge, and practical ability to use language for the international communication of Chinese culture. The examination questions include translation, essays, case analysis, etc.

 第十六 - 十七周：阅卷与登分

- 阅卷与成绩管理：教师完成期末考试阅卷工作，统计成绩并录入系统，针对学生整体表现进行分析总结，为后续课程改进提供依据。

 Weeks 16 - 17: Marking and Grading

- Examination Paper Marking and Grade Management: Teachers complete the marking of the final exam papers, count the scores and enter them into the system, analyze and summarize the overall performance of students, and provide a basis for the improvement of subsequent courses.
