课程简介（中文）

本课程采用双语教学，介绍随机分析中的基本理论和方法，包括布朗运动、随机积分、伊藤公式、随机微分方程等，讨论如何将随机分析中的基本理论和方法应用到金融定价中，推导著名的Black-Scholes公式。

课程简介（英文）

This course adopts a bilingual teaching approach to introduce the fundamental theories and methods of stochastic analysis, including Brownian motion, stochastic integral, Itô's formula, and stochastic differential equations. It also discusses how to apply the basic theories and methods of stochastic analysis to financial pricing, culminating in the derivation of the famous Black-Scholes formula.

教学目标

本课程旨在引导学生深入理解随机分析在金融研究中的核心作用，从金融不确定性建模的视角出发，系统掌握布朗运动、随机积分、Itô引理等基本理论与方法。学生将通过Black-Scholes模型、风险中性定价及利率模型等应用案例，初步建立运用随机工具解决衍生品定价、风险管理等金融实际问题的分析能力，为后续高阶量化金融课程奠定扎实基础。

教学方式

课堂授课+讨论

教学内容与进度安排

  • 1st week (3 hours): Concepts of infinite probability spaces and two typical examples; concepts of random variables and distributions; computation of distribution measures.

  • 2nd week (3 hours): Concepts and properties of expectations; concepts of convergences of random variables and also of integrals; Monotone convergence theorem and Dominated convergence theorem.

  • 3rd week (3 hours): Computation of expectations; change of measures.

  • 4th week (3 hours): Concepts of σ-algebras and independence; equivalent conditions of independence of random variables; jointly normal distribution.

  • 5th week (3 hours): Concepts and properties of conditional expectations; the independence lemma; concepts of martingales and Markov processes.

  • 6th week (3 hours): Concepts and properties of random walk; limiting distribution of the scaled random walk; definition of Brownian motion.

  • 7th week (3 hours): Properties of Brownian motion; quadratic variation; volatility of geometric Brownian motion; Markov property.

  • 8th week (3 hours): Exponential martingale; the reflection principle; first passage time distribution.

  • 9th week (3 hours): Definition and properties of Ito integral.

  • 10th week (3 hours): Ito-Doeblin formula for Brownian motion and also for Ito process.

  • 11th week (3 hours): An important application of Ito formula: Black-Scholes-Merton equation.

  • 12th week (3 hours): Risk-Neutral measure and Girsanov’s theorem; pricing of European options under the Risk-Neutral measure.

  • 13th week (3 hours): Martingale representation theorem; hedging strategies for European options.

  • 14th week (3 hours): Introduction to stochastic differential equations; solve linear stochastic differential equations and several important examples.

  • 15th week (3 hours): The Markov property; connections with partial differential equations-Feynman-Kac theorems: proof and applications to pricing.

  • 16th week (3 hours): Revision and final exam.
