课程简介（中文）

主要教学内容同时也是要求学生掌握的内容包括：

各种现实网络介绍及网络的特征度量（3学时）；小世界网络、无尺度网络、加权网络、分形网络等复杂网络的演化机制与建模方法（6学时）；网络上的疾病、病毒传播的建模与算法（6学时）；网络安全与可靠性建模与算法（3学时）；网络的压缩算法（3学时）；网络上的导航与搜索算法（3学时）；网络社团结构的挖掘算法（3学时）；网络上的路由策略（3学时）；网络上的随机游走及网络流（6学时）；网络上的博弈（3学时）；网络上的谱分析方法及其应用（6学时）；基于网络的个性化推荐算法（3学时）；网络同步与控制算法（3学时）；网络在生物信息、金融信息等相关领域的应用（3学时）。

课程简介（英文）

The main teaching content, which students are also required to master, includes:

Introduction to various real-world networks and network characteristic measurements (3 hours); Evolutionary mechanisms and modeling methods for complex networks such as small-world networks, scale-free networks, weighted networks, and fractal networks (6 hours); Modeling and algorithms for disease and virus transmission on networks (6 hours); Network security and reliability modeling and algorithms (3 hours); Network compression algorithms (3 hours); Navigation and search algorithms on networks (3 hours); Algorithms for mining network community structures (3 hours); Routing strategies on networks (3 hours); Random walks and network flows on networks (6 hours); Game theory on networks (3 hours); Spectral analysis methods on networks and their applications (6 hours); Network-based personalized recommendation algorithms (3 hours); Network synchronization and control algorithms (3 hours); Applications of networks in bioinformatics, financial information, and related fields (3 hours).

教学目标

通过开设这一课程，让学生了解网络科学这一崭新交叉学科的发展历史、掌握其主要特征度量、熟悉经典网络模型的构造、掌握网络科学在计算机科学中的主要应用（网络建模、病毒传播、信息检索、路由策略等）、初步了解网络科学在计算机相关学科的应用（如生物信息学、金融信息学等）。
