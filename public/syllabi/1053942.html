课程简介（中文）

本课程专为国际本科留学生开设，此课程大纲针对第一学期教学。本课程涉及中国的国情概览、环境与资源、历史、政治、法律、经济和科技等七章。课程以基础知识点为纲，通过具体案例和数据的呈现，展现当代中国在经济发展、社会治理及全球化进程中的前沿探索，帮助学生由表及里地理解当代中国。

课程简介（英文）

Designed specifically for international undergraduate students, this syllabus outlines the curriculum for the first semester. The course covers seven core chapters: an overview of China's national conditions, environment and resources, history, politics, law, economy, and science & technology. Grounded in fundamental concepts, the course utilizes specific cases and data to showcase contemporary China's frontier explorations in economic development, social governance, and globalization, helping students gain a comprehensive and in-depth understanding of the country today.

教学目标

Knowledge: Build a Holistic Understanding of Contemporary China
Guide students to explore the essentials of China's geography, history, economy, and society. The course aims to help students move beyond fragmented facts to grasp the cultural logic and real-life stories behind the nation's development.

Perspective: Cultivate a Global and Interactive Outlook
Encourage students to examine China's development through a global lens. Through interactive discussions and collaborative projects, students will learn to appreciate diverse cultural backgrounds and articulate their own unique viewpoints in a multicultural classroom.

Thinking: Foster Critical and Empathetic Engagement
Inspire students to think independently rather than just memorizing information. The course focuses on developing the ability to connect course insights with personal interests and majors, promoting open-minded inquiry and empathetic communication with others.

 

教学方式

Interactive Lectures: This approach aims to transform passive listening into active intellectual engagement, helping students intuitively grasp the cultural logic and real-life stories behind China's development.
Collaborative Inquiry: Organize students into diverse, multicultural teams to explore specific themes related to contemporary China. Through regular group discussions and peer-to-peer learning, students will practice articulating their viewpoints, listening with empathy, and building mutual understanding across different cultural backgrounds.
Case-Based Exploration: Utilize vivid real-world cases, current social phenomena, and cultural products as primary learning materials. Instead of memorizing abstract theories, students will be guided to analyze concrete examples, fostering a holistic understanding of China's geography, history, economy, and social dynamics.
Project-Based Learning (PBL): Encourage students to connect course insights with their own majors and personal interests through creative projects. Whether it is a presentation, a short video, or a thematic report, this hands-on approach allows students to apply their knowledge flexibly and experience the joy of autonomous inquiry.
