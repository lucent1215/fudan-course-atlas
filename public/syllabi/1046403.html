课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲授一元函数微积分学的核心内容。课程涵盖函数的极限与连续性、导数与微分（含计算、中值定理及应用）、不定积分与定积分（含计算及几何物理应用），并对反常积分和常微分方程作初步介绍。旨在为学生构建扎实的微积分知识基础，培养严格的数学思维和运用微积分工具解决实际问题的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course systematically covers the core elements of single-variable calculus. It includes the limit and continuity of functions, derivatives and differentials (computation, mean value theorems, and applications), indefinite and definite integrals (computation and geometric/physical applications), and provides an introduction to improper integrals and ordinary differential equations. The course aims to build a solid foundation in calculus for students, cultivate rigorous mathematical thinking, and develop the ability to apply calculus tools to solve practical problems.

教学目标

通过微积分发展史的脉络与核心思想，培养学生的科学探索精神与严谨求实的学术品格，理解数学作为基础科学对国家科技自立自强的重要性；系统、严密地传授一元函数极限、连续、微分、积分及常微分方程初步的核心概念、基本理论、计算方法和重要应用，构建完整知识框架；着重训练学生的抽象思维、逻辑推理与精确计算能力，提升其运用数学模型分析和解决实际问题的综合能力，为未来从事学术研究或高技术行业工作奠定坚实基础。

By tracing the historical development and core ideas of calculus, this course aims to cultivate students' spirit of scientific exploration and their rigorous, truth-seeking academic character, as well as to help them understand the importance of mathematics as a foundational science for national technological self-reliance and self-strengthening. It systematically and rigorously teaches the core concepts, fundamental theories, computational methods, and key applications of limits, continuity, differentiation, integration of functions of one variable, and an introduction to ordinary differential equations, thereby helping students build a complete knowledge framework. The course places special emphasis on training students' abilities in abstract thinking, logical reasoning, and precise computation, while enhancing their comprehensive competence in using mathematical models to analyze and solve practical problems, thus laying a solid foundation for their future engagement in academic research or careers in high-tech industries.

教学内容与进度安排

第一周：导言；函数的概念；函数图像；函数的性质；复合函数；反函数；初等函数。无穷小量；无穷小量的运算；数列的极限的概念；数列极限的性质及四则运算法则。

 

第二周：收敛数列的性质；单调有界数列；Cauchy收敛准则。自变量趋于有限值时函数的极限性质；单侧极限；无穷远处的极限。函数极限的性质及四则运算法则；两个重要的极限。

 

第三周：函数在一点的连续性；函数的间断点；区间上的连续函数；闭区间上连续函数的性质；无穷小和无穷大的连续变量。曲线的渐近线。

 

第四周：微分的概念；导数的概念；导数的意义；微分的几何意义。初等函数的导数；四则运算的求导法则；复合函数求导的链式法则；反函数求导法则；对数求导法；高阶导数。

 

第五周：基本初等函数的微分公式；微分运算法则；一阶微分的形式不变性；隐函数求导法；参数方程确定的函数求导；微分的应用：近似计算、误差估计。

 

第六周：局部极值与Fermat定理；Rolle定理；微分学中值定理；Cauchy中值定理。

 

第七周：型的极限；型的极限；其它不定型的极限。带Peano余项的Taylor公式；带Lagrange余项的Taylor公式；Maclaurin公式。

 

第八周：函数的单调性；函数的极值；最大值和最小值；函数的凸性；曲线的拐点；函数图像的描绘。方程的近似求解。

 

第九周：定积分的概念，定积分的性质；原函数；微积分基本定理。不定积分的基本公式；第一类换元积分法（凑微分法）；第二类换元积分法。

 

第十周：不定积分的分部积分法；有理函数的积分；某些无理函数的积分；三角函数有理式的积分。

 

第十一周：定积分的分部积分法；换元积分法；数值积分：梯形公式、抛物线公式（Simpson公式）。定积分的应用：面积问题；已知平行截面面积求体积；旋转体的体积。

 

第十二周：曲线的弧长；旋转曲面的面积；由分布密度求分布总量：质量、引力、液体对垂直壁的压力；动态过程的累积效应：功。

 

第十三周：无穷限的反常积分；比较判别法；无界函数的反常积分；Cauchy主值积分；Γ函数；Β函数。

 

第十四周：常微分方程的概念。变量可分离方程；齐次方程；全微分方程；一阶线性方程；Bernoulli方程。


第十五周：二阶线性微分方程；线性微分方程的解的结构；二阶常系数齐次方程的通解；二阶常系数非齐次方程；Euler方程。

 

第十六周：形式为的方程；形式为方程；形式为的方程。微分方程的幂级数解法。常系数线性微分方程组简介。


第十七周、第十八周：答疑、考试周、批卷阅卷、登分。

Week 1: Introduction; concept of functions; graphs of functions; properties of functions; composite functions; inverse functions; elementary functions. Infinitesimals; operations on infinitesimals; concept of limit of a sequence; properties of sequence limits and the four arithmetic operation rules.

 

Week 2: Properties of convergent sequences; monotone bounded sequences; Cauchy convergence criterion. Properties of limits of functions as the independent variable approaches a finite value; one-sided limits; limits at infinity. Properties of function limits and the four arithmetic operation rules; two important limits.

 

Week 3: Continuity of functions at a point; discontinuities of functions; continuous functions on an interval; properties of continuous functions on closed intervals; continuity of infinitesimals and infinities. Asymptotes of curves.

 

Week 4: Concept of differentials; concept of derivatives; meaning of derivatives; geometric meaning of differentials. Derivatives of elementary functions; differentiation rules for the four arithmetic operations; chain rule for composite functions; differentiation rule for inverse functions; logarithmic differentiation; higher-order derivatives.

 

Week 5: Differential formulas for basic elementary functions; differential operation rules; form invariance of first-order differentials; implicit differentiation; differentiation of functions defined by parametric equations; applications of differentials: approximate calculations, error estimation.

 

Week 6: Local extrema and Fermat's theorem; Rolle's theorem; mean value theorem in differential calculus; Cauchy's mean value theorem.

 

Week 7: Limits of the form 0/0; limits of the form ∞/∞; limits of other indeterminate forms. Taylor's formula with Peano remainder; Taylor's formula with Lagrange remainder; Maclaurin's formula.

 

Week 8: Monotonicity of functions; extrema of functions; maximum and minimum values; convexity of functions; inflection points of curves; graphing of functions. Approximate solution of equations.

 

Week 9: Concept of definite integrals; properties of definite integrals; antiderivatives; fundamental theorem of calculus. Basic formulas of indefinite integrals; first substitution method (composition of differentials); second substitution method.

 

Week 10: Integration by parts for indefinite integrals; integration of rational functions; integration of certain irrational functions; integration of trigonometric rational expressions.

 

Week 11: Integration by parts for definite integrals; substitution methods for definite integrals; numerical integration: trapezoidal rule, parabolic rule (Simpson's rule). Applications of definite integrals: area problems; finding volumes given cross-sectional areas; volumes of solids of revolution.

 

Week 12: Arc length of curves; surface area of solids of revolution; finding total quantities from distribution densities: mass, gravitational force, pressure on a vertical wall due to a liquid; cumulative effects of dynamic processes: work.

 

Week 13: Improper integrals with infinite limits; comparison tests; improper integrals of unbounded functions; Cauchy principal value; gamma function; beta function.

 

Week 14: Metric spaces. Open and closed sets, boundary of sets, their properties. Continuity and compactness, homeomorphisms. Properties of continuous mappings on compact sets. Pointwise convergence and uniform convergence of functions. Continuity of the limiting function.

 

Week 15: Pointwise continuity and uniform continuity of functions. The Cantor theorem on uniform continuity of functions on the compact sets. Lipschitz mappings and contraction mappings. Banach fixed-point principle for contraction mappings.

 

Week 16: Concepts of ordinary differential equations. Separable equations; homogeneous equations; exact equations; first-order linear equations; Bernoulli equations.

 

Weeks 17–18: Review sessions, examination week, grading, score recording.
