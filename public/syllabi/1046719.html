课程简介（中文）

本课程包括一系列约30部英语短篇小说代表作品的细读和批评式赏析，其中作者主要涵盖包括英国、美国、爱尔兰、加拿大等英语国家的著名作家群。

课程简介（英文）

The course consists of a series of interpretive and critical readings of about 30 English short story masterpieces, by authors ranging from British, American (for the early stage of the course development), Irish, Canadian and Australian (to be added as replacements later on).

教学目标

The course aims at introducing to the students the art of short story writing and the cultural messages therein contained, as well as the way to interpret and write critically on short stories.

教学方式

lecture series，in-class discussion, ex-class reading assignments
