课程简介（中文）

本课程以各类主题讲座及当代社会热点话题为切入点，紧密结合演讲、研讨、专题访谈等优质音视频素材，系统开展英语听力与口语技能训练。在听力维度，聚焦信息抓取核心能力，有效应对不同口音、语速的学术听力场景；在口语维度，以听力素材为基础，通过话题复述、观点阐释、讨论辩论等分层任务，训练学生清晰表达观点的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to systematically develop students’ English listening and speaking skills. It takes a wide range of academic and current social topics as entry points, closely integrating rich audio and video materials to enable students to practice and master various methods and techniques for capturing information. At the same time, it broadens the scope of listening content and helps students engage in relatively in-depth discussions on specific topics or phenomena. 

教学目标

The course Academic English: Listening and Speaking is designed for learners at the Developing Stage (Levels 5–6) of the China’s Standards of English Language Ability (CSE). It aims to cultivate students’ listening comprehension and oral communication skills. Upon completion of the course, students are expected to approach Level 7 of the CSE in listening and speaking.

教学方式

Multimedia-based instruction

Group and class discussions
