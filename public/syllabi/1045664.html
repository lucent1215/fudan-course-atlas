课程简介（中文）

本课程系统讲授一元函数微积分学的核心内容。课程涵盖函数的极限与连续性、导数与微分（含计算、中值定理及应用）、不定积分与定积分（含计算及几何物理应用），并对反常积分和常微分方程作初步介绍。旨在为学生构建扎实的微积分知识基础，培养严格的数学思维和运用微积分工具解决实际问题的能力。

课程简介（英文）

This course systematically covers the core elements of single-variable calculus. It includes the limit and continuity of functions, derivatives and differentials (computation, mean value theorems, and applications), indefinite and definite integrals (computation and geometric/physical applications), and provides an introduction to improper integrals and ordinary differential equations. The course aims to build a solid foundation in calculus for students, cultivate rigorous mathematical thinking, and develop the ability to apply calculus tools to solve practical problems.

教学目标

通过微积分发展史的脉络与核心思想，培养学生的科学探索精神与严谨求实的学术品格，理解数学作为基础科学对国家科技自立自强的重要性；系统、严密地传授一元函数极限、连续、微分、积分及常微分方程初步的核心概念、基本理论、计算方法和重要应用，构建完整知识框架；着重训练学生的抽象思维、逻辑推理与精确计算能力，提升其运用数学模型分析和解决实际问题的综合能力，为未来从事学术研究或高技术行业工作奠定坚实基础。

Through tracing the main threads and core ideas in the history of calculus, this course cultivates students’ spirit of scientific exploration and rigorous, truth-seeking academic character, and helps them understand the importance of mathematics as a basic science to national scientific and technological self-reliance and strength. It systematically and rigorously imparts the core concepts, fundamental theories, computational methods, and important applications of limits, continuity, differentiation, and integration of single-variable functions, as well as elementary ordinary differential equations, thereby building a complete knowledge framework. Emphasis is placed on training students’ abstract thinking, logical reasoning, and precise computational abilities, enhancing their comprehensive capacity to use mathematical models to analyze and solve practical problems, and laying a solid foundation for future careers in academic research or high-tech industries.

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1: Introduction; the concept of function; graphs of functions; properties of functions; composite functions; inverse functions; elementary functions. Infinitesimals; operations with infinitesimals; the concept of the limit of a sequence; properties of limits of sequences and the algebraic limit laws.

Week 2: Properties of convergent sequences; monotone bounded sequences; Cauchy convergence criterion. Properties of limits of functions as the independent variable approaches a finite value; one-sided limits; limits at infinity. Properties of limits of functions and the algebraic limit laws; two important limits.

Week 3: Continuity of a function at a point; points of discontinuity; continuous functions on intervals; properties of continuous functions on closed intervals; infinitesimals and infinitely large continuous variables. Asymptotes of curves.

Week 4: The concept of differential; the concept of derivative; the meaning of derivative; geometric meaning of the differential. Derivatives of elementary functions; differentiation rules for arithmetic operations; chain rule for differentiating composite functions; differentiation rule for inverse functions; logarithmic differentiation; higher-order derivatives.

Week 5: Differential formulas for basic elementary functions; rules for operating with differentials; invariance of the first differential form; implicit differentiation; differentiation of functions defined by parametric equations; applications of differentials: approximate computation, error estimation.

Week 6: Local extrema and Fermat’s theorem; Rolle’s theorem; mean value theorem of differential calculus; Cauchy’s mean value theorem.

Week 7: Limits of the form ...; limits of the form ...; limits of other indeterminate forms. Taylor’s formula with Peano remainder; Taylor’s formula with Lagrange remainder; Maclaurin’s formula.

Week 8: Monotonicity of functions; extrema of functions; maximum and minimum values; convexity of functions; inflection points of curves; sketching graphs of functions. Approximate solution of equations.

Week 9: The concept of the definite integral; properties of the definite integral; antiderivatives; the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus. Basic formulas for indefinite integrals; integration by substitution of the first kind (recognizing differentials); integration by substitution of the second kind.

Week 10: Integration by parts for indefinite integrals; integrals of rational functions; integrals of some irrational functions; integrals of rational expressions in trigonometric functions.

Week 11: Integration by parts for definite integrals; integration by substitution; numerical integration: trapezoidal rule, parabolic rule (Simpson’s rule). Applications of definite integrals: area problems; volumes from known parallel cross-sectional areas; volumes of solids of revolution.

Week 12: Arc length of curves; area of surfaces of revolution; finding total quantities from distribution densities: mass, gravitational force, pressure of a liquid on a vertical wall; cumulative effects of dynamic processes: work.

Week 13: Improper integrals over infinite intervals; comparison test; improper integrals of unbounded functions; Cauchy principal value integral; Gamma function; Beta function.

Week 14: Concept of ordinary differential equations. Separable equations; homogeneous equations; exact differential equations; first-order linear equations; Bernoulli equations.

Week 15: Second-order linear differential equations; structure of solutions of linear differential equations; general solution of second-order homogeneous equations with constant coefficients; second-order nonhomogeneous equations with constant coefficients; Euler equations.

Week 16: Equations of the form ...; equations of the form ...; equations of the form ... . Power series solution of differential equations. Introduction to systems of linear differential equations with constant coefficients.

Weeks 17 and 18: Q&A, examination weeks, grading, and recording scores.
