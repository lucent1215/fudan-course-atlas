课程简介（中文）

科创企业的创始人团队及其他利益相关者需要深入了解作为公司制度和机制“顶层设计”的公司治理，进而在创业实践中解决诸如（1）科创企业股权架构如何设计？（2）如何运用公司治理控制科创企业？（3）科创企业如何运用股票期权激励？以及（4）如何构建有效的科创企业治理架构和具体机制，为科创企业注入内生增长动力和内生增长能力？等一系列重要问题。

科创企业是典型的技术高度密集型、人力资本高度密集型的企业，其所面临的人力资本的动态性问题要比一般企业要严重得多，因而非常需要在公司治理层面做动态性的制度安排与机制设计。“动态”可以说是科创企业治理的灵魂。有鉴于此，课程主要内容包括：

  • 公司治理与科创企业治理：剖析公司治理的基本问题，进而阐释公司治理的理论框架；讨论科创企业的特点与科创企业治理的一般逻辑框架。
  • 科创企业股权架构设计：讨论谁能成为合伙人，股权架构原则，股权蛋糕怎么切，创始合伙人的份额分配，股权架构的法律设计等问题；而后给出股权架构设计实例并进行“股权分配和股权架构设计”的课堂游戏。
  • 科创企业控制权竞争：从股权控制、董事会控制等层面讨论如何设计具体、可行的公司治理制度和机制实施对科创企业的有效和持续的控制。
  • 科创企业股权激励计划：介绍股票期权制度，分析股票期权激励的核心价值和股票期权激励的误区，介绍美国股票期权制度，分析股权激励模式与股权激励实践，尝试设计科创企业股票期权激励机制。
  • 科创企业动态股权治理平台：基于对华为案例的分析，尝试对创造华为奇迹的制度优势进行解析，并进一步讨论华为特色制度机制的适应性；而后，我们给出了科创企业动态股权治理平台的一般性设计并讨论其可能达到的公司治理效应。

课程简介（英文）

Founders and other stakeholders of tech-innovation enterprise need to understand corporate governance—the "top design" of corporate institutions and mechanisms, and thus solve a series of important problems in the business practice, such as (1) How to design corporate ownership structure? (2) How to use corporate governance to control tech-innovation enterprise? (3) Tech-innovation enterprise how to use executive stock options? And (4) How to build an effective tech-innovation enterprise governance structure and specific governance mechanisms to enhance endogenous growth motivation and endogenous growth capacity of tech-innovation enterprise?

Tech-innovation enterprises are typical ones with high technology intensity and high human capital intensity. The dynamic problem of human capital they face is much more serious than that of ordinary enterprises. Therefore, it is very necessary to make dynamic institutional arrangements and mechanism design at the level of corporate governance. "Dynamic" can be said to be the soul of tech-innovation enterprise governance. In view of this, the course includes the following these parts:

  • Chapter 1, Corporate Governance and Tech-innovation Enterprise Governance: to analyze basic issues of corporate governance and illustrate theorectical framework of corporate governance; and to discuss the characteristics of tech-innovation enterprises and the general logical framework of tech-innovation enterprise governance.
  • Chapter 2, Ownership structure design of tech-innovation enterprise: to discuss partners of tech-innovation enterprise, principles of ownership structure, how to cut equity cake, shares distribution of founding partners, the legal design of ownership structure. Then to give some examples of ownership structure design and carry out the classroom game of "ownership allocation and ownership structure design".
  • Chapter 3, Control rights competition of tech-innovation enterprise: From the perspectives of equity control, the board control and etc., to analyze how to design specific, practical corporate governance institutions and mechanisms to realize effective and sustained control of tech-innovation enterprise.
  • Chapter 4, Executive stock options of tech-innovation enterprise: To introduce executive stock option, to analyze the core value of executive stock option and the errors of executive stock option, and then to introduce the American stock option system, to analyze the equity incentive model and equity incentive practice, to try to design executive stock option of tech-innovation enterprise.
  • Chapter 5, Dynamic Equity Governance Platform of tech-innovation enterprise: the chapter, based on the case study of Huawei, attempts to analyze institutional strengths resulting in Huawei miracle and to further discuss adaptation of Huawei special institutions and mechanisms design. Then, we give the general design of dynamic equity governance platform of tech-innovation enterprise and discuss its possible effects of corporate governance.

教学目标

1）介绍公司治理的基本问题和公司治理的理论框架；探讨科创企业的特点与科创企业治理的一般逻辑框架。

2）在理论和实践层面深入讨论科创企业股权架构设计、科创企业控制权竞争、科创企业股权激励计划、科创企业治理架构和具体治理机制设计等一系列重要问题，完善科创企业赖以生存并获得持续成功的最为重要的内部制度和机制保障。

3）培养能将公司治理理论与科创企业治理实践相结合的创新创业人才。
