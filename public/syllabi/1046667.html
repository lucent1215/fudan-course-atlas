课程简介（中文）

本课程将带领学生探索日常生活中隐藏的毒物危机，揭示其对健康的潜在影响。从生活住家、工作场所到社会环境，课程将揭开毒物的神秘面纱。通过分析空气污染、食品毒素、生活场所毒物、药物中毒等案例，深入探讨毒物知识、监管法规及防治实践。通过案例分析和互动讨论，培养学生的问题解决能力和批判思维，使其在面对现实生活中的毒物挑战时游刃有余。

课程简介（英文）

This course will guide students in exploring the hidden crises of toxins in daily life, revealing their potential impacts on health. From homes and workplaces to the broader social environment, the course will unveil the mysteries of toxins. Through case studies on air pollution, food toxins, household poisons, workplace hazardous substances, and drug poisoning, it will delve into toxin knowledge, regulatory standards, and prevention practices. Through case analysis and interactive discussions, the course aims to cultivate students' problem-solving skills and critical thinking, enabling them to navigate real-life toxin challenges with confidence.

教学目标

这门课程不仅仅是关于毒物的知识，更是关于如何保护自己和我们所珍视的环境。我们将穿插社会热点事件，以毒理学的角度对毒物中毒的经典案例进行深入解读。通过了解这些案例，学生将能够更好地认识有害因素，培养安全意识、环保意识和可持续发展观念，培养出对社会热点问题的敏感度和解决问题的能力，以及提升自我保护意识。

This course is not just about the knowledge of toxins, but also about how to protect ourselves and the environment we cherish. We will incorporate social hot topics and provide in-depth interpretations of classic cases of poisoning from a toxicological perspective. By studying these cases, students will gain a better understanding of harmful factors, develop safety awareness, environmental consciousness, and a sense of sustainable development. They will also cultivate sensitivity to social issues, enhance problem-solving skills, and strengthen their self-protection awareness.

教学方式

本课程将主要采用讲授式教学，期间不定期穿插互动对话与讨论。课程最终将以翻转课堂和期末成果汇报作为总结，让学生能够主动参与并展示对课程内容的理解与掌握。

The course will primarily adopt a lecture-based format, with periodic interactive dialogues and discussions interspersed throughout. The learning experience will culminate in a flipped classroom session and a final presentation, allowing students to actively engage with and demonstrate their understanding of the course material.
