课程简介（中文）

本课程设置八大宏观主题，深入剖析自新石器时代延续至今人类发展史上的重大历史事件与核心议题。课程除常规课堂讲授外，特设四场专题辩论研讨课。各教学模块将安排 1–4 个课时进行全面讲解，每课时包含两节学分授课时段，单次授课时长 1.5 小时。以下为课程详细教学安排：

模块一：经济史导论（1 课时）

模块二：石器时代至古代帝国崛起（2 课时 + 1 节补充研讨课时）

模块三：近代早期经济增长（3 课时 + 1 节补充研讨课时）

模块四：新大陆、哥伦布大交换与黑奴贸易（3 课时 + 1 节补充研讨课时）

模块五：工业革命与大分流（3 课时 + 1 节补充研讨课时）

模块六：世界大战（2 课时）

模块七：战后世界重建（3 课时 + 1 节补充研讨课时）

课程简介（英文）

The course includes eight broadly defined topics that delve into significant events and issues in the history of humanity, spanning from the Neolithic era to the present day. In addition to our regular lectures, we have allocated four sessions dedicated to debates. Each module will be comprehensively explored within one to four sessions, with each session consisting of two credit-hour lecture periods, totaling 1.5 hours each. Below are the detailed particulars of our course:

  • lModule 1: Introduction to Economic History (1 session)
  • lModule 2: From Stone Age to the Rise of Ancient Empires (2 sessions+1 catch-up)
  • lModule 3: Early Modern Growth (3 sessions+1 catch-up)
  • lModule 4: The New World, Columbian Exchange & Slave Trade (3 sessions+1 catch-up)
  • lModule 5: The Industrial Revolution and the Great Divergence (3 sessions+1 catch-up)
  • lModule 6: The Great Wars (2 sessions)

lModule 7: Rebuilding the World (3 sessions+1 catch-up)

教学目标

This course has been meticulously crafted to offer students a unique opportunity to grasp the long-term trajectory of global economic development, spanning from the dawn of civilization to the present day. It will delve into pivotal historical milestones, including the birth of civilization, religious reform movements, the Industrial Revolution, the Columbian Exchange, the Black Death, the Great Depression, the World Wars, and more. Diverging from typical history courses that primarily focus on historical facts, this course seamlessly melds economics with history and history with economics. Upon completing the course, you will not only possess a profound comprehension of the historical roots underpinning our contemporary economic and social systems but also recognize how the study of history can profoundly enrich your understanding of economic theory. You will gain a deeper insight into the intricate processes governing economic and social development, their causes, and their far-reaching consequences. Furthermore, you will be astutely attuned to ongoing transformations, better equipping yourself for the challenges of the future.

教学方式

Lecture, Q and A sessions, discussions. Class participation is highly recommended. Students are encouraged to share their thoughts or current works in class.
