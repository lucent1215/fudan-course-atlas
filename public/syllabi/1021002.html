课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在为本科生打开一扇通往了解宗教世界和宗教研究的大门，引导学生以客观、理性的态度审视这一复杂的社会文化现象。课程内容主要分为三大模块：首先，介绍宗教学学科的诞生，了解宗教的定义方式，掌握宗教学的诸子学科和内部逻辑，概述宗教学的基本对象、任务和规范。其次，介绍宗教学各个子学科的内容和理论来源及其传统，包括宗教人类学、宗教现象学、宗教心理学、宗教社会学等。再者，邀请宗教学系的各位老师对世界各大宗教和本土宗教进行概述，包括佛教、基督教、伊斯兰教等世界主要宗教的梳理，介绍其基本教义、历史演变与核心仪式等。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to open a door for undergraduate students to understand the world of religion and the study of religion, guiding them to examine this complex social and cultural phenomenon with an objective and rational attitude. The course content is divided into three main modules: First, it introduces the emergence of the discipline of religious studies, explores the ways in which religion is defined, acquaints students with the various sub-disciplines and internal logic of religious studies, and outlines the fundamental subject matter, tasks, and norms of the field. Second, it presents the content and theoretical origins and traditions of the various sub-disciplines of religious studies, including the anthropology of religion, the phenomenology of religion, the psychology of religion, and the sociology of religion. Third, it invites faculty members from the Department of Religious Studies to provide an overview of the world's major religions and local religions, including a systematic introduction to major world religions such as Buddhism, Christianity, and Islam, presenting their basic doctrines, historical evolution, and core rituals, among other aspects.

教学目标

让学生对作为一种文化现象的宗教的本质和功能有一定的认识，对宗教学研究领域的方法有一定的了解，对宗教学研究书目有一定水准的认识；掌握宗教学的基本知识点，对现实世界的宗教现象能做识别和评判；提高对宗教学的兴趣，对宗教学的理论著作阅读理解的能力，自学能力及撰写学习报告的能力。提升同学们对社会现象和人文价值的理论关切，推进思考深度，对社会价值和信仰体系的重建有所参与。
