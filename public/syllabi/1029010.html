课程简介（中文）

The course  covers three core themes:

1)Global health-related concepts, including global health, global health governance, global health research, health diplomacy,etc;

2)Specific practices in global health governance;

3)global health practice in diffference areas.

This course will seek to present the current state of health in the global health field, its role in improving the health of the population, and its future prospects with detailed data and  case studies.

课程简介（英文）

The course  covers three core themes:

1)Global health-related concepts, including global health, global health governance, global health research, health diplomacy,etc;

教学目标

This course is designed to familiarize students with the relevant aspects of global health governance and practice and to understand the role of global health with the context of globalization. 

教学方式

Lecture and case discussion
