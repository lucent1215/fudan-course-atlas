课程简介（中文）

20世纪初，西方史学在方法、对象与问题意识上经历了深刻转型。马克·布洛赫和吕西安·费弗尔开创的年鉴学派，对传统政治史与事件史提出反思，强调历史研究应关注社会整体、人的经验与现实问题。本课程以布洛赫的《为历史学辩护》和费弗尔的《为历史而战》为主要研读文本，引导学生思考“为什么历史学可爱”“历史学有什么用”等基本问题。课程通过文本细读与课堂讨论相结合的方式，帮助学生理解史学经典的思想内涵，培养主动阅读、表达与讨论的能力。本课程不要求学生具备阅读法语原文的能力。课堂以中文译本为主要文本，教师将结合原文法语进行必要的对照讲解，特别关注词语选择、句法节奏与语义张力，以帮助学生体会史学原典在不同语言中的表达方式及其背后的思想意涵。

课程简介（英文）

In the early twentieth century, Western historiography underwent a profound transformation in its methods, objects of inquiry, and historical problematics. Created by Marc Bloch and Lucien Febvre, the Annales School reflected critically on traditional political and event-centered history, emphasizing the study of society as a whole, human experience, and the relationship between history and contemporary concerns.

This course takes The Historian’s Craft by Marc Bloch and Combats pour l’histoire by Lucien Febvre as its main reading texts, guiding students to reflect on fundamental questions such as “Why do we love history?” and “What is history useful for?” Through a combination of close textual reading and classroom discussion, the course aims to help students understand the intellectual significance of classic historiographical works and to develop their ability to read, articulate, and discuss ideas actively.

This course does not require students to have the ability to read French original texts. Classes are based primarily on Chinese translations; however, the instructor will provide selective comparisons with the French originals, with particular attention to word choice, syntactic rhythm, and semantic tension, in order to help students appreciate how historiographical ideas are expressed across different languages and the intellectual implications behind such expressions.

教学目标

本课程立足学校人文教育与拔尖人才培养目标，引导学生在教师指导下研读20世纪西方史学原典，理解其写作背景、问题意识与学术关怀，认识现代史学反思的基本立场与价值取向。课程通过文本细读、课堂讨论与学生发言相结合的方式，强化学生对经典文本的理解能力、表达能力与回应能力，培养主动思考、理性讨论与批判性阅读的学术素养，帮助学生形成对历史学作为一种知识实践与思想活动的整体认识。
