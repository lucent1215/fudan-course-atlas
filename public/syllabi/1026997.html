课程简介（中文）

《社会计算导论》课程内容包括社会计算领域的起源与发展、研究范式与方法、典型应用与平台，社会计算中的贡献激励、承诺鼓励、行为规范、新用户处置、新平台的开辟等核心问题和挑战，社会计算中常用的社会科学理论及发展的特色理论，社会计算中的实验研究方法、调查研究方法、人种学研究方法以及其他典型研究方法，社会计算中的基于日志的人类行为建模与理解等。要求学生熟悉并使用多种社会计算应用或平台，并对社会计算应用或平台的使用有所理解和思考；有探究社会计算平台中独特或涌现社会现象、过程和现实的好奇心；并有基本的统计、编程和文献阅读、思辨、报告撰写能力；具有较好的想法实践与执行能力，以及项目实现、部署与实证能力。

课程简介（英文）

The course "Introduction to Social Computing" covers the origins and development of the field, research paradigms and methodologies, typical applications and platforms, as well as core issues and challenges such as contribution incentives, commitment encouragement, behavioral norms, handling of new users, and the establishment of new platforms in social computing. It also includes commonly used social science theories in social computing, along with distinctive theories developed within the field. The course addresses experimental research methods, survey research methods, ethnographic research methods, and other typical research approaches in social computing, as well as human behavior modeling and understanding based on logs in social computing. Students are required to familiarize themselves with and utilize various social computing applications or platforms, demonstrating an understanding and reflection on their usage. They should exhibit curiosity in exploring unique or emergent social phenomena, processes, and realities within social computing platforms. Additionally, students must possess basic statistical, programming, and literature reading skills, critical thinking, and report writing abilities. They should also demonstrate strong conceptualization, practical execution, project implementation, deployment, and empirical validation capabilities.

教学目标

帮助学生了解社会计算这门“以人为中心”融合计算科学与社会科学的前沿跨学科，掌握社会计算相关的模型、理论、原则、技术、方法，了解典型的社会计算的应用和平台，并能合理运用这些理论、方法和技术，围绕信息社会典型场景中的复杂现象、过程和现实，进行计算建模、行为分析、交互设计、体验改善和应用构建。
