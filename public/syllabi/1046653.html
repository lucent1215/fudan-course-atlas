课程简介（中文）

德国思想巨擘马克斯·韦伯在20世纪初，以其深邃的洞察力发现了欧洲文明进程中宗教信仰对近代资本主义诞生做出的特殊贡献，从而提出了不同于马克思“经济基础决定上层建筑”的历史发展观，并由此引发了一系列引人深思的文化、社会和宗教学问题。本课程将以对马克斯·韦伯的《新教伦理与资本主义精神》的深度阅读为基础，让学生对韦伯的宗教社会学研究有初步的了解，进而深入思考文化与社会互动的基本关系，提升学生的人文素养和培养独立思维精神。
本课程在系统介绍韦伯《新教伦理与资本主义精神》背景的基础上，让学生直接、深入阅读文本本身，同时补充一些欧洲文化史的相关内容（文艺复兴、新教改革、启蒙运动、资本主义兴起），并穿插一些韦伯社会研究方法论的基本观念（例如理想型、价值中立、选择亲缘性等），使得学生深入思考文化和社会之间的双向、互动的关系。此外，本课程还将着重培养学生扩展阅读（韦伯的其他著作，如《儒教与道教》、《经济与社会》等）和独立思考的能力。如有可能，本课程还将引导大家来反思个人的生活选择，例如对时间、金钱和职业的态度。

课程简介（英文）

The German intellectual giant Max Weber, in the early 20th century, used his profound insight to uncover the special contribution that religious faith made to the emergence of modern capitalism within the context of European civilization. In doing so, he put forward a view of historical development distinct from Marx's thesis that "the economic base determines the superstructure," thereby giving rise to a series of thought-provoking cultural, social, and religious questions. This course, based on an in-depth reading of Max Weber's The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism, aims to give students a preliminary understanding of Weber's sociology of religion, to further reflect on the fundamental relationship between culture and social interaction, and to enhance their humanistic literacy and cultivate independent thinking.

On the basis of systematically introducing the background to Weber's The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism, this course will have students engage directly and deeply with the text itself, while supplementing it with relevant content from European cultural history (the Renaissance, the Reformation, the Enlightenment, and the rise of capitalism) and interspersing some basic concepts from Weber's methodology of social science (such as ideal type, value neutrality, and elective affinity). This will enable students to think deeply about the two-way, interactive relationship between culture and society. In addition, the course will place particular emphasis on developing students' abilities in extended reading (of Weber's other works, such as The Religion of China and Economy and Society and independent thinking. Where possible, the course will also guide students to reflect on their own life choices, such as their attitudes toward time, money, and vocation.

马克斯·韦伯著、林荣远译：《经济与社会》，北京：商务印书馆，1997年。[韦伯思想的集大成著作，篇幅较大，可以有选择地阅读。推荐指数：★★☆☆☆]
