课程简介（中文）

近年来，蓬勃发展的考古发掘与研究，为揭示源远流长、博大精深中国历史和文化提供了重要的科学依据和物质支撑。本课程将以介绍重要考古发现及相关研究为线索，从“衣、食、住、行”等角度阐释中国传统文化的精髓，旨在让学生，尤其留学生能学学习和了解中国的地理、历史和物质文化的发展规律，透物见人，更深层次地认知中国文化的内涵。本课程为学生提供了中国考古学和中国历史的概要性介绍。通过研究物质文化和历史文献，探索中华文明的起源与发展，学生将从中学习考古学方法与理论及其在中国历史与文化研究中的应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a general introduction to Chinese archaeology and Chinese history.   It explores the origin and development of Chinese civilization through the investigation of material culture and historical texts. Students will learn about the methods and theories of archaeology as applied to the studies of Chinese history and culture.

教学目标

In recent years, the vigorous archaeological excavation and research have provided important material evidence for revealing the long, extensive and profound Chinese history and culture. This course illustrates the essence of Chinese traditional culture from various aspects based on important archaeological discoveries and related studies. It helps students to get familiar with China's geography, history and the development of material culture, so as to understand contents of Chinese culture and civilization at a deeper level.

By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1）Understand the basic principles and methods of archaeological research

2）Recognize the origin and formation process of Chinese civilization and Chinese culture through archaeological discoveries and studies.

3）Analyze the relationship between material culture and social, political, and economic systems in ancient China.

4）Evaluate the role of archaeology in understanding Chinese culture and history.

5）Develop critical thinking through the analysis of archaeological data.

教学内容与进度安排

 

Weeks

Topics

Objectives

Week 1 （Hu）

Introduction to Archaeology and Chinese History

· Overview of the course structure, expectations, and assessment methods.

· Define archaeology as a discipline: its scope, methods, and relationship with history and anthropology.

· Explore the significance of archaeology in reconstructing Chinese civilization, understanding cultural continuity and change, and correcting historical biases.

Week 2（Hu）

Methodology and Procedures of Archaeology

· Trace the evolution of archaeological methods from antiquarianism to scientific excavation

· Describe the process of an archaeological project, from research design to publication.

· Understand the role of scientific analysis in archaeological researches

Week 3 （Hu）

Introduction to Chinese Geography and Environment

· Identify China’s major geographic regions and the key geographical features

· Analyze how geographic factors influenced the development of distinct cultural cores

· Understand the impact of human-environment interactions on the formation of Chinese civilization   

Week 4 （Hu）

Introduction to Chinese History (I)

· Provide a chronological overview of early Chinese history from prehistoric to Bronze Age

· Introduce key archaeological cultures in Prehistoric periods

· Discuss the Xia, Shang, Zhou dynasties and their archaeological evidence

Week 5 （Hu）

Introduction to Chinese History (II)

· Continue the historical overview of China during the imperial periods.

Week 6  (Hu)

Archaeology in Shanghai

· Challenge the perception of Shanghai as a "modern-only" city by exploring its deep archaeological past

· Introduce important archaeological discoveries and key sites in Shanghai

· Explore how local heritage is preserved and presented

Week 7 （Hu&Xue）

Presentation I

· Introduce the geography in your country

· Discuss how it shaped or influenced the formation of cultures and civilizations

Week 8 （Xue）

Museum Visiting  

· Evaluate how museum exhibitions help visitors understand archaeological excavations and findings

· Experience the historical and archaeological contexts in a vivid and engaging manner

Week 9 （Xue）

Origin of Agriculture in China

· Basic concepts and major theories of the origin of agriculture

·  Archaeological sciences applied to the study of agriculture and domestication

· Archaeological discoveries about the origin and development of agriculture in north and south China

Week 10 （Xue）

Jades in Prehistoric China

· The historical and cultural significance of jades in China

· Major archaeological discoveries of jades in prehistoric period

· Jades as symbols of social status and power, and their role in ritual practices

Week 11 （Xue）

Metallurgy and Ritual Bronzes 

· The origin and spread of metallurgy

· Major discoveries of ritual bronzes in early China

· The ritual system represented by the manipulation of bronze vessels and its profound impact on Chinese culture 

Week 12 （Xue）

Urbanization and the emergence of complex societies in ancient China 

· The relationship between urbanization and social complexity

· The process of urbanization and state formation in ancient China

· Major archaeological discoveries of urban centers in prehistoric and early historic period of China

Week 13（Xue）

Pottery and Porcelain

· Distinguish between earthenware, stoneware, and porcelain based on material and firing technology.

· Identify the major stylistic and technological developments in Chinese ceramics from the Neolithic to the Han Dynasty.

· Analyze how pottery serves as a key tool for archaeologists in dating sites and tracing cultural interactions.

· Discuss the social and economic roles of pottery, from everyday use to elite burial goods  

Week 14（Xue）

Presentation II

· Give a presentation based on archaeological discoveries and researches related to China in your country

Week 15（Xue）

Review for Final Exam

·  Integrate key concepts, chronologies, and archaeological discoveries from the entire course.

· Clarify any remaining questions about major themes and case studies.

· Practice exam-style questions and review effective study strategies.

Week 16 （Xue）

Final Exam
