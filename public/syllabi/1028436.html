课程简介（中文）

本课程通过七个单元（每单元2周）向医学生系统传授基础医学英语知识，涵盖中医、神经、泌尿生殖、内分泌系统等核心主题。课程采用真实医学文本、互动活动与人工智能探索相结合的教学模式，每个单元包含以下6大模块：视频导入、术语解码、基础阅读、拓展阅读、语言训练、AI探索区

课程简介（英文）

This course introduces medical students to essential medical English through seven units (2 weeks per unit), covering foundational topics such as Diagnosis, Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), and Cardiovascular System. Designed for beginners, the course emphasizes scaffolded learning with authentic texts, interactive activities, and guided AI exploration. Each unit follows a structured framework:

  • Viewing to Warm Up: Short, engaging videos (2–5 minutes) with basic comprehension exercises (e.g., true/false, matching).
  • Medical Terminology: Focus on root words and prefixes/suffixes, reinforced through crossword puzzles and flashcards.
  • Reading for the Basics: Text with phonetic guides, vocabulary lists, and content-based fill-in-the-blank exercises.
  • Expanded Reading: Text on medical ethics and cultural topics, paired with guided group discussions.
  • Language Skill Development: Beginner-friendly grammar exercises, basic translation tasks, and vocabulary quizzes.
  • AI-Empowered Exploration: Structured AI tasks (e.g., Ask ChatGPT about diabetes prevention) with step-by-step reflection prompts.

教学目标

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

  • understand foundational medical terminology (e.g., diagnosis, endocrine, sensory systems) and apply it in basic clinical and academic contexts.
  • interpret authentic medical texts with guided vocabulary support.
  • develop basic language skills through structured exercises in grammar, translation, and oral communication.
  • explore medical humanities through multi-dimensional topics (e.g., patient empathy, TCM basics) to cultivate ethical awareness.
  • use AI tools for introductory research tasks, focusing on information gathering and basic critical evaluation.
  • engage with multimodal resources (videos, interactive games) to enhance learning engagement and adaptability.

教学方式

  • Interactive Lectures: Teacher-led explanations of terminology and simplified case studies (e.g., Common diagnostic tools).
  • Gamified Learning: Flashcards, crossword races, and vocabulary bingo to reinforce retention.
  • Structured Group Work:
  • Role-Plays: Simulate basic clinical interactions (e.g., "Explaining a prescription to a patient").
  • Peer Feedback Sessions: Review AI project drafts in small groups.
  • Multimedia Support: QR codes linking to animated videos and online learning at Chaoxing platform.
