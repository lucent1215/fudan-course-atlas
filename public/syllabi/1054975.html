课程简介（中文）

本英语口译课程专为非英语专业学生设计，旨在提升其语言能力并掌握实用的口译技能。课程从基础概念入手，逐步深入到交替传译、接续传译及初级同声传译的强化训练，这些技能高度适用于广泛的学术与社会场景。课程将理论教学与沉浸式实践相结合，涵盖学术研讨会、文化交流活动、非正式商务会议等模拟场景。通过构架式小组练习、案例分析及个人实践，可培养学生不同语言间进行精准意义转换的能力。

同时，贯穿学期的系统速记训练，可夯实学生听力理解与信息记录的核心能力，强化信息抓取的即时性，助力学生从容应对国内标准化英语考试与海外英语能力测评的听力、口语模块。

课程简介（英文）

This English Interpretation course is tailored for non-English majors seeking to enhance their language proficiency and acquire practical interpretation skills. The curriculum commences with fundamental concepts, gradually progressing to in-depth training in alternative interpretation, consecutive interpretation and primary simultaneous interpretation that are highly applicable to a wide range of academic and social contexts. The course integrates theoretical instruction with immersive practical sessions, featuring simulated scenarios such as academic seminars, cultural exchange activities and informal business meetings. Through structured group exercises, case analyses and individual practice, students will develop the proficiency to convey meaning precisely between languages. 

Meanwhile, systematic note‑taking training throughout the semester consolidates students’ core competencies in listening comprehension and information notation, and enhances the immediacy of information capture, helping students confidently navigate the listening and speaking modules of both domestic standardized English examinations and international English‑proficiency assessments.

教学目标

Students will systematically master core techniques, including efficient note-taking methodologies, rapid semantic conversion strategies, and the ability to maintain linguistic accuracy under immense time pressure. Upon completion, learners will be well-equipped to navigate multilingual communication effectively, enhancing their competitiveness in both academic pursuits and future professional endeavors.

学生将系统掌握核心技巧，包括高效笔记法、快速语义转换策略，以及在高强度时间压力下保持语言准确性的能力。课程结束后，学习者将具备有效应对多语言沟通的能力，从而在学术追求与未来职业发展中提升竞争力。

教学方式

讲授为主；课堂学习以教师讲授为主，并辅以相关师生互动以及学生实操练习。

教学内容与进度安排

Week 1｜Course Overview

Introducing course features and specific course assessment criteria to orient students to course objectives and interpreting‑training requirements, conducting warm-up exercises

Week 2-3｜Social media

Acquiring domain‑specific terminologies on social‑media topics, exploring trending social media news report, introducing basic interpreting concepts.

Week 4-5｜Travel & culture

Studying travel‑culture‑related expressions and practicing interpreting themed excerpts to consolidate note‑taking strategies for cultural‑narrative content.

Week 6-7｜Foreign reception

Mastering conventional formulas for foreign‑reception scenarios and conducting relevant interpreting drills to equip students with note‑taking skills for ceremonial discourse.

Week 8-10｜Education

Grasping core educational terminologies and interpreting subject‑related texts to deepen thematic knowledge and refine information‑notation competencies.

Week 11-12｜Economy & trade

Learning key economic‑trade jargon and practicing interpreting business‑oriented materials to strengthen instant information capture for expository passages.

Week 13-14｜Artificial intelligence

Acquiring AI‑relevant terminology and interpreting technology‑themed segments to hone note‑taking performance for tech‑focused listening input.

Week 15｜Urban life

Processing vocabulary and discourse excerpts concerning urban life and conducting interpreting exercises to reinforce thematic note‑taking application.

Week 16｜Q & A session

Addressing students’ persistent questions across all themes and reviewing core interpreting and note‑taking takeaways of the whole course.
