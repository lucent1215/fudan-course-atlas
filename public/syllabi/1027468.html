课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在透过跨文化的视角，围绕汉语诗歌独特的音乐性传统，用中英双语帮助留学生了解、欣赏并感受中国古典诗乐之美。课程内容涵盖从先秦《诗经》、楚辞到唐诗、宋词及元曲的演变脉络 。教学特色在于将诗学批评与吟诵吟唱相结合 ，通过讲授与工作坊实践 ，帮助留学生跨越语言壁垒，亲身体验汉语的韵律美感与中国文化的博大精深 。

 
 
 
 
 

 

课程简介（英文）

Adopting a cross-cultural perspective, this course explores “shige” (song-word) to understand how Chinese people express feelings about life and the universe. It highlights the unique musical tradition of Classical Chinese poetry. Over 16 weeks, students will explore major genres from the Pre-Qin Book of Songs to Yuan Dynasty Qu poetry. The curriculum creatively integrates Chinese poetics, musicology, and vocal performance, combining theoretical criticism with practical reciting and chanting workshops. By participating in lectures, seminars, and a final poetry recital, students will overcome language barriers to experience the charm of Chinese language and culture through an engaging, performance-based approach.

教学目标

本课程立足复旦大学通识教育与国际化人才培养目标，针对留学生跨文化学习特点，旨在通过“诗乐合一”的教学实现三维目标：

  1. 知识传授：构建汉语诗歌生成与发展的历史框架，使学生掌握从《诗经》到元曲的中国古典诗乐主要美学特征与演变脉络 。
  2. 能力培养：通过吟诵与吟唱的艺术实践，训练学生对汉语韵律的感知力，提升其跨文化艺术欣赏与学术思考能力 。
  3. 价值引领：引导学生透过诗意表达深刻体认中国文化的精神内核与东方智慧，探索文明互鉴的可能途径，发现文明对话的空间 。

教学方式

本课程采取“理论讲授与实践互动相结合”的混合教学模式 ，旨在通过多元化的教学组织形式实现教学目标：

  1. 专题讲授与体验式教学交替进行：课程共设6个专题，每个专题历时2周。第一周重点讲授相关理论与作品批评；第二周为互动实践课堂，通过“欣赏、示范与模仿”进行诗歌吟诵与吟唱体验，让学生在身体力行中感知诗乐魅力 。
  2. 跨学科综合教学法：课程综合运用诗学、音乐学、文字学及表演学的理论方法 ，打破单一学科壁垒，将文学批评与艺术表演深度融合，引导学生从多维视角理解中国诗乐文化。
  3. 工作坊与任务驱动：课程后段设置8学时的“身体、生命与诗性表达”工作坊 。通过小组课题研究汇报、讨论以及专业的诗歌排练，最终以举办一场“诗乐吟诵会”作为结课汇报 。这种形式鼓励学生主动参与，从排练到演出全方位锻炼其跨文化表达与协作能力。

UNIT1. INTRODUCTION 

  • Week 1: The Musical Tradition of the Chinese Poetry
  • Week 2: Major Aspects of Chinese Poetry
  • Themes, Genres and Subgenres, Oral and Literati Traditions, Prosody, Structure
  • Rhythm, Syntax and Vision of Chinese Poetry

UNIT2. POETRY IN PRE-QIN TIMES

  • Week 3: The Book of Poetry (Shijing) & The Lyrics of Chu (Chuci)
  • Tetrasyllabic Shi Poetry: The Book of Poetry (Shijing)
  • Sao Poetry: The Lyrics of Chu (Chuci)
  • Week 4: Poetry Experience: Reciting and Chanting the Chinese Poems

UNIT3. POETRY IN THE HAN DYNASTY

  • Week 5: Music Bureau Poems (Yuefu), The “Nineteen Old” and An Ancient-Style Rhapsody (Gufu)
  • Shi Poetry: Music Bureau Poems (Yuefu)
  • Pentasyllabic Shi Poetry: The “Nineteen Old Poems”
  • Week 6: Poetry Experience: Reciting and Chanting the Chinese Poems

UNIT4. Shi POETRY IN THE TANG DYNASTY

  • Week 7: Recent-Style Shi Poetry & Ancient-Style Shi Poetry
  • Regulated Verse (Lvshi)
  • Quatrains (Jueju)
  • Ancient-Style Shi Poetry
  • Week 8: Poetry Experience: Reciting and Chanting the Chinese Poems

UNIT5. Ci POETRY IN THE SONG DYNASTY

  • Week 9: Short Song Lyrics (Xiaoling) and Long Song Lyrics (Manci)
  • Week 10: Poetry Experience: Reciting and Chanting the Chinese Poems

UNIT6. Qu POETRY IN THE YUAN DYNASTY

  • Week 11: Qu Poetry: Song Poems (Sanqu) of the Yuan Dynasty
  • Week 12: Poetry Experience: Reciting and Chanting the Chinese Poems

WORKSHOP: BODY, LIFE AND POETIC EXPRESSION

  • Week 13: Group Presentations & Discussions I + Rehearsal I
  • Week 14: Group Presentations & Discussions II + Rehearsal II
  • Week 15: A Poetry Recital + Overall Rehearsal
  • Week 16: Course Conclusion & Final Paper Submission
