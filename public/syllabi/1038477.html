课程简介（中文）

本课程介绍了北欧国家的社会、政治和文化特征，以及现代北欧国家和社会如何区别于其他社会、政治和文化体系。在探讨“北欧模式”及其可持续性的过程中，每堂讲座都会围绕一个特定主题展开，内容涵盖从宏观历史比较、去中心化政策、福利国家的变化与挑战、多元文化、家庭政策、福利治理、高等教育到健康政策等各个方面。课程共包含14次讲座，其中大部分将在现场进行。剩余的在线讲座将利用分组讨论进行，后进行统一总结和反馈。讲师们来自不同的北欧大学，并分别位于不同的北欧国家。

课程简介（英文）

The course consists of 14 lectures, with most of them taught on-site. The remaining online lectures will use break-out rooms for discussions and reflections, and plenaries for summaries and feed-back. The lecturers, representing different Nordic universities and based in different Nordic countries, are all keen to engage in discussions, and to be challenged, on ways to understand and interpret welfare state policies and developments. The course also includes a workshop dedicated to preparing you for writing your course paper.

教学目标

This course presents the social, political and cultural characteristics of the Nordic countries and how the modern Nordic countries and societies – taken as a whole – can be distinguished from other social, political and cultural systems. Examining the 'Nordic model’ and its sustainability, each lecture explores a specific theme, ranging from macro-historical comparisons, through decentralization policies, changes and challenges to the welfare state, its multiculturalism, family policies, welfare governance, higher education, and health policies. The course explores the rich sociocultural aspects of Nordic societies, covering also themes such as sustainable development, Nordic visual culture and developments in the aquaculture sector.

教学方式

Mixed with on-site and online lectures.
