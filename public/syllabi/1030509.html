课程简介（中文）

泰语（二）课程以泰语读写基础能力培养为核心，循序渐进地引导学生从认识泰文字母，发展到能够自信地阅读和书写句子及短段落。

本课程重点包括：

  • 理解泰语辅音类别及声调规则
  • 掌握元音、双元音及辅音连读（辅音丛）
  • 通过读写训练实现准确的声调发音
  • 识别不规则拼写与特殊符号
  • 逐步进行构词训练、句子阅读及简短对话练习

课程结束时，学生将能够解码陌生的泰语词汇，阅读简短文本，并在日常语境中进行基础的泰语书面表达。

课程简介（英文）

Thai Language 2 focuses on foundational Thai literacy, guiding students from recognizing Thai script to reading and writing sentences and short paragraphs with confidence.
The course emphasizes:

  • Understanding Thai consonant classes and tone rules
  • Mastery of vowels, diphthongs, and consonant clusters
  • Accurate tone production through literacy
  • Recognition of irregular spellings and special symbols
  • Progressive word building, sentence reading, and short conversations

By the end of the course, students will be able to decode unfamiliar Thai words, read short texts, and produce basic written Thai in daily contexts.

教学目标

This course aims to develop students’ foundational Thai literacy through a structured and progressive approach to reading and writing. By the end of the course, students will be able to:

  1. Recognize and accurately write the full set of Thai consonants, vowels, diphthongs, and common orthographic forms.
  2. Distinguish Thai consonants by sound, class (middle, high, low), and positional function (initial and final).
  3. Apply Thai tone rules by integrating knowledge of consonant classes, vowel length, and tone marks.
  4. Decode unfamiliar Thai words through systematic analysis of sound, spelling, and syllable structure.
  5. Read Thai words, sentences, and short paragraphs with basic comprehension.
  6. Produce simple written Thai sentences and short dialogues in familiar, everyday contexts.
  7. Develop confidence in reading Thai script independently and engaging with basic written Thai materials.

 

教学方式

This course adopts a student-centered, phonics-based, and discovery-oriented approach to Thai literacy learning. Teaching methods include:

  • Sound-first instruction
    Emphasizing listening and sound recognition before formal rule explanation, allowing students to build intuitive phonological awareness.
  • Guided discovery learning
    Students actively group, compare, and analyze Thai letters and sounds to discover patterns (e.g., sound families and consonant classes) before formal terminology is introduced.
  • Scaffolded literacy development
    Instruction progresses from symbol recognition → sound grouping → word building → sentence and paragraph reading, with gradual increases in complexity.
  • Interactive and collaborative activities
    Including card sorting, movement-based classification, pair and group discussions, and problem-solving tasks to reinforce understanding.
  • Integrated reading and writing practice
    Writing activities reinforce reading skills through stroke practice, dictation, sentence construction, and short written dialogues.
  • Flipped classroom elements
    Pre-class exposure (charts, audio recordings, and simple review tasks) is used to maximize in-class time for practice, discussion, and clarification.
  • Continuous formative feedback
    Ongoing teacher feedback during class activities helps students correct errors early and build confidence without high-stakes pressure.
