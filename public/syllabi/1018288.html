课程简介（中文）

围绕细胞的结构和功能，从单个细胞到细胞与环境的相互作用，由浅入深地展开教学。

课程简介（英文）

CORE CONCEPTS OF CELL BIOLOGY CURRICULUM
• Structural basis of functions
• Signal transduction
• Interactions: compartments within the cell; or the cell with its environment

The course will be taught using a blended learning approach, with videos hosted on https://2.teach.bio

教学目标

通过教学使顺利完成学习的学生：
• 能勾画细胞生物学的学科知识体系并概述相关知识点
• 能举例说明细胞及亚细胞结构如何参与不同的生物学过程
• 能解释并简单评价细胞生物学领域的研究论文
• 对前沿细胞生物学的研究具备好奇心

Students who successfully complete the course will be able to:
• Outline the disciplinary knowledge framework of cell biology and summarize related key concepts
• Provide examples illustrating how cells and subcellular structures participate in various biological processes
• Interpret and briefly evaluate research papers in the field of cell biology
• Develop curiosity regarding cutting-edge research in cell biology
