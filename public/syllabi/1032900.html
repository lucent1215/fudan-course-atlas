课程简介（中文）

针对学生在计算理论领域的高阶内容，特别是计算复杂性、密码学基础、单向函数、伪随机、概率检查证明、交互式证明、随机计算、量子计算，安全协议、进行系统性地教学与训练。本课程的核心是系统介绍计算理论的高级内容，包括计算复杂性、密码学基础、单向函数、伪随机、概率检查证明、交互式证明、随机计算、量子计算、安全协议等。该课程需要学生前期修过离散数学、概率论、信息安全数学基础等课程。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to systematically teach and train students on advanced topics in the field of computational theory, particularly including computational complexity, fundamentals of cryptography, one-way functions, pseudo-randomness, probabilistically checkable proofs, interactive proofs, randomized computation, quantum computation, and security protocols. The core of this course is to systematically introduce advanced contents of computational theory, such as computational complexity, fundamentals of cryptography, one-way functions, pseudo-randomness, probabilistically checkable proofs, interactive proofs, randomized computation, quantum computation, and security protocols. Students are required to have completed courses such as discrete mathematics, probability theory, and mathematical foundations of information security before taking this course.
