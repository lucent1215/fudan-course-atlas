课程简介（中文）

本课程面向已具备一定英语口语能力的学生，旨在全面培养学生的英语公众演讲技能

课程简介（英文）

A public speaking course aimed at teaching comprehensive English public speaking skills to students with existing oral English skills.

教学目标

Teaching Objectives:

  • To build your self-confidence in English public speaking and communication;
  • To develop your ability to write effective English public speeches;
  • To develop your ability to effectively deliver English public speeches;
  • To develop your responsibility as a public speaker;
  • To develop your listening responsibility and skills;
  • To develop your research

教学方式

The course is heavily student led with teaching focusing on short lectures and examples given in class which students are then expected to rehearse in groups. 

教学内容与进度安排

Teaching Plan:

Week 1

 

-          Course overview

-          Reading assignment, APS chapters 1, 2 & 3

Week 2

-          Managing speech anxiety

-          Ethics in public speaking

-          Instructions for the first speech

Week 3

Speech Day

-          Students deliver introductory speeches (10%)

Week 4

 

-          Topic selection for the informative speech; audience analysis

-          Making a specific purpose statement (SPS)

-          Reading, APS chapter 10 (language)

Week 5

 

-          Avoiding language problems; using language devices

-          Information gap activity

-          Read APS, 11

Week 6

-          Speech delivery

-          Read APS 6, 7 & 8

Week 7

-          Using evidence; organizing the speech (introduction, body & conclusion)

-          Instructions for the informative speech

-          Review of students’ SPS

Week 8

Speech Day

-          Students give the recitation speech (10%)

-          Read APS, 9, 12 & 13

Week 9

-          Outlining  the speech; using visual aids; speaking to inform

-          Review of informative speech outlines

Week 10

Speech Day

-          Students deliver informative speech (20%)

-          Read APS, 15

Week 11

 

-          Students finish delivering informative speeches

-          Speaking on special occasions

Week 12

Speech Day

-          Students deliver ceremonial speech (10%)

-          Read APS, 14

Weeks 13 and 14

-          Speaking to persuade

-          Instructions on persuasive speech

Week 15

Speech Day

-          Students deliver persuasive speech (30%)

Week 16

-          Students deliver persuasive speech
