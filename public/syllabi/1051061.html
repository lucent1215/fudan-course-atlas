课程简介（中文）

    本课程是为已掌握600词左右或通过了HSK三级的汉语学习者设计。课程重点在于听读简单的叙述性文本（100-200字）以及日常实用材料，并通过小组讨论活动训练口语技能。同时，讲解文本时都配有相应的图片或视频，以帮助学生熟悉现实生活中的汉语语言环境。为强化对语言点的理解和阅读技能，课程中包含了多种练习，如完型填空、选择题、判断题、词汇积累和话题拓展活动。

课程简介（英文）

    This course is designed for Chinese learners who have mastered around 600 words or have passed HSK Level 3. The focus of the course lies in listening and reading simple narrative texts (100-200 words) and daily practical materials, and training oral skills through group discussion activities. Authentic images and videos with text are also provided to help students become familiar with real-life Chinese language environments. To enhance understanding of language points and reading skills, a variety of exercises are included—such as information completion, multiple-choice questions, true/false judgments, vocabulary building and topic extension activities.

教学目标

        本课程旨在培养学生快速阅读并根据语境推断生词意思的能力。同时，本课程还强调理解正式书面语言，并教授识别文本风格和作者语气的技巧。学生在拓展词汇量和语法知识的同时，也将培养阅读正式书面中文的专业技能以及用流利的口语表达自己观点的能力。另外，为学生提供进一步学习、工作和旅行中文的基础，通过文本学习和社交互动，鼓励学生认识和欣赏来自不同文化背景的人们的不同观点， 激发好奇心、创造力和终身学习语言的乐趣。

       This course aims to develop students' ability to read quickly and infer the meaning of new words from context. It also emphasizes understanding promotional language and introduces techniques for identifying a text’s style and the author’s tone. Students will expand their vocabulary and grammar knowledge while cultivating specialized skills in reading formal written Chinese and the ability to express one's own views in fluent spoken language. In addition, students are provided with the foundation for further studying, working, and traveling in Chinese. Through text learning and social interaction, students are encouraged to recognize and appreciate different perspectives from people with diverse cultural backgrounds, stimulating curiosity, creativity, and the joy of lifelong language learning.

教学方式

1.通过视觉辅助引入关键词汇
2.通过听对话来理解新单词的意思
3.使用新学的词汇造句
4. 阅读文本并用自己的话进行总结
5.通过撰写短文来练习应用新单词
6.通过互动游戏或小组讨论来拓展学习，以加深对词汇的掌握

1.Introducing key vocabulary with visuals

2.Grasping the meaning of new words through listening to conversations

3.Constructing sentences using the newly learned vocabulary

4.Reading the text and summarizing it in one's own words

5.Applying new words in practice by writing short passages

6.Expanding learning with interactive games or group discussions to deepen word mastery
