教学目标

 

通过教师引导讲授、学生阅读论文与课堂讨论、选定应用场景研究等方式，促使学生理解目前网络体系结构、协议机制和应用研究的最新进展

 

帮助学生学习和掌握在网络领域如何进行科研工作

 

在教师的指导下，完成课程项目

（1）     就网络某一题目，提出自己的新见解，并进行理论论证或者系统认证，完成研究报告或论文；

（2）     就网络某一应用场景，结合所学，提出解决或者优化方案，并尝试原型验证或者实现初步应用；

（3）     在阅读最新文献基础上进行分析和总结，完成高质量的综述报告或者论文；

 

This course can help students to learn the knowledge and techniques in related computer network research area. During the course, the students need to understand the architecture, protocol, and application for state-of-the-art computer networks. Specifically, the main targets of this course are as follows.

(1) The students need to provide a new insight for a given topic in computer network research area and verify their correctness by theoretical analysis or system experiments. Accordingly, the students need to finish a research report or paper.

(2) The students need to propose an optimization proposal and implement a prototype system for a given application scenario.

(3) The students need to finish a high quality survey report or paper by summarizing current research papers.

教学方式

老师讲授、学生汇报、师生交流等。
