课程简介（中文）

本课程自2017年夏季以来已成功开设五轮，广受学生好评，是一门面向不同专业背景来华留学生的通识教育选修课。课程旨在为国际学生提供深入了解上海医疗服务体系真实面貌的宝贵机会。

课程采用“课堂讲授+实地参访+结课展示”相结合的教学模式。学员将在授课教师的带领下，走进上海市各级医疗卫生机构，亲身观察医疗服务实际运作过程，系统收集相关信息，并进行横向比较与分析。

通过本课程的学习，学生不仅能够增进对中国城市医疗卫生体系的直观认识，也将在跨文化观察与调研中提升批判性思维和学术表达能力。

欢迎对公共卫生、医疗服务或中国社会发展感兴趣的国际学生积极选修，一同开启这场富有深度与现实意义的探索之旅。

课程简介（英文）

The course has been implemented five times since the summer of 2017 and set a high value. It is a general education elective course, designed for the international students with different education background. Provide you the opportunity of exploring the truth of Healthcare service in Shanghai.

The course contains lectures, on site visiting and final presentation.

Students who are involved need to follow the lecturer, observe what happens in Shanghai’s healthcare facility, gather the information and draw a parallel.

教学目标

The course will be open to all foreign students in different majors, focus on introducing the diversity and rapid development of healthcare service in Shanghai.

Shanghai has a reputation for having the best medical facilities in China’s mainland, serving the 25 million permanent population and the residents from the other provinces. Even for the expats, there is no worry in finding quality healthcare or transportation when needed. The healthcare system in Shanghai includes public hospitals, private hospitals, international hospitals or clinics for the foreigners (like Parkway and United Family Hospital). The public hospitals are the mainstream of healthcare system, divided into three levels: tertiary, secondly and primary care center, and some of them are combined into partnership. The application of IT, including AI and mobile Health helps the rapid evolving in Shanghai, for example, cloud/digital hospital. The medical cost and mode of payment also differ from each other.

In general, it is acceptable for different kind of patients and there must be one pattern suitable for each person.

教学方式

Lecture, on site visiting, small group discussion
