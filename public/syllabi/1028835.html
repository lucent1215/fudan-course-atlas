课程简介（中文）

“病原生物与人类”课程以病原生物及其感染性疾病危害人类健康为切入点，引导学生从历史、地理、社会和生物学相结合的维度来理解病原生物与人类及自然的关系，思考人类迁徙、疾病流行和人体免疫、遗传、生产生活方式、生态环境改变之间的相互影响，理解动态演变中的病原生物及疾病的概念，认识到保持人和自然和谐、可持续发展的重要性，并引导学生以实际行动保障人类、动物和环境的健康，关爱弱势群体，尊重生命价值。

课程简介（英文）

Starting from the perspective of pathogens and their infectious diseases endangering human health, this course guides students to understand the relationship between pathogens and humans as well as nature through an integrated lens of history, geography, society, and biology. It encourages students to reflect on the interplay among human migration, disease outbreaks, and factors such as human immunity, genetics, production and lifestyle patterns, and ecological changes. The course also helps students grasp the concept of dynamically evolving pathogens and diseases, recognize the importance of maintaining harmony between humans and nature for sustainable development, and inspires them to take concrete actions to safeguard the health of humans, animals, and the environment, care for vulnerable groups, and respect the value of life.

知识目标（充分利用平台内外的各种AI工具助力知识目标的完成。）

  2. 说出控制感染性疾病流行三环节的具体内容，并能举例说明；

作业：提交一份不限字数和形式的游学日记（备选作业之一）或在研讨区交流分享或在下次课分享收获。

研讨学习要求：（充分引入AI工具）
