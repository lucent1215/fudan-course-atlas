课程简介（中文）

全面介绍全球与中国人口的发展及其老龄化趋势、需求及对社会发展带来的影响；老年健康特征与综合评估、老年内在能力评估；健康老龄化（WHO的ICOPE），WHO健康老龄化策略和中国健康老龄化策略：国内外健康老龄化的社会保障、整合性公共卫生体系，健康服务体系、长期护理服务体系；国内外典型案例分析：如日本长期护理体系和上海社区和居家养老服务体系建设等。

教学要求：要求学生了解全球与中国人口的发展及其老龄化趋势、需求及对社会发展带来的影响；熟悉老年健康特征与综合评估、老年内在能力评估；掌握健康老龄化概念，WHO健康老龄化策略和中国健康老龄化策略：熟悉国内外健康老龄化的社会保障、整合性公共卫生体系，健康服务体系、长期护理服务体系。能够运用健康老龄化理论与知识，为应对老龄化和建设适老型社会的国家健康战略而服务。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides a comprehensive overview of population development and aging trends at both global and national levels, examining their associated demands and far-reaching impacts on social development. Key topics include the health characteristics and comprehensive geriatric assessment of older adults, with a focus on evaluating functional capacity. The course explores the concept of healthy aging through the lens of the WHO's ICOPE framework, alongside the WHO's global strategy and China's national strategy for healthy aging. System-level discussions cover social security systems, integrated public health systems, health service systems, and long-term care service systems, drawing on both domestic and international perspectives. The course also features comparative case studies, including Japan's long-term care insurance system and Shanghai's community-based and home-based elderly care service model.

Upon completion of this course, students will be able to:
  • Understand global and Chinese population development, aging trends, associated needs, and their impacts on social development.
  • Be familiar with the health characteristics, comprehensive assessment, and evaluation of intrinsic capacity in older adults.
  • Master the concept of healthy aging, as well as the WHO's and China's strategies for healthy aging.
  • Gain knowledge of social security systems, integrated public health systems, healthcare services, and long-term care services related to healthy aging, from both domestic and international perspectives.
  • Apply theories and knowledge of healthy aging to support national health strategies aimed at addressing population aging and building an age-friendly society.

教学目标

通过本课程引导学生关注全球老龄化，特别是我国人口老龄化的发展趋势、需求及对社会发展带来的影响，学习老年健康特征与评估、健康老龄化及其策略、国内外老年社会保障和社会养老服务体系，结合国内外典型案例深入讨论分析健康老龄化、长期护理保障体系的实践，以问题为导向，培养学生老龄社会实际问题的观念认知与能力，增强学生的人口老龄化国情意识，推动老龄社会时代背景下的创新型、应用型、技能型人才培养，为积极应对人口老龄化、建成老年友好型社会提供坚实的专业人才保障。
