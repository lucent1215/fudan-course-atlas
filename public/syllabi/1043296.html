课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在从宏观视角系统介绍中国古代主流思想，聚焦于三大核心哲学流派——儒学、道学与禅宗。课程不仅深入探讨各学派在形而上学、道德修养与人性论等方面的理论体系，更注重在比较视域下审视不同思想之间的异同与交融，从而彰显中国哲学思维方式的独特魅力与深刻洞见。

通过本课程的学习，学生将获得理解中国古代思想整体脉络的基础框架，培养跨文本、跨流派的比较分析能力，并在东西方哲学对话的背景下，深化对中国传统文化精神内核的体认。

本课程适合对中国哲学、比较哲学或中国文化感兴趣的各层次学生修读，期待与您一同走进儒道禅的思想世界，展开一场跨越千年的智慧对话。

课程简介（英文）

This course will provide a high-level introduction to mainstream ancient Chinese thought by focusing on the doctrines of three pivotal philosophical schools, namely, Confucianism, Daoism and Chan Buddhism. Apart from discussions on their theories of metaphysics, moral cultivation, and human nature, their viewpoints will also be constantly examined from a comparative perspective in order to further demonstrate the uniqueness of their philosophical reasoning.

教学目标

  1. Familiarize students with the history of Chinese thought.
  2. Help students gain a systematic knowledge of the philosophy of Confucianism, Daoism and Buddhism.
  3. Enhance students’ academic research and writing ability.
  4. Enhance students’ presentation skills.

教学方式

Lecture instruction plus discussion.
