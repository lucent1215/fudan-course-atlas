考核与成绩构成

  • 出勤Attendance 15.0% Full attendance earns full points for this component. Each unexcused absence will result in a deduction of 3 points. Every two instances of tardiness will be counted as one absence. Students who are absent for more than one‑third of the total class hours will receive 0 points for this component.
  • 课堂表现Participation 15.0% This is assessed according to the frequency and quality of active engagement (e.g., voluntarily answering questions, raising insightful questions, and leading group discussions). Students who attend class but do not interact actively will receive 60% of the weight allocated to this component; those who make active contributions will receive 80%–100%, depending on their performance.
  • 作业/实验/实践Assignment 20.0% Evaluation criteria include: (1) timely submission; (2) completeness; (3) accuracy and logical coherence of answers; and (4) quality of laboratory/practical reports (where applicable). Each assignment is graded on a 100‑point scale. The final score for this component is calculated as the average of all assignment scores, multiplied by 20%.
  • 期末考试Final Examination：闭卷 50.0% The score is calculated directly from the raw score earned on the exam.

课程简介（中文）

本课程面向已完成约一学期（或300学时）汉语学习的学习者。课程聚焦于阅读简单叙事文本（100–200字）及日常实用材料，如通知、海报等。课程提供真实图文资料，帮助学生熟悉现实生活中的汉语语言环境。为强化阅读技能，设置多种练习形式，包括信息填空、选择题、正误判断、词汇扩展及话题延伸活动。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed for learners who have completed approximately one semester or 300 hours of Chinese study. It focuses on reading simple narrative texts (100–200characters) and everyday practical materials such as notices and posters. Authentic images with text are also provided to help students become familiar with real-life Chinese language environments. To strengthen reading skills, a variety of exercises are included—such as information completion, multiple-choice questions, true/false judgments, vocabulary building and topic extension activities.

教学目标

This course is strictly aligned with the HSK Level 4 test syllabus and is designed to help intermediate learners, who have completed approximately 300 hours of Chinese study, transition from “daily survival Chinese” to integrated, task-based language application. By the end of the course, students are expected to achieve the following objectives:

Vocabulary and Grammar: Master approximately 600 newly added Level4 core vocabulary items and over 50 sets of key connective expressions and fixed sentence patterns. Students will be able to accurately differentiate the usage contexts of nearsynonyms in both written and spoken Chinese.

Listening Comprehension: Comprehend short dialogues and workplace/campus monologues delivered at a speed of about 120–140 characters per minute. Students will develop the ability to capture crucial information (time, quantity, cause and effect) and infer the speaker’s intention.

Reading Comprehension: Read narrative and expository texts of medium length (400–600 characters) with reasonable speed. Students will achieve a stable accuracy rate of over 80% in readingsection question types, including cloze tests, sequencing, and matching exercises.

Writing: Independently compose a narrative passage of at least 80 characters based on provided prompts or pictures, ensuring complete sentence structures, appropriate use of logical connectors, and a coherent discourse flow.

教学方式

1.Introducing key vocabulary with visuals

2.Grasping the meaning of new words through listening to conversations

3.Constructing sentences using the newly learned vocabulary

4.Reading the text and summarizing it in one's own words

5.Applying new words in practice by writing short passages

6.Expanding learning with interactive games or group discussions to deepen word mastery
