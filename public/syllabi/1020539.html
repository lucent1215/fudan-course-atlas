课程简介（中文）

  学生需要应对当今瞬息万变的世界，尤其是在像中国这样发展、竞争和数字化水平都无与伦比的国家。传统的教学法侧重于培养学生的逻辑性和循序渐进的认知能力，旨在有目的地构建其专业能力或思维方式。而本课程通过模拟真实体验和全流程的体验式创业教育，来补充学生能力和技能的培养。

  本课程将运用设计思维、创意思维、故事讲述以及其他与企业发展相关的创造性理论。企业发展的课程目标，是帮助学生掌握识别和把握商业机会的工具与方法，同时培养解决问题的能力，这些能力在他们生活的多个方面都将发挥重要作用。

课程简介（英文）

Students need to be prepared for a world that has an unprecedented rate of change, especially in countries such as China, with unparalleled levels of development, competition and digitalization. Traditional pedagogy is focused to create logical, sequential learning that is purposefully aimed at building competency or perspectives. Simulating experiences and a fully experiential entrepreneurship allows this course to complement those skills mentioned. This course applies design thinking, creative thinking, story telling and other entrepreneurial development creative theories.The goal of entrepreneurial development is to equip students with tools and methodologies that can help them identify and pursue business opportunities, while developing problem solving skills that will be useful in many aspects of their lives. 

教学目标

  • Entrepreneurial Mindset: Understanding the entrepreneurial mindset and its importance.
  • Innovation and Creativity: Understanding the relevant literature and methodologies that drive innovation, creativity, and disruption.
  • Problem Solving and Opportunity Recognition: Identifying potential problems, formulating solutions, and recognizing how they can be transformed into business opportunities.
  • Resource Management: Understanding that entrepreneurship is possible even with limited resources.
  • Opportunity Identification and Empathy: Identifying opportunities with economic, social, or environmental impact, and empathizing with client and market needs.
  • Creative Problem-Solving Skills: Developing skills for creative processes and approaching/solving problems from different perspectives.
  • Customer Experience Mapping: Learning how to structure and create a customer experience map.
  • Communication and Customer Engagement: Understanding how to effectively communicate ideas and connect with potential customers.
  • Chinese Innovation Environment: Understanding China's innovation environment and the opportunities it presents for entrepreneurs.
  • Chinese Economic Trends and Cross-Border Impact: Identifying growing trends in the Chinese economy and understanding their potential impact on cross-border economic development.
  • Interaction with Chinese Entrepreneurs: Interacting and engaging with entrepreneurs who are conducting business in China.

教学方式

The teaching methods may include, but are not limited to, simulations, experiential learning, workshops, master classes, group projects, guest lectures, presentations, and research projects.

The course is designed for students to develop individual and group skills. The course will contain workshops, presentations, individual and group presentations, reading comprehension evaluation, and invited guests.
