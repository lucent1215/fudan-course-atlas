课程简介（中文）

宏观经济学是经济学的核心学科。扎实掌握宏观经济学知识，是理解经济运行规律、制定经济政策的基础。本入门课程系统讲解国内生产总值、失业、通货膨胀、利率、汇率、经济增长与经济周期等基础理论，并运用相关理论解读古今宏观经济现象，分析货币政策与财政政策带来的影响。

课程简介（英文）

Macroeconomics is a core discipline in economics. A solid grasp of macroeconomics lays the foundation for understanding economic operations and formulating economic policies. This introductory course elaborates on fundamental theories concerning GDP, unemployment, inflation, interest rates, exchange rates, economic growth and business cycles. It applies these theories to interpret past and contemporary macroeconomic phenomena, as well as to analyze the impacts of monetary and fiscal policies.

教学目标

本课程旨在向学生讲授两大核心内容：一是经济如何运行（实证经济学视角），二是我们能否以及如何优化经济运行态势（规范经济学视角）。顺利修完本课程后，学生将掌握宏观经济学核心理论，养成宏观经济问题批判性思维，并具备研判宏观经济政策实施效果的能力。

The main goal of this course is to introduce students to two core themes: how the economy operates (positive economics perspective), and whether and how we can improve economic performance (normative economics perspective). Upon successful completion of this course, students will master core macroeconomic theories, develop critical thinking on macroeconomic issues, and be capable of evaluating the impacts of macroeconomic policies.

Factor market

Product market

Consumption demand, investment demand

Money market

Monetary system (functions of money, money multiplying process, banking regulations, monetary policy); money demand

4

Long-run model

Classical assumption and classical dichotomy: (1) Real side: permanent shocks affect saving rate, which in turn affects the growth path; (2) Nominal side: money supply determines price level

5

Very-long-run model

(1) Real side: growth of GDP; (2) Nominal side: money growth and inflation

6

AD−AS framework: from short run to long run

Understand the self-correcting price adjustment assumption; able to do three-step shock analysis

7

Case study: 2008 Subprime crisis

Understand asset price bubbles and how they may lead to economic crises through financial accelerator

Part III: Extension to open economy

 

 

1

Short-run and long-run models for open economy

Two-step analysis: before and after cross-border asset arbitrage begins

2

Case study: 1997 Asian economic crisis

Understand the importance of stability of banking system for open economies

3

Long-run model for open economy

Decoupling between real exchange rate and nominal exchange rate due to flexible prices
