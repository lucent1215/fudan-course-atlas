课程简介（中文）

这门课程主要涵盖有限域的基础知识及其在编码和密码学中的应用。有限域是编码和密码学中重要的数学工具，可用于构建各种编码方案和加密算法，实现信息的安全传输和存储。编码和密码学是网络空间网络安全的核心技术和基础支撑。
这门课程将主要分为两部分：第一部分包括群、环、域、有限域的扩张、有限域的结构、有限域上的多项式等内容的讲解。第二部分则专注于有限域的应用，包括其在编码和密码学中的应用。

课程简介（英文）

This course mainly covers the foundational knowledge of finite fields and their applications in coding and cryptography. Finite fields are important mathematical tools in coding and cryptography, which can be used to construct various encoding schemes and encryption algorithms, achieving secure transmission and storage of information. Coding and cryptography are core technologies and foundational support for cybersecurity in cyberspace.

This course will be mainly divided into two parts: The first part includes lectures on groups, rings, fields, extensions of finite fields, structures of finite fields, polynomials over finite fields. The second part focuses on the applications of finite fields, including their applications in coding and cryptography.

教学目标

Through this course, students will master the basic concepts and properties of finite fields, including groups, rings, fields, extensions of finite fields, structures of finite fields, polynomials over finite fields. Additionally, they will gain insight into the applications of finite fields in coding and cryptography. By completing this course, students in the field of cybersecurity will establish a solid foundation for engaging in related research.
