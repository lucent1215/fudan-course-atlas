课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在帮助学生熟悉宏观经济学的核心概念，尤其侧重近年宏观经济政策与金融市场的动态。课程将引导学生探索宏观经济学与（国际）金融学交叉领域的现代经济建模技术，重点培养并传授可用于分析各类宏观经济问题的工具及求解方法。

课程内容涵盖以下主题：
从生产与价格分析出发，结合消费与储蓄的理论框架，融入现代增长理论、劳动力与资本市场分析，并借助现代金融体系探讨货币与财政政策的前沿议题与实践。课程视角将从封闭经济体延伸至开放经济体，为学生提供宏观经济学的全球视野。

课程简介（英文）

This course familiarizes students with key concepts in macroeconomics. Particular attention is given to the recent macroeconomic policy and financial market. This course will expose students to modern economic modeling techniques at the intersection between macroeconomics and (international) finance. The aim of this course is to develop and teach tools and includes solution procedures that students can apply to a variety of macroeconomic issues.

This course will cover the following topics:

Starting from production, price, this course will combine the analysis framework of consumption and savings. It will also incorporate modern growth theory, labor and capital markets, and the modern financial system to analyze and discuss cutting-edge issues and practices in monetary and fiscal policy. From closed economies to open economies, this course will provide a global perspective on macroeconomics.

教学目标

Through theoretical research and empirical study of the frontier of macroeconomics, this course will improve students' ability to master and apply the basic framework of macroeconomics and help them analyze international macroeconomic issues and financial markets in the era of globalization. This course will combine the academic research frontier, global financial market, and policy debates. The course also covers a series of advanced topics in macroeconomics and finance.
