课程简介（中文）

《全球卫生治理导论》是一门专门为中国和国际学生设计的跨学科课程。本课程既提供全球视角，又将卫生问题置于中国独特的国情背景下进行探讨。课程系统地介绍了全球卫生各关键领域（包括卫生治理、卫生融资、传染病防控以及妇幼保健）中的核心概念、政策实践及前沿动态。

本课程将包含三个主要模块。
模块1：全球卫生领域的最新发展与挑战；
模块2：全球卫生治理的机制与发展趋势
模块3：中国对全球卫生治理的贡献。

 

课程简介（英文）

《Global Health Governance and China’s Engagement》 is an interdisciplinary course specifically designed for international students. It offers a global perspective while contextualizing health issues within China's unique national circumstances. The course systematically introduces essential concepts, policy practices, and cutting-edge developments across key global health domains, including health governance, health financing, infectious disease prevention and control, and maternal and child health.

The aim of this course is to provide international students with insights into global health as it relates to China's national context. Students will learn to analyze practical pathways for global health cooperation, recognize China's evolving role in global health governance, and gain an intuitive understanding of the institutional and cultural logic underlying the "Chinese model." Ultimately, the course will enhance their critical thinking and practical skills in tackling global health challenges within a multicultural framework.

This course will comprise three major modules. 
Module 1: Current Developments and Challenges in Global Health;
Module 2: Global Health Governance: pathway, actors, and so on; 
Module 3: China's Contributions to Global Health Engagement.

Through comparative analysis, this course will help students understand China’s strategic choices and achievements as a representative of the Global South in building health systems and addressing health challenges. Additionally, it highlights China’s significant contributions to global health in recent years.

 

教学目标

本课程将通过实证数据和案例介绍，帮助学生理解和掌握全球卫生、全球卫生治理等的基本概念，熟悉地缘政治等国际对冲对全球卫生的深刻影响。通过比较分析，帮助学生理解中国作为“全球南方”代表在构建卫生体系及应对卫生挑战方面的战略选择与成就。同时，本课程也旨在让学生了解中国近年来对全球卫生事业做出的重要贡献。
