课程简介（中文）

本课程为复旦大学国际暑期课程，旨在为本科生了解计算理论的基础知识。

课程内容包括有限自动机、正则语言、图灵机及其变体、可计算性和复杂性理论等，重点讲解图灵机和NP完全性，帮助学生理解计算的定义、可能性及局限性。

通过课堂讲解，学生将深入了解计算模型及其应用。本课程不要求学生具有深厚的计算机背景，但需要具备一定的数学基础和编程经验。

课程简介（英文）

This course, part of the FudanISS (Fudan University International Summer Session), offers a beginner-friendly introduction to the Theory of Computation for undergraduate students with a science or engineering background.

Topics include finite automata, regular languages, Turing machines and their variants, computability, and complexity theory. The course aims to help students understand 1) the definition of computation, 2) which problems are computable, and 3) which problems can be computed efficiently.

Through lectures and hands-on exercises, students will gain a rigorous understanding of computation, its possibilities, and limitations. A basic knowledge of mathematics and programming experience is required, though no advanced computer science background is necessary.

教学目标

Computation is everywhere. Oftentime, people overlook the theory behind it.

This course will give you a beginner-friendly introduction to the Theory of Computation. The Theory of Computation seeks to categorize computational problems based on their inherent difficulty, measured by the resources (primarily time and space) needed to solve them. It also aims to explore the relationships between different problems, such as determining whether problem X is not harder than problem Y. This course will help you gain a rigorous understanding of computation, including its definition, possilbilities, and limitations.

Topics include finite automaton and regular language, Turing machine and its variants, computability, and complexity theory.

教学方式

The instructor will give in-class lectures, give self-contained proofs for the key results, and handwrite all the board notes.

The instructor will ask questions, provide in-class exercise, to ensure students have understood the materials.

Students will gain hands-on experience by programming with a Turing machine simulator.

Throughout the course, students are encouraged to ask questions. They may do so via email or the course WeChat group, and the instructor or teaching assistant will respond.

教学内容与进度安排

Lecture 1. Big O notation, alphabet and languages
Lecture 2. Finite automaton
Lecture 3. Regular language
Lecture 4. Turing machine and its variants
Lecture 5. Universal Turing machine, computability
Lecture 6. Turing halting problem, undecidable problems
Lecture 7. Reductions, Church–Turing thesis
Lecture 8. Complexity class P, NP
Lecture 9. Cook-Levin theorem
Lecture 10. Turing reduction and Cook reduction
Lecture 11. More NP-complete problems
Lecture 12. Final exam

There will be a total of five assignments and one exam. The final exam will be a closed-book exam conducted in class.
