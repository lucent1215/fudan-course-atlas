课程简介（中文）

这是一门微观经济学课程。课程重点讲解微观经济学的分析工具，并将这些工具应用于分析微观经济问题。习题集旨在帮助学生练习运用这些工具。学生的成绩将基于他们对这些工具的理解以及将这些工具应用于解决新问题的能力进行评估。学生必须做到以下几点才能获得满意的课程成绩：(1) 课前做好准备，积极参与课堂讲座和讨论；(2) 阅读指定的阅读材料；(3) 完成习题；(4) 及时复习课堂内容。

课程简介（英文）

This is a course of microeconomics. Lectures focus on the presentation of analytical tools of microeconomics and the application of these tools to analyze microeconomic issues. Problem sets provide students with practice applying these tools. Students will be evaluated on their understanding of these tools and their ability to apply them to new problems. Students are required to (1) attend lectures and in-class discussions with preparation before each lecture, (2) read assigned readings, (3) do problem sets, and (4) do timely reviews of class materials. All the above are needed to obtain a satisfactory grade for this course.

教学目标

Students can establish a solid understanding of the basics of microeconomics, including: (1) rigorous analytical tools, (2) theoretical reasoning to analyze microeconomics questions, and (3) a preliminary outlook of research on microeconomics. This course is also career-oriented. After taking the course, students are equipped with microeconomic knowledge and skills for advanced academic degrees, non-academic careers, and life in general. For example, the course will equip students with knowledge and skills for better answering interview questions in consulting/data science/tech firms, and students have chances to practice during lectures.

教学方式

The instruction is mainly conducted by lectures and in-class discussion. Lectures focus on the presentation of basic theories and analytical tools. Problem sets provide students with practice applying these tools. Students will be evaluated through exams on their understanding of these tools and their ability to apply them to new problems. In lectures, students have a chance to apply knowledge and analytical tools to answer practical questions, as in job interviews.
