课程简介（中文）

课程具体教学内容涉及汉字、词汇、课文、语法及文化等各个方面，话题涉及日常生活交际的基本层面，以学校生活为主要场景。内容包括学习生活、时尚、食物、节日习俗、退休、买车、待客礼仪、志愿者等方面，学生将学习认读、使用及书写420个汉语词汇，重要语言点21个。

课程简介（英文）

The specific teaching content of the course covers various areas such as Chinese characters, vocabulary, texts, grammar, and culture. Topics focus on fundamental aspects of daily communication, with school life as the primary setting. Content includes academic life, fashion, food, festival customs, retirement, car purchasing, hospitality etiquette, volunteering, and more. Students will learn to recognize, use, and write 420 Chinese vocabulary items and 21 key language points.

教学目标

此课程针对汉语非母语的医学专业一年级本科生开设，本学期教学旨在帮助学生掌握汉语常用词汇和基本语法，通过听、说、读、写的全面训练使学生具有基本的听说读写能力、言语交际能力, 能满足日常在中国生活、学习和一般社交场合的交际需要。本学期课程结束后学生汉语水平达到HSK三级或以上。

教学方式

  1. 词汇教学：PowerPoint辅助讲解与练习，近义词辨析，写故事，“你来比划我来猜”等游戏。
  2. 句子教学：学生看图说句子，改错句等。
  3. 语法教学：创设生活、学习等现实情景，帮助学生理解语法知识，然后运用该语法知识完成实际的语言任务。
  4. 对话教学：角色扮演。
  5. 课文教学：朗读课文，复述课文，表演课文，讨论与发表观点等。
  6. 阅读教学：阅读拓展课文，回答问题，引导学生围绕一些话题发表自己的观点。
  7. 写作教学：课堂上规定时间写一段话，课后每个作业中都有一篇150-200字的写作练习。
  1. Vocabulary teaching: PowerPoint-assisted explanation and practice, analysis of near-synonyms, story writing, games like "You Act and I Guess", etc.
  2. Sentence teaching: Students describe sentences based on pictures, correct wrong sentences, etc.
  3. Grammar teaching: Create real-life scenarios (such as daily life and study) to help students understand grammatical knowledge, and then use this knowledge to complete practical language tasks.
  4. Dialogue teaching: Role-playing.
  5. Text teaching: Reading texts aloud, retelling texts, performing texts, discussing and expressing opinions, etc.
  6. Reading teaching: Reading extended texts, answering questions, and guiding students to express their opinions on relevant topics.
  7. Writing teaching: Writing a paragraph within a specified time in class, and each after-class assignment includes a 150-200-word writing exercise.
