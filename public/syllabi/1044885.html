课程简介（中文）

环境对人类健康具有重要意义。室内外空气污染与不同人群的各类健康问题密切相关。气候与室外空气污染之间存在密切的相互作用，并独立地影响人类健康。目前，全球气候变化已被观测到，热浪和寒潮的发生频率据报也在不断增加。在本课程中，我们将讨论以下问题：室内外环境中存在哪些类型的空气污染？这些污染由哪些化合物构成？它们能否被测量？我们是否了解不同污染物的浓度水平？室外环境与室内环境之间有何关系？当室外空气污染严重时，待在室内是否更安全？我们是否有措施来预防或降低污染水平？这些污染物对健康有何影响？它们会影响我们的呼吸系统、心血管系统，甚至内分泌系统吗？当前气候变化及最新信息如何？气候变化与空气污染的相互作用程度如何？它如何影响我们的健康，以及国际或国家层面有哪些应对策略？

通过与学生积极互动，全英文教学，部分采用面授，部分采用线上自学（本课程配有录制视频），学生将获益良多：获得更多关于空气污染、气候变化与人类健康的科学知识，积累理解环境健康影响的经验，提高英语交流能力，并为未来生活中的国际学习做好更充分的准备。

课程简介（英文）

Environment is of great significance to human health. Air pollution, both indoor and outdoor, is closely related with different health problems in different populations. Climate closely interacts with ambient air pollution and affects human health independently. Currently, global climate change has been observed and heat wave and cold wave has been reported to happen more frequently. In this course, the following questions will be talked and discussed: What types of air pollution are there in indoor and outdoor environment? What types of compounds form the pollution? Can they be measured? Do we know the levels of different pollutants? How about the relationship between outdoor and indoor environment? Is it safer to stay in indoor environment if there is a high outdoor air pollution? Do we have any measures to prevent or reduce the pollution level? What are the health effects of these pollutants? Do they affect our respiratory health, cardiovascular system or even our endocrine systems? How about the current climate change and updated information? How much does the climate change interact with air pollution and affect our health and international or national strategies on it?

By active interactions with students, partly in person and partly online for self-learning (with video recorded for this course), students will benefit by obtaining more scientific knowledge in air pollution, climate change and human health, getting more experienced in understanding the health effects of environment, becoming more skilled in communication in English and prepared better for international study in the future life.

教学目标

This course aims to

1) introduce the basic scientific knowledge about indoor and outdoor air pollution, as well as global and regional climate change, in relation to human health to undergraduates in Fudan University;

2) to make the students prepared to be enrolled in interested scientific research project in this field;

3) to broaden students’ vision on the environment and health in international perspectives;

4) to enhance students’ abilities in communication, scientific thinking and wring in English.

教学方式

Teaching Methods:

 Combination of in person and online. Oral presentation in person combined with students’ self-learning online. AI -based learning and discussion in person.
