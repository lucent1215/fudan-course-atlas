课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在提升学生的医学英语学术写作能力，重点培养学生医学研究论文的撰写技巧。课程首先通过夯实基础——包括医学学术论文的语言风格、时态运用和语态选择等核心要素——来系统提升学生的写作素养。在完成基础训练后，本课程将聚焦医学英语论文写作的核心模块，采用"讲练结合"的教学模式。课程既系统讲授引言、方法、结果、讨论以及摘要和参考文献等论文各章节的写作规范，更通过实践训练使学生将写作原则与技巧转化为应用能力，实现从机械记忆到内化掌握的能力跃迁。

 

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to improve the students’ writing abilities with the focus on how to write a biomedical research paper. For this purpose, the course will first refine the students’ writing by going through the fundamentals such as language styles, tense, and voice in journal articles. After the foundation building, the course will come to its core of biomedical research paper writing. Lectures on how to write each section of a typical biomedical paper, namely the Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion as well as the Abstract and References, will be integrated with student practices so that the principles and skills will be absorbed and internalized through using instead of memorizing.

 

教学目标

After learning this course, students are expected to be able to

  • Identify the fundamentals in English writing;
  • Know the principles to abide by while writing a medical paper in English;
  • Follow the format requirements of medical papers in English;
  • Analyze the components of a typical medical journal paper and their characteristics;
  • Critique biomedical research paper;
  • Use academic English to communicate in conference.

教学方式

Lectures, Seminars, Presentations, Case Studies
