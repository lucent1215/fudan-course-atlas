课程简介（中文）

本课程为一门阅读课程。阅读材料为西方学者对中国儒家思想不同发展阶段的解释与批评类论文及著作节选。 每篇文章长度为三千至五千单词，内容涵盖原始儒家思想、汉代董仲舒对儒家思想的发展、魏晋南北朝时期玄学与儒学的互动、宋明理学的兴起与发展、清代考据学的哲学批判，以及当代新儒家的兴起。阅读材料环环相扣，系统介绍了儒家思想两千年的发展。

课程简介（英文）

This course focuses on the reading of articles pertaining to the deveopment of Confucianism in its various stages. These articles, written by renowned schloars in the research of Confucianism, cover the development of Original Confucianism, Dong Zhongshu' contribution to Confucianism in the Han Dynasty, the interaction between Confuciansim and the Mysterious Learning, the rise of Neo-Confucianism, critical analysis of Evidential Learning in the Qing Dynasty and the rise of Contemporary New Confucianism.  The readings are selected in a logical and accessible way to offer a coherent introduction to the manifold dimensions of the Confucian persuasion from its beginnings to the present. 

教学目标

The mission of this course is to help Chinese students acquaint themselves with western studies of Confucianism, improve their skills in using English to translate Chinese classics or express the main ideas of Confucianism, and learn how to write professional academic articles. International students will gain further understanding of the predominant Chinese thought and culture upon completing this course.

教学方式

Each class will have two sections:  

  1. Lecture: The lecture will cover the main concepts of each unit, and highlight the academic features of the articles under discussion, including the use of formal English, the strength of the author’s argument and the signpost language.
  2. Discussion of the articles: students will be divided into groups to share their ideas about the questions for discussion in each unit.
