课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在根据《中国英语能力等级量表》4-6级的要求，培养学生的听力能力与听力策略。课程将融入丰富多样的练习，通过教材、媒体及语言学习网站中的真实视听材料，针对性训练学生语音理解、细节捕捉、篇章结构分析等核心技能。

课程简介（英文）

This course aims to cultivate students’ listening abilities and strategies as required by the Levels 4-6 of China’s Standards of English Language Ability. The course will incorporate a rich variety of exercises designed to address key skills in understanding pronunciation, specific details, and overall structure by using authentic audiovisual materials from the textbook and media and language learning websites. Students are expected to learn the phonetic characteristics of fluent speech and listening strategies to identify, analyze and summarize information.

教学目标

Upon completion of this course, students are expected to understand authentic English conversations in social and campus scenarios, as well as English lectures, speeches, discussions, news reports, etc. They should grasp the central ideas, understand key points, and capture important details.

students should achieve the following learning objectives:

  1. Comprehend listening materials at a speech rate of 100-140 words per minute（wpm）, which specifically includes:

      1) Oral instructions and authentic conversations, such as announcements in public places (e.g. train stations and sports arenas), multi-step instructions related to study, work, and daily life, as well as everyday conversations, academic dialogues, and business negotiations. Students should identify the viewpoints and attitudes of the communicators, and understand the cultural connotations within the discourse.

     2) Oral narration. Students are expected to grasp the general idea of news or television programs presented at 100-140 wpm; and understand complex stories and their underlying morals or philosophies.

     3) Oral argumentation. Students should be able to understand lectures and conference speeches on general topics at 100-140 wpm, capturing key points and specific details.

  1. Apply various listening strategies: such as guessing unfamiliar words based on context; predicting the information in listening materials; adjusting or confirming predictions during listening; and taking notes using codes and abbreviations to record key points and details of listening materials.

教学方式

The course is lecture-based, with the teacher providing in-class instruction with multi-media assistance and guiding activities in a task-based approach, supplemented by out-of-class exercises. Students are expected to actively engage in classroom activities, complete both in-class and after-class listening assignments，and fully utilize supplementary materials.
