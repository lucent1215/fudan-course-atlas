课程简介（中文）

软件过程管理主要以敏捷开发过程和开发运维一体化为软件研发背景，重点介绍当代软件开发过程规范、软件过程管理方法以及关键技术和支撑平台。课程内容在理论讲解的基础上，以当前业界主流软件过程管理实践为抓手，重点研讨工业界软件过程管理的系统平台、最佳实践，涵盖软件过程管理中需求管理、质量管理等核心内容，对软件过程管理的关键环节和实践问题进行分析，使得学生在理解软件过程管理的必要性和基本方法的基础上，实际动手实践开发运维一体化的软件过程管理，为工程化的软件开发和管理奠定基础。在课程中，传授软件工程师道德规范，提升工程能力和责任感，了解国产软件开发平台、持续集成和自动化部署环境的成就。

课程简介（英文）

Software Process Management​ is set against the backdrop of agile development processes and DevSecOps, focusing on contemporary software development process standards, management methods, key technologies, and supporting platforms. The course builds on theoretical explanations and leverages current industry mainstream software process management practices to delve into the systematic platforms and best practices of industrial software process management. It covers core aspects such as requirements management and quality management in software process management, analyzing key links and practical issues. This enables students, based on understanding the necessity and fundamental methods of software process management, to gain hands-on experience in DevSecOps-based software process management, laying a foundation for engineered software development and management. Throughout the course, software engineering ethics are taught to enhance engineering capabilities and sense of responsibility, while also introducing achievements in domestic software development platforms, continuous integration, and automated deployment environments

教学目标

本课程的教学目标是通过系统化的软件过程管理理论学习和项目实践，使学生掌握软件过程的规范和管理方法，熟悉业界常用的软件过程管理系统与工具，熟悉敏捷开发和开发运维一体化的关键环节和技术，具备初步的软件过程管理实践能力。
