课程简介（中文）

课程旨在培养理工科学生在职场和创新创业场景中的英语沟通能力，帮助学生理解跨文化沟通总体原则，掌握求职面试、团队协作、技术汇报、会议协商、产品企划、项目路演等核心场景的表达策略。授课结合模拟实践及AI工具辅助等，帮助学生熟悉专业表达技巧，提升语言流利度、逻辑表达能力、人际沟通能力和应变能力，为其未来职业发展和参与国际科技竞争奠定坚实基础。

课程简介（英文）

This course is designed to develop the English communication skills of Science and Technology students for workplace and entrepreneurial contexts. It aims to help students understand key principles of intercultural communication and master practical expression techniques for core scenarios such as job interviews, team collaboration, technical presentations, meeting negotiations, product planning, and project pitching. Through simulated practice and the integration of AI-assisted tools, the course enables students to become familiar with professional communication strategies, enhancing their language fluency, logical expression, interpersonal communication, and adaptability. It lays a solid foundation for students’ future career development and active participation in global scientific and technological competition.

 

教学目标

结合复旦大学拔尖创新人才培养目标，本课程致力于培养理工科学生在未来职场和创新创业场景中的英语沟通能力，启迪其在科技发展背景下对人际沟通价值的思考和实践。通过授课、模拟训练和AI辅助等手段，帮助学生理解跨文化沟通总体原则，掌握科技相关工作和创业核心场景的人际沟通技巧；提升语言流利度、逻辑表达能力和应变能力，为其未来职业发展和加入国际科技竞争奠定坚实基础。/Aligned with Fudan University's goal of cultivating top-tier innovative talent, this course is designed to develop the English communication skills of science and tech students for future professional and entrepreneurial settings. It aims to inspire students to reflect on and practice interpersonal communication in the context of technological advancement. Through lectures, simulation training and AI-assisted activities, the course helps students understand the principles of intercultural communication and master interpersonal communication techniques in key scenarios related to technology and entrepreneurship. It also enhances their language fluency, logical expression, and adaptability, laying a solid foundation for their future career development and participation in global scientific and technological competition.

教学方式

  1. 任务驱动教学：通过模拟真实场景的任务（如模拟面试、会议主持、项目路演），让学生在实践中学习。Task-Based Learning: Students learn through hands-on practice by completing simulated real-world tasks such as mock interviews, meeting facilitation, and project pitching.
  2. 小组合作与角色扮演：学生分组完成项目任务，并通过角色扮演练习沟通技巧。Group Collaboration and Role-Playing: Students work in teams to complete project tasks and practice communication skills through role-playing exercises.
  3. AI工具辅助：利用AI工具进行对话训练，提供即时反馈。AI-Assisted Learning: AI tools are used for conversation training, providing instant feedback.
  4. 翻转课堂：课前提供学习材料（如视频、文章），课堂上进行互动和实践。Flipped Classroom: Learning materials such as videos and articles are provided before class, with in-class time focused on interaction and practical application.
  5. 创新创业案例教学：结合真实科技创业案例，分析沟通策略和技巧。Case-Based Learning in Innovation and Entrepreneurship: Real-world technology entrepreneurship cases are used to analyze communication strategies and techniques.

教学内容与进度安排

第1周：基础交流技巧与跨文化沟通原则

教学目标：

  - 掌握基础英语交流技巧（如自我介绍、礼貌对话等）

  - 了解跨文化沟通的基本原则和常见问题

  - 通过讨论发言的形式思考人机时代的人际沟通价值、科技领域的文化差异

 

第2周：求职面试

教学目标： 

  -掌握科技领域求职面试中的常见问题和回答技巧

  -学习如何展示技术能力和职业规划

  -模拟练习技术岗位面试

 

第3周：新人报到

教学目标：

- 理解科技公司内部的常见组织架构 

- 学习如何会见上司和同事

- 掌握如何询问和讨论工作职责

 

第4周：融入环境

教学目标：

- 了解办公室内的礼貌问候

- 学习基本的电话和邮件礼仪

 

第5周：团队协作

教学目标： 

  - 学习科技团队协作中的沟通技巧（如工作分配、责任归属） 

  - 掌握如何在团队中清晰表达技术观点

  - 熟悉如何与同事协商不同意见

 

第6周：综合任务（1）

教学目标：

  - 以小组为单位，现场完成综合以上学习内容的情景对话或书面沟通任务

 

第7周：客户往来

教学目标：

  - 理解与科技行业客户沟通的基本原则

  - 掌握与客户会晤、达成协议等的现场沟通方法

 

第8周：会议技巧

教学目标： 

  - 学习如何主持和参与科技领域的线上线下会议

  - 掌握会议中的议程管理技巧和相应语言表达

  - 学习会议记录制作

 

第9周：工作汇报

教学目标： 

  - 学习如何准备和呈现技术工作汇报

  - 实践工作汇报的PPT制作

 

第10周：福利权益

教学目标：

  - 学习如何理解公司福利

  - 掌握交涉薪资、升迁等的原则和技巧

 

第11周：综合任务（2）

教学目标：

  - 以小组为单位，现场完成综合以上学习内容的情景对话或书面沟通任务

 

第12周：产品推介

教学目标： 

  - 思考如何推广科技产品或服务

  - 实践设计一个科技产品推广海报并围绕它展开演说

 

第13周：商业愿景

教学目标：

  - 学习解说科技创新创业项目（如技术可行性和市场潜力等）

  - 掌握投资者沟通策略

 

 第14周：综合任务（3）：宣传路演

教学目标：

- 综合运用前两个单元中的核心策略，完成一次完整的路演展示。

- 在限时展示中进一步练习信息整合、团队协作、视觉材料配合及现场问答，提升真实情境下的说服性表达与即时应对能力。

 

 第15周：复习课

教学目标：

  - 复习本学期所学内容

 

第16周：答疑

 

第17周：期末考试

 

Week 1: Basic Communication Skills and Principles of Intercultural Communication

Learning Objectives:

  • Master basic English communication skills (e.g., self-introduction, polite conversation)
  • Understand key principles and common challenges of intercultural communication
  • Reflect on the value of interpersonal communication in the age of AI and cultural differences in the tech world through class discussions

Week 2: Job Interview

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn common questions and effective responses in tech-related job interviews
  • Understand how to present technical skills and career plans
  • Practice mock interviews for technical positions

Week 3: First Day on the Job

Learning Objectives:

  • Understand the typical organizational structure of tech companies
  • Learn how to meet and greet supervisors and colleagues
  • Master how to inquire about and discuss job responsibilities

Week 4: Workplace Integration

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn polite greetings in the office environment
  • Practice basic phone and email etiquette

Week 5: Team Collaboration

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn communication strategies for teamwork in tech settings (e.g., task delegation, clarifying responsibilities)
  • Express technical ideas clearly in a team context
  • Practice negotiating differing opinions with colleagues

Week 6: Integrated Task I

Learning Objectives:

  • In groups, complete a live scenario-based dialogue or written communication task that integrates the content learned so far

Week 7: Client Communication

Learning Objectives:

  • Understand basic principles of communicating with clients in the tech industry
  • Learn how to conduct on-site communication such as meetings and deal-making

Week 8: Meeting Skills

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn how to lead and participate in both online and offline meetings in the tech field
  • Master agenda management and relevant expressions used in meetings
  • Learn how to take effective meeting minutes

Week 9: Work Presentations

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn how to prepare and deliver technical work reports
  • Practice designing presentation slides (PPTs)

Week 10: Benefits and Employee Rights

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn how to understand and discuss employee benefits
  • Master the principles and skills for negotiating salary and promotion

Week 11: Integrated Task II

Learning Objectives:

  • In groups, complete a live scenario-based dialogue or written communication task that integrates the content learned so far

Week 12: Product Pitching

Learning Objectives:

  • Explore strategies for promoting tech products or services
  • Practice designing a product promotion poster and delivering a pitch based on it

Week 13: Business Vision

Learning Objectives:

  • Learn how to present tech innovation or entrepreneurship projects (e.g., technical feasibility, market potential)
  • Master strategies for communicating with investors

Week 14: Integrated Task III: Demo-day pitch

Learning Objectives:

  • Integrate core strategies in the previous units through a complete Demo-day pitch
  • Coordinate with teammates and visual aids
  • Respond effectively to audience questions in real time.

Week 15: Course Review

Learning Objectives:

  •    Review course content of the semester

Week 16: Q&A and Review

Week 17: Final Exam
