课程简介（中文）

本课程旨在全面审视中国在全球治理中的角色，特别关注其在不同地区的参与和影响。课程内容将深入探讨中国与世界主要地区和国家，如中亚、欧洲、日本、拉丁美洲、中东、俄罗斯、南亚以及东南亚的互动。学生将理解中国的重要外交政策、战略性倡议以及其区域合作伙伴关系，以及中国的地区和全球参与是如何塑造国际政经格局的。

课程简介（英文）

This course provides an overview of China’s role in global governance, with a regional focus. It explores how China engages with major parts of the world, including Central Asia, Europe, Japan, Latin America, the Middle East, Russia, South Asia, and Southeast Asia. Students will understand and analyse China's key foreign policies, strategic initiatives, and regional partnerships. By examining case studies and engaging in discussions, students will develop a nuanced understanding of how China’s global and regional activities shape international politics, regional economies, and diplomatic relations.

教学目标

I.Value Objectives
• China Perspective: Foster an understanding of global engagement and global governance from the Chinese perspective, promoting cross-cultural empathy and appreciation.
• Global Awareness: Cultivate an awareness among students of the significant challenges facing the world today, fostering an understanding of the complexities and intricacies inherent in addressing global issues.
•Cultural Sensitivity: Encourage students to develop cultural sensitivity and cross-cultural competence, recognizing the importance of understanding and respecting diverse cultural norms and practices in the context of global governance.

II.Knowledge Objectives
• Historical Context: Provide historical context for China's global engagement, enabling students to understand its evolution and contemporary significance.
• Regional Understanding: Equip students with comprehensive knowledge of the political, economic, and cultural dynamics of key regions.
• Theoretical Background: Cultivate in students a thorough understanding of pertinent theories within the realms of international relations and developmental studies, empowering them to approach case studies from diverse theoretical perspectives.
• Comparative Analysis: Enable students to conduct nuanced comparative analyses of China's global engagement approach compared to other major powers.

III.Ability Objectives
• Critical Analysis: Develop students' ability to critically and comprehensively analyze foreign relations, economic collaborations, and geopolitical challenges.
• Effective Communication: Enhance students' communication skills by fostering the ability to articulate well-reasoned arguments and engage in constructive debates on complex international issues.

教学方式

This course primarily adopts a lecture-based format to provide systematic knowledge and theoretical frameworks. Lectures will be complemented by class discussions, which encourage students to engage critically with key issues, share diverse perspectives, and apply concepts to real-world cases.
