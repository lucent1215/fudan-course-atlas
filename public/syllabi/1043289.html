课程简介（中文）

本系从1958年开始从事针灸和气功的机理研究，积累了大量针灸治疗疾病的临床和实验经验，近年来在《Nature》、《Neuron》和《Nature Communication》等发表了针刺镇痛、抗抑郁、女性生殖疾病、多发性硬化、围手术期和肿瘤放化疗的研究的高分论文。在医学院开设《中西医结合基础概论》本科生和研究生课程的基础上，开设英文的针灸研究课程，旨在深入探讨针灸这一传统中医实践的丰富历史、理论基础和实际应用，它对全球健康和福祉产生了深远影响，促进文化交流和理解。

通过学习针灸技术、经络系统和穴位定位的，介绍针灸在神经生物学和生理效应方面的最新研究，丰富我们学生的学术体验，并为全球医疗和健康对话做出贡献。

课程简介（英文）

This course has a total of 36 class hours, mainly taught in class, including classroom discussions and questioning. Using course reports as the assessment method.

Our department has been engaged in the mechanism research of acupuncture and moxibustion and Qigong since 1958, and has accumulated a lot of clinical and experimental experience in the treatment of diseases with acupuncture and moxibustion. In recent years, the Department has published high scoring papers on acupuncture analgesia, antidepressant, female reproductive diseases, multiple sclerosis, perioperative period, and tumor chemoradiotherapy in Nature, Neuron, Nature Communication, etc. On the basis of the undergraduate and graduate courses of Basic Introduction to Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine offered by our department, the English course is designed to deeply explore the long history, theoretical basis and practical application of acupuncture and moxibustion, a traditional Chinese medicine practice, which has had a profound impact on global health and well-being and promoted cultural exchange and understanding.

Through learning acupuncture and moxibustion technology, meridian system and acupoint positioning, introduce the latest research of acupuncture and moxibustion in neurobiology and physiological effects, enrich students' academic experience, and make contributions to the global medical and health dialogue.

教学目标

本课程作为复旦国际化课程FISS的医学专业课，教学目标如下：

  1. Understand the origin, development, clinical application and dominant diseases of acupuncture and moxibustion;
  2. Be familiar with the experimental progress and scientific research of acupuncture and moxibustion in the treatment of pain, mental disorders, reproductive metabolic diseases, degenerative diseases, bone and joint diseases, etc.
