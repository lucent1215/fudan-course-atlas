课程简介（中文）

通过学习，掌握疼痛诊疗学的概念、范畴，了解疼痛诊疗工作的重要意义和疼痛诊疗学的发展简史和目前状况。

通过理解疼痛的机制、诊断及治疗原理，掌握疼痛的治疗方法及测量与评估的方法。

理解疼痛性疾病的病史采集、体格检查、影像学诊断和有关实验室诊断。

理解治疗疼痛的常用药物种类和临床药理特性。

学习神经阻滞疗法的作用机制、适应症、禁忌症和并发症，熟悉常用神经阻滞的特点和操作要点。

通过对颌面部常见疼痛性疾病的学习，掌握三叉神经痛、熟悉面神经麻痹和面肌痉挛临床表现、诊断、鉴别诊断及治疗。

在理解颈、肩及上肢疼痛疾病的病因与病理的基础上，学习颈椎病、肩周炎、肱骨外上髁炎、肩肌筋膜炎、腱鞘炎的临床表现、诊断、鉴别诊断和治疗方法。

在理解腰及下肢痛的病因、病理的基础上，掌握腰及下肢痛的临床表现、诊断与治疗。

在理解腰及下肢痛的病因、病理的基础上，掌握腰及下肢痛的临床表现、诊断与治疗。

在理解带状疱疹的发生机制的基础上，学习带状疱疹的分期、临床特点、治疗与预防。

了解术后镇痛对机体的影响及镇痛的重要性，熟悉术后镇痛的方法。

课程简介（英文）

Through study, master the concepts and scope of pain medicine, understand the importance of pain management work, and gain an overview of the historical development and current status of pain medicine.

By understanding the mechanisms, diagnosis, and principles of treatment for pain, master methods for pain therapy as well as techniques for its measurement and evaluation.

Understand the procedures for medical history collection, physical examination, imaging diagnosis, and relevant laboratory tests for painful diseases.

Grasp the commonly used types of analgesic drugs and their clinical pharmacological properties.

Study the mechanisms of action, indications, contraindications, and complications of nerve block therapy, and become familiar with the characteristics and key techniques of commonly used nerve block procedures.

Through the study of common painful diseases of the orofacial region, master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and treatment of trigeminal neuralgia, and become familiar with the clinical features, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and treatment of facial paralysis and hemifacial spasm.

Based on understanding the etiology and pathology of neck, shoulder, and upper limb pain, learn the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, differential diagnosis, and treatment of cervical spondylosis, periarthritis of the shoulder (frozen shoulder), lateral epicondylitis of the humerus (tennis elbow), shoulder myofascial pain syndrome, and tenosynovitis.

Based on understanding the etiology and pathology of low back and lower limb pain, master the clinical manifestations, diagnosis, and treatment of such conditions.

Based on understanding the pathogenesis of herpes zoster, study its stages, clinical features, treatment, and prevention.

Understand the effects of postoperative analgesia on the body and the importance of pain control, and become familiar with the methods of postoperative analgesia.

教学目标

熟悉并掌握疼痛诊疗学的基本概念和所涉及的诊疗范畴。了解疼痛日常诊疗工作的重要意义。了解疼痛诊疗学的发展简史和目前学科的发展状况。培养学生刻苦钻研、以人为本的良好品格。牢记从医初心，真正做到为患者服务，体现出医者仁心。
