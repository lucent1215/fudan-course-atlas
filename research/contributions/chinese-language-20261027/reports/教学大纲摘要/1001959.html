课程简介（中文）

语言学概论是中国语言文学系的一门基础课，无论对语言专业还是文学专业都十分重要。本课程介绍普通语言学的有关重要而且是基础的知识。涉及语言的基本运作原则、语音学、语法学、语义学和语用学等方面。这些知识的掌握有助于语言学后续课程的学习。

课程简介（英文）

The course of “Introduction to General Linguistics” is a basic one for the students of the department of Chinese language and literature. It is very important for both the students majoring either in the linguistics or in the literature. It introduces briefly some important issues on the general linguistics, including (1) phonology; (2) grammar, such as how to classify words, how to analyze a sentence, etc.; (3) semantics, such as the meaning of words or phrases, semantic orientation, semantic roles and supposition; (4) pragmatics, such as the principles of conversational cooperation, speech acts. Some basic principles of language production and understanding are included as well. Knowing these knowledge will be helpful to the learning of other related courses.

教学目标

本课程是中国语言文学专业的基础课之一，介绍普通语言学的有关重要而且是基础的知识；旨在通过这些知识的学习，能够提高语言理解能力和实际运用能力，学会如何从实际出发分析问题的方法。对语言专业学生继续学习其他的专业课更为必要。

教学方式

讲授为主，辅以讨论。
