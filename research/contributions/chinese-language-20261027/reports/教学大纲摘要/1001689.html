课程简介（中文）

本课程一方面致力于对中外具有影响力的侦探小说、电影、电视剧、连环画、漫画文本进行细读和讲授，教授阅读侦探小说的基本方法并帮助同学积累相关阅读经验；另一方面，也辅之以相关叙事学、类型文学、流行文化、大众传播、粉丝经济角度的理论著作，增强同学的文类研究意识和比较文学意识。总体上来说，本课程是在中西比较文学、跨媒介研究、类型文学研究的视域下讨论侦探小说，并以侦探小说个案研究为入口，培养同学对相关研究方法和问题意识的深入理解。

课程简介（英文）

This course is dedicated to two main objectives. On one hand, it focuses on close reading and analysis of influential detective fiction, films, TV series, comic strips, and graphic novels from both Chinese and international traditions. It aims to teach fundamental methods for reading detective fiction and help students accumulate relevant reading experience. On the other hand, the course incorporates theoretical works from perspectives such as narratology, genre literature, popular culture, mass communication, and fan economics to enhance students' awareness of genre studies and comparative literature. Overall, the course examines detective fiction within the frameworks of Sino-Western comparative literature, cross-media studies, and genre literature research. Through case studies of detective fiction, it cultivates students' deeper understanding of relevant research methodologies and critical thinking.

教学目标

培养学生以专业眼光解读类型文学作品及相关大众文化现象；增强学生的文化批判思考能力 To cultivate students' ability to analyze genre literature and related popular culture phenomena with a professional perspective, and to enhance their critical thinking skills in cultural studies.

教学方式

教师讲授为主，同时辅之以适当的课堂提问和互动环节。

教学内容与进度安排
