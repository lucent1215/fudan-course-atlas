课程简介（中文）

本课程的引导性问题为为什么要对文学现象做理论性的分析和探究，进一步回顾理论阐释与日常阅读间之间的差异。本课程主要介绍考察文学现象的各种视角，文学的内涵与外延（体裁）；梳理阐释文学文本的基本模式：修辞学、诗学、诠释学和语文学的基本理论；探讨文学与其他艺术（如造型艺术、音乐、电影）间的关系。每个单元都将提供了相关拓展文献，供进一步学习。

课程简介（英文）

This course serves as an introduction to literary theory, a modern discipline that takes literary phenomena as its object of study. It will cover the discipline's significant research perspectives and major theoretical orientations. The guiding question for this course is why theoretical analysis and inquiry into literary phenomena are necessary, further reviewing the various differences between theoretical interpretation and everyday reading. The course will primarily introduce diverse perspectives for examining literary phenomena, including the intension and extension of literature (various genres). It will delineate the basic modes of literary text interpretation: fundamental theories of rhetoric, poetics, hermeneutics, and philology. Furthermore, the course will explore the relationships between literature and other art forms (such as plastic arts, music, film, and media). Each unit of the course will provide foundational readings and supplementary advanced texts for students to engage in further reading and deeper learning.

教学目标

本课程将以导论的方式介绍文学理论这门以文学现象为研究对象的现代学科。一方面，文学理论拒斥阅读时未经反思的成见：“文学”即本质，“作者”乃文本意义之权威，作品以“现实/世界”为主题，“阅读”是读者与作者对话，“风格”是选择写作方式，“文学史”是逐一列举大作家。帮助学生重新审视各种文学现象，学会学理性的反思。另一方面，理论动摇了常识，但常识也顶住了理论的攻击，文学理论为了摧毁常识常有过激言论，结果可能使自己陷入悖论，在课程中需重审理论的有效性及其边界。
