课程简介（中文）

本课程按照传统四部分类法的类目格局展现宋代文史典籍的基本面貌。并在每一类目下择宋代文史典籍的代表性著作予以讲解，分别介绍该著作的性质、大要、版本源流及其承载的宋代学术思潮与发展历程。使得中文专业的学生对宋代文献与宋代学术的基本格局及部类特点有所了解，初步掌握宋代文史研究的基本路径与阅读利用宋代典籍的基本原则。

课程简介（英文）

This course presents the fundamental aspects of Song Dynasty historical and literary texts according to the traditional four-part classification system. Under each category, representative works of Song Dynasty historical and literary texts are selected for detailed explanation, introducing the nature, summary, textual lineage, and the academic trends and developmental history they embody. This enables students majoring in Chinese literature to gain an understanding of the basic structure and categorical characteristics of Song Dynasty literature and scholarship, and to preliminarily grasp the fundamental approaches to Song Dynasty historical and literary research, as well as the basic principles for reading and utilizing Song Dynasty texts.

教学目标

本课程旨在通过科学、系统的教学，使学生了解宋代典籍的构成和分类，掌握宋代文史研究的基本途径以及阅读利用宋代典籍的基本原则，获得对重要宋代典籍的大致认识，为深入学习宋代文史以及进入宋代文史研究的专门领域打好基础。
