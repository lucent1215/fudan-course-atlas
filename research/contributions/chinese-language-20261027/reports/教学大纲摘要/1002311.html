课程简介（中文）

“文学是什么？为什么我要读文学？如何读文学？”——由于很难一劳永逸地给出这三个问题的答案，二十世纪以来的“理论家”们围绕为什么这么问（“文学”的兴起）、谁在提问（作者、读者或批评家）、依据什么来回答（作品本身或产生的社会条件）等角度给出了各种说法，进而形成了“文学理论”这门学科。为避免理论的“空转”，本课程无意于挨个介绍各家流派，而是旨在反思这些回答的意义与局限，并结合具体作品，引导学生形成独属于自己的对文学的自洽理解，进而掌握鉴赏各种文体与文类的能力。又因当下是传统的延续，今天对文学的认识是在二十世纪以来的古今与中西思想交汇中形成的，故而针对具体问题，课程适当追溯中西传统，在中西语言与思维差异中把握文学、尤其是中国文学的理论特质。

课程简介（英文）

"What is literature? Why should I read literature? How should I read literature?" — Since it is difficult to provide definitive answers to these three questions, theorists since the twentieth century have offered various perspectives on why these questions are asked (the rise of the concept of "literature"), who is asking them (authors, readers, or critics), and on what basis they can be answered (the work itself or the social conditions of its production). This discourse ultimately formed the academic discipline known as "literary theory." To avoid the "idle spinning" of theory, this course does not aim to introduce every school of thought one by one. Instead, it seeks to reflect on the significance and limitations of these various answers. By engaging with specific literary works, it guides students toward developing their own coherent understanding of literature and, consequently, cultivating the ability to analyze and appreciate various genres and forms. Furthermore, because the present is a continuation of tradition, our contemporary understanding of literature has been shaped by the convergence of Chinese and Western thought—past and present—since the twentieth century. Therefore, when addressing specific issues, the course will appropriately trace both Chinese and Western traditions, aiming to grasp the theoretical characteristics of literature—particularly Chinese literature—within the context of linguistic and cognitive differences between these traditions.

教学目标

本课程从“导论：什么是理论”、“文学是什么”、“文学与传统”和“文学与当下”四部分展开，分别讨论理论的特点、文学的本质、文学与语言、文学与社会等话题，以中西参照的方式介绍各理论流派，分析其优长与局限，并按内容难易程度安排课时（参考思维导图）。每节课的相关话题附阅读材料，上传至elearning供课前预习和课后拓展。

教学方式

任课教师随堂讲授，课后线上师生共同讨论。
