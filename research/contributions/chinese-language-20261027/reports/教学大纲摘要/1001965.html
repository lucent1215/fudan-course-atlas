课程简介（中文）

《现代语言学》是一门入门的语言学课程，你不需具备任何先前的语言学知识即可选修。 语言充斥在我们每天的生活之中。在认知、社会、经济和政治上，语言无所不在。在此课程中，我们将会从「语言是人类认知中的一个基本面向」的角度来探索语言。语言是如何影响我们的思想和思考方式呢？语言和我们的心理机制是如何互动的呢？我们是如何加工语言的呢？我们的大脑有一块特别的脑区专门用来处理语言吗？我们是如何习得语言的呢？母语习得和第二语言习得的方式有何不同呢？语言在日常生活中又是如何被使用的呢？ 这门课将会让你学习到语言的复杂性、创造性、习得和作用。就好像呼吸一样，语言的产出和理解是我们每天都在做的事，是件我们几乎不用花太多力气和意识就能完成的任务。此门课将会探索我们是如何在这看似简单但实而复杂的语言背后，抽丝剥茧，进而理解和使用语言。你对语言理解越深、对语言如何习得和加工理解越多，你就越能了解自己和你周遭的人。这就是语言的奥秘和博大。

课程简介（英文）

Modern Linguistics is an introductory course in linguistics, requiring no prior knowledge of linguistics. Language is pervasive in our daily lives. It exists across cognitive, social, economic, and political dimensions. In this course, we will explore language from the perspective that it is a fundamental aspect of human cognition. How does language shape our thoughts and thinking? How does it interact with our psychological mechanisms? How do we process language? Are there specific brain regions specialized for language? How do we acquire language, and how does first language acquisition differ from second language acquisition? How is language used in everyday communication? This course will teach you about the complexity, creativeness, acquisition, and functions of language. Just like breathing, producing and understanding language is something we do every day, a seemingly effortless task requiring little conscious effort. Yet, this course will uncover the complex mechanisms underlying this apparent simplicity, helping you understand how we use and comprehend language. The deeper your understanding of language, its acquisition, and processing becomes, the more you will understand both yourself and the people around you. This reveals the fascinating depth and power of human language.
