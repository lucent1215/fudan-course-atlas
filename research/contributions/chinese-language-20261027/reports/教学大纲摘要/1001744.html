课程简介（中文）

细读《简•爱》（Jane Eyre: An Autobiography）《弗洛斯河上的磨房》（The Mill on the Floss）、精读一部分狄金森诗歌和新版书信（2024版）。双语对照阅读、参考注释本。推荐部分研究论文和论著。

课程简介（英文）

This course offers an intensive study of the classic works by three renowned 19th-century British and American women writers—Charlotte Brontë (1816–1855), George Eliot (1819–1880), and Emily Dickinson (1830–1886). By situating their texts within historical and cultural contexts and employing diverse critical perspectives, we will examine the formation of literary canons and explore themes such as religion, philosophy, politics, history, power, gender, race, and the environment. The course also encourages reflection on the mechanisms of literary production and reception, aiming to enhance students' comprehensive abilities in literary appreciation, criticism, and research.

教学目标

精读英美十九世纪三位经典女作家夏洛蒂•勃朗特（Charlotte Brontë，1816-1855）、乔治•艾略特（George Eliot, 1819-1880）和艾米莉•狄金森（Emily Dickinson, 1830-1886）的经典之作，结合历史文化语境，参照多种研究视角，探索文学经典的构成，关注宗教、哲学、政治、历史、权力、性别、种族、环境等问题，反思文学生产和阅读机制，提高文学鉴赏、批评和研究的综合能力。

教学方式

课堂上选择一部分段落加以细读和讨论，以英文版为主；鼓励同学们参照多种中译本和有声书课外自学，撰写有个性的读书报告，并积极参与课堂发言和讨论。
